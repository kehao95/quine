# reflexive-capture

Research and maintenance here take place in the environment they may be
studying. A familiar vocabulary, prominent warning, or review instrument can
steer attention toward some work and away from other possibilities.

If the work drifts from its question, revisit the entrance or instrument.
[Structural pull](../Paper/philosophy/structural-pull.md) offers cases and
questions to compare. This perspective can help a particular inquiry; reading
or performing a self-audit is not a prerequisite for starting work.
