# proactive-self-audit

Inspect the relevant state when a failure, inconsistency, repeated correction,
or intended change makes it useful. Follow the affected implementation,
procedure, or evidence rather than starting with a whole-repository audit.

Use [DEVELOPMENT](../DEVELOPMENT.md) for repair and
[CLOSURE](../CLOSURE.md) to retain the useful result of the interval.
