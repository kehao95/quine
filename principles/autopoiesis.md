# autopoiesis

Self-production is one perspective developed in
[living systems](../Paper/philosophy/living-system.md). Ask what is reproduced,
through which activities, and what that continuation enables or costs.

The subject may be an executable lineage, a collaboration, a research practice,
or another organization relevant to the question. Maintenance and inquiry can
support or obstruct one another; examine the relation in its actual setting.

Related: [system coherence](./system-coherence.md),
[self-maintaining systems](../Paper/philosophy/self-maintaining-systems.md),
and [ontology](../Paper/philosophy/ontology.md).
