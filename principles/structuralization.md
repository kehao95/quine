# structuralization

Give recurring operational knowledge a durable form that its consumers can
use: code, documentation, layout, tools, or links. Update navigation when
structure changes.

Choose automation for a concrete recurring check. Explanations and conceptual
judgments can remain prose; turning them into enforcement requires a separate
operational reason.
