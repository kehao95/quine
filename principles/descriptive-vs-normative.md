# descriptive-vs-normative

A description makes a claim about what happens. An instruction or design
commitment guides what participants should do. The same document can contain
both; make the intended role clear where it affects a decision.

When observation and expectation disagree, investigate the discrepancy. The
repair may concern the implementation, the account, the expectation, or their
relationship. A rule's usefulness and a claim's support can be reconsidered.

Descriptions can influence action when participants use them; instructions can
become objects of research. [Mutual encoding](../Paper/philosophy/mutual-encoding-network.md)
develops these returns. Their presence does not give any document a final view
of the whole.
