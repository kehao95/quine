# experience-promotion-gate

Retain useful experience in a research record, discussion, or
[evolution history](../EVOLUTION.md). Add standing guidance when there is a
concrete future use for it; a question or insight can remain valuable without
becoming a rule. Keep mutable operational facts with their working source.

## Failure mode its absence produces

Noise accretion: the self-model accretes session-local explanations that do not
change future behavior, growing without becoming denser. The mirror failure
(promoting nothing) produces addressability-debt. See "Experience promotion
pressure" in [`evolution/patterns.md`](../evolution/patterns.md).
