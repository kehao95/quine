# closure

Retain the changes, evidence, decisions, and open alternatives needed to resume
the work. Completed tasks and unresolved research can be recorded together.

Remove scaffolding whose useful content is retained elsewhere. A discussion or
exploratory record can remain useful in its own form.

Detailed integration guidance lives in [CLOSURE](../CLOSURE.md).
