# control-plane-imperative

Maintain useful routes to implementation, evidence, current work, and research.
Update operational guidance when its consumers or behavior change.

[Control-plane indexes](./control-planes-as-maintained-indexes.md) help locate
material. [Absorption](./absorption-rule.md) keeps decisions and unresolved work
available to later participants.
