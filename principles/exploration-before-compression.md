# exploration-before-compression

Give an idea room to develop in its own terms. Preserve differences between
candidate explanations, boundaries, and directions when they matter.

A summary can help someone resume work, and it can also hide alternatives or
be read as an instruction. Record consequential choices and unresolved
questions alongside it. A local commitment, prototype, or completed argument
can expose possibilities that an indefinitely open discussion would miss.
