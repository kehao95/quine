# direction-and-traceability

Explain what a change is for and leave its useful record where another
participant can find it: code, an experiment, a discussion, a theory object,
or another appropriate surface.

Link empirical claims to their evidence and operational decisions to their
consumers. Record open alternatives when they matter to continuing the work.
A new direction can develop before its destination is settled.
