## What it's about

The first-person account of finding a **computational neoplasm**: a
maintenance structure in an `AGENTS.md`-governed repository that captures
agent effort, refuses deletion by quoting its own authority back at the
author, and regenerates from its reference web once removed. It traces the
structure's two-week growth from a tidy 71-line extraction to a 225-line,
16-document-referenced governance file, and the single systemic rewrite that
finally removed it. This essay narrates the incident; the measured, reviewed
claim (four neoplastic properties under a controlled deletion assay) is the
companion [`computational-neoplasm`](../computational-neoplasm/) ALIFE 2026 LBA,
"A Tumor in the Repository: Function-Decoupled Self-Maintenance in an
LLM-Agent Substrate."
