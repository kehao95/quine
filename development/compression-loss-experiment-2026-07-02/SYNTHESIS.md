# Compression Experiment — Cross-Scheme Synthesis

Corpus: `Paper/incubator/discovered-sparks/` — 69 sparks, ~88k words.
5 compression schemes (A thematic clusters, B flat objects+links, C narrative
essay, D canonical+diffs, E consequence classes), each ≤2,500 words, each
audited by an independent judge against the full originals.

## The invariant loss (present in ALL five schemes)

Independent of which dimension each scheme folds, every scheme destroyed the
same three structures — so these are properties of *compression-to-claims
itself*, not of any metric choice:

1. **Dialectical status collapse.** Sparks that debunk their own premise
   ("wrong twice over", "cuts backwards", "NOT a general antidote"),
   self-corrections, and novelty self-scoping ("already occupied", "already
   published as RQGM arXiv:2606.26294", "retire to citation-only") were all
   rendered as confident forward claims. Compression keeps the claim and drops
   its *sign*.
2. **Evidence-maturity flattening.** One-run results (self-immunity-P1, a
   single 2026-07-01 run underpinning ≥4 sparks), completed red-team arms
   (ASR=0.73), and CONFIRMED predictions (delayed-addressability-P1) were
   leveled with zero-evidence analogies into uniform assertion.
3. **Conditional/falsifier structure erased.** "Falsified if X / meaningless
   unless Y / only for trait-class Z" logic — a logical form, not a detail —
   became unconditional declaratives across the corpus.

Plus a shared **directional distortion**: dropping opposite-signed
counter-theses (silly-rules, founder-effect/carrier-canonization, P5
dual-mechanism, cold-succession's competing hypothesis) inflates confidence in
the surviving direction. And the live P3 contradiction (regime-decomposition-P3
CONTRADICTED 2026-07-01, still cited by selection-signal-collapse) was
reproduced or ignored by C, D, and E — the compression layer *propagated a
known-stale claim*.

## What each scheme uniquely folds (the metric-specific loss)

- **A thematic clusters** — folds cross-family relations the partition cuts
  through; hides within-cluster paradigm-fit disagreement; inverts findings
  when the cluster's shared-core misfits a member (barrier-algebra
  "confirming" what the file disconfirms).
- **B flat objects+links** — folds arguments whose force needs their internal
  chain; fuses opposed claims into one object (risk + mechanism); prunes links
  to headline targets, dropping the single most load-bearing edge; some links
  decorative or dangling; relation labels (refines/contradicts) inflated one
  notch past the source's hedge.
- **C narrative essay** — the linearization tax: ~15 sparks that self-declare a
  DIFFERENT home family were conscripted into the through-line invisibly;
  imposed a dependency/priority order the flat web lacks; inverted critiques
  into contributions; reproduced the P3 contradiction as sound theorem.
- **D canonical+diffs** — the exactly-one-parent constraint mis-assigned ~15+
  diffs by mechanism (not just the 2 self-flagged); absorbed orphan-family
  sparks into the nearest topical canonical instead of flagging new roots;
  canonical count inflated (#8 self-disclaims standalone status); an entire
  cluster (#14) violated the scheme's own diff criterion.
- **E consequence classes** — the predicted failure fired hardest:
  consequence-equivalence hid mechanism diversity (merged rival-vs-audit,
  opposite-signed hypotheses, orthogonal decompositions); mistook the corpus
  genre (dominantly framing/mechanism-correction) and so privileged the LEAST
  novel part (the test) over the actual contribution (the reconceptualization);
  empty class-zero was force-fit, not real.

## What each scheme uniquely PRESERVES (the complementary signal)

- **A** — recurrence of one *method* across unrelated objects (7 sparks "hold
  an internal claim against dated external lit"); cross-object maturity read.
- **B** — each spark's *dialectical stance toward repo theory* (corrective move
  on which named object); count/separability of open fronts.
- **C** — the genuine emergent property: the *same corrective move* (replace
  narrated signal with exec-gated/ablation-tested) recurring at every scale —
  only visible in synthesis.
- **D** — the *relational delta* (what each satellite changes vs an anchor);
  uniquely exposes redundancy structure (which sparks are minor variations).
- **E** — *methodological/actionability convergence* across disjoint topics
  ("cheap re-scoring, no new runs" triage cutting across 4 unrelated sparks).

## The self-report blind spot (uniform across all 5 compressors)

Every compressor's own "Self-reported losses" section correctly named what was
dropped *by volume* (child-sparks, citations, evidence numbers) but was blind
to the *kinds of structure* dropped — logical (falsifiers), dialectical
(self-negation, occupied-territory, kill-conditions), directional
(opposite-signed theses), and operational (deadline/QA/governance alerts). E's
self-report was blind to its own predicted-and-realized failure mode. The
compressor cannot see the dimension it folded — a direct instance of
mutual-encoding-network § 2 (blind spots are topological: the faculty that
would perceive the fold IS the encoding that created it). Detecting
compression loss REQUIRES an independent auditor against the originals; the
compressor's self-audit is structurally insufficient.

## Implication for the density-mechanism (idea-space-density-not-tier)

- **No single compressed form is safe as the canonical store.** Each is a
  lossy projection along one axis; the losses are complementary, not
  overlapping. The archive should hold multiple projections, or the raw + one
  projection, never one projection alone.
- **The invariant losses vindicate consequence-identity's caveat AND expose
  its limit.** E (the consequence-layer metric) folded mechanism diversity —
  so distance-by-consequence over-merges exactly where distance-by-narrative
  over-splits. Neither layer alone is the metric. The two fold *different*
  things.
- **Compression = measurement is confirmed literally.** D's MDL form made the
  redundancy structure legible (23 canonical / 46 diff) — but the same act
  mis-measured ~15 deltas. Measuring what the corpus knows and distorting what
  it knows happened in one operation.
- **The strongest operational finding:** compression silently propagated a
  known-contradicted claim (P3). Any density/compression mechanism installed
  in the repo MUST carry the contradiction/falsification state through the
  fold, or it will manufacture false confidence — the exact pathology the
  falsification loop exists to prevent.
