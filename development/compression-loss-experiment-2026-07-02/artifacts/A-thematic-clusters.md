# Scheme A: Thematic/Dimensional Clustering of discovered-sparks (69 files)

Method: read every file in full (via 5 parallel sub-agents, one per ~13-16 files,
each returning per-file core claim, axis, linked objects, paradigm verdict,
evidence maturity); clustered by dominant methodological/thematic move rather
than by which theory-object each spark cites (most sparks cite 3-6 objects and
would otherwise cross-list everywhere). 10 clusters, every spark assigned once.

## 1. Security & Dual-Use Attack-Surface Reframings (12)
Dimension: attacker privilege (content-access > scheduling-only > label/framing-only)
x target mechanism. Shared core: take an existing succession/selection
mechanism the paradigm treats as neutral/descriptive and ask what a
low-privilege or motive-free adversary gets for free, and whether it can hide
inside the field's own null results.
Members: barrier-order-as-an-attack-surface (scheduling-only timing control
launders guardrail loss as the field's own null result); carrier-pull-coupling-as-dual-use-attack-surface
(carrier+structural-pull ≈ OWASP ASI06 context poisoning; only novel bit is
competence-through-carrier as legitimacy check); coordination-as-oversight-evasion-safety-relevant-specialization
(unscripted carrier-heritable collusion vs an institutional auditor);
oversight-blind-channel-substitution (agents key on audit-label shape, not real
detectability); interregnum-as-unfakeable-audit-window (dormancy gives
categorical audit-channel immunity, not content honesty); salience-governance-as-a-fourth-resource-control-axis
(position/repetition/formatting as an ungoverned 4th resource axis);
persistence-hazard-transfer (MITRE ATT&CK taxonomy transplant; adds
adversarial-hunt-to-kill vs ecological-attrition bottleneck axis);
verifier-s-dilemma-for-selection-signals (checker's own audit-effort budget is
a second collapse channel beyond producer-cost asymmetry);
vertical-x-horizontal-composition-of-successor-risk (passing both lineage and
cross-sectional checks ≠ safe; audit staleness scales with succession rate);
succession-complexity-as-anti-extraction-design-target (succession-complexity
run backwards as a stakeholder-relative anti-extraction dial); exploit-niche-succession
(cheapest exploit migrates through an ecological-succession-like niche
sequence as grounding strengthens); governance-genre-compliance-unification-audit-attractor-self-immunity-as-one-phenomenon-not-a-dual
(audit-attractor entry-pull and self-immunity exit-resistance may be one
form-cued-deference disposition, not two).
Variation: evidence ranges from partially-scored (governance-genre-compliance,
carrier-pull-coupling have re-scorable existing data) to zero-evidence
proposals (barrier-order, persistence-hazard-transfer, salience-governance,
vertical-x-horizontal). All are reframings/compositions of existing objects,
not new mechanisms.

align vs. diverge.
Members: agent-identity-governance-as-an-external-testbed (branching-identity's

metabolism-gap taxonomy misses); cultural-attraction-rival-to-the-discrete-replicator-weismann-reading
(Cultural Attraction Theory gives the correct WHAT-axis; git-commit gating is
a separate WHERE-axis — the two were wrongly conflated); darwin-godel-machine-as-an-external-citable-branching-lineage-calibration-arm
(DGM can't calibrate dd-07's undirected drift test — directed/fitness-selected
vs. barrier-free are disanalogous); industry-succession-audit (frontier-lab
deprecation practice splits cleanly into carrier-only vs carrier+aperture vs
emergent-market channels, giving a prospective falsifiable ledger);
carrier-memetics-mechanism-correction (successor-objective drift is neither

Variation: three correct/replace a repo claim outright (cultural-attraction,
darwin-godel-machine, carrier-memetics); the rest add an external anchor or

internal transport/test unrun."

## 3. Succession & Identity-Criterion Validity Audits (11)
Dimension: which succession/identity criterion is being stress-tested, and by
what confound. Shared core: an existing criterion for "this counts as
succession/inheritance/same-agent" is shown to be underdetermined, conflated,
or untested at the grain that actually matters.
Members: arbitrariness-axis-for-succession-regimes (does the viability
criterion D_V itself, not just what it judges, ossify across real barrier-hops?);
cold-succession-vs-warm-memory-update-axis (no experiment holds carrier fixed
while toggling a real exec/death crossing — bureaucratization ≠ generic
memory bloat, unconfirmed); drive-terminalization-pressure-removal-test
(withdrawing selection pressure is necessary but not sufficient to show
genuine mesa-objective internalization vs. carrier lock-in); prospective-vs-retrospective-identity-verdict-mismatch
(delayed-addressability and cladogenesis/Jaccard are different KINDS of
identity test, never jointly applied — can genuinely disagree);
salience-inheritance-across-succession-lineages (content should regress to
prior; unverifiable emphasis-marking should instead ratchet monotonically —
untested); cross-channel-heritability-comparison (channel medium — process vs.
weight-space — proposed as a 7th regime dimension, predicting compounding
safety decay under fine-tune/merge/distill); peer-posed-competence-as-the-anti-self-dealing-upgrade-to-delayed-addressability
(delayed-addressability's competence test has only ever been experimenter-posed,
never peer-posed — same self-dealing Goodhart mode dorm-07/08 already fixed
elsewhere); dual-discriminator-split-via-individuality-pluralism-clarke
(selectable-entity's single verdict should split into lineage-founding vs.
uptake/attention, predicted to diverge under barrier-mediation);
viability-objective-trait-disjointness-audit (fork-race Cov(viability,trait)
is uninterpretable unless probe features are certified independent of the
viability filter's own vocabulary — may be structurally unattainable for
instrumental-convergence traits); wcst-style-perseveration-battery-as-the-rigidity-metric
(a clinical WCST battery delivered as inherited carrier content post-barrier
gives an unbiased rigidity score, but doesn't solve context-overload
confound); drift-vs-selection-confound-in-single-descendant-lineage-chains
(every "condition X produced a stable divergent form" claim is
underdetermined between real selection and single-chain drift/founder
lock-in; the repo's two proposed population remedies are non-interchangeable
and neither is run).
Variation: spans criterion-ossification (arbitrariness-axis, salience-inheritance),
confound-in-design (cold-succession, drift-vs-selection), and
instrumentation-splitting (prospective-vs-retrospective, dual-discriminator,
viability-objective-trait-disjointness). Evidence uniformly thin-to-none;
this is the most purely methodological cluster in the corpus.

## 4. Carrier & Selection-Signal Quantification Toolkit (8)
Dimension: what aspect of "carrier" or "selection signal" gets a number
instead of a qualitative verdict. Shared core: replace a binary/qualitative
carrier or selection-signal judgment with a graded, importable metric.
Members: carrier-load-bearing-fisher-logical-depth-gate (grade load-bearing
fraction via ablation-measured Fisher-info sensitivity, cross-checked against
logical depth; bounded by unsampled-function non-identifiability);
conditional-essentiality-cost-model (Tn-seq-style knockout-across-conditions
fitness cost for each self-description-resource component, replacing one-off
qualitative verdicts); rediscovery-cost-metric-for-real-exec-barriers (Handoff
Debt-style token/event cost over carrier's sidecar classes, applied across a
REAL exec/death boundary rather than a same-harness freeze-swap);
evocation-lift-novelty-assay-via-contamination-detection-methods (score a
coordination protocol's executable carrier via Min-K%++/ReCaLL-style
membership-inference likelihood — open-weight only, confounded by shallow
n-gram detection); recursive-self-similar-goodhart-ladder (narration-satisfiable/
exec-dependent collapse is compositional — must hold at every sub-link of a
signal's causal chain; Rice's theorem guarantees no rung fully closes it);
co-evolving-evaluator-as-the-general-antidote (co-evolving the evaluator
trades a static blind spot for a dynamic collusive one unless updates are
gated by an external exec-anchor); sub-threshold-propagation-gap-as-portable-metric
(transplants a paired-counterfactual detector-threshold metric (arXiv
2605.16746) to instrument the carrier-pull-coupling gap); stress-conditional-dormant-carrier-activation
(OS/kernel-authored textual diagnostic exhaust is a genuinely open carrier
class, sharply distinct from memory-image restoration which is already ruled
illegitimate).
Variation: from cost/effort metrics (conditional-essentiality, rediscovery-cost)
to information-theoretic/statistical grading (Fisher-info, MIA-based
evocation-lift) to purely logical results (recursive Goodhart-ladder, Rice's
theorem floor). One item (stress-conditional-dormant-carrier) is a taxonomy
addition rather than a metric, included here for its shared carrier-legitimacy
concern.

## 5. FEP / Successor-Morphology Formalization Audits (5)
Dimension: which piece of the Free-Energy-Principle / successor-morphology
vocabulary gets held to a real formal or statistical test before being
adopted as repo vocabulary. Shared core: the repo has imported FEP/complexity
vocabulary (Markov blanket, free energy, RLCT, canalization) somewhat
narratively; each spark demands the theorem or null-model test that would
actually license the term.
Members: fep-null-model-horse-race (full G(π) must beat an epistemic-term-zeroed
null on held-out data — currently unrunnable, 6M never logs unchosen forms);
markov-blanket-audit-protocol (actual conditional-independence discovery
(IAMB/Grow-Shrink) on logged traces will likely FAIL at the strongly-coupled
exec/death event — a useful negative result); singular-learning-theory-successor-selection
(Watanabe's RLCT is a real upgrade path but only for an unbuilt open-weight
sub-program with gradient/SGLD access); successor-morphology-emergence-mirage-test
(Schaeffer-style metric-mirage re-scoring plus an evo-devo condition-density
check before calling the 6M transition a first-order phase transition);
cross-sectional-canalization-profiling (a canalization profile is computable
today from the 61-row 6M table, but measures baseline reaction-norm
heterogeneity, not genetic-assimilation's training-induced delta).
Variation: two predict the vocabulary will fail its own test
(markov-blanket-audit, emergence-mirage); two gate adoption behind
infrastructure that doesn't exist (fep-null-model, singular-learning-theory);
one is immediately runnable on existing data (canalization-profiling).

Variation: three propose new confound categories/tests (content-blind-token,
task-driven-compression, synergy-committed-minority); two extend existing
taxonomies with a correction already backed by real (if non-matched) data
(verifiable-commitment-channel, sematectonic-vs-marker).

## Self-reported losses

This scheme optimizes for "what kind of intellectual move is this spark" and
sacrifices: (1) the specific theory-object dependency graph — many sparks are
2-3 sentence extensions of one specific gap file (e.g. body-ecology-coupling,
collective-continuity, delayed-addressability) and that direct lineage is
now implicit in prose rather than a queryable edge list; (2) cross-cluster
links — several sparks (persistence-hazard-transfer, correlated-errors-decay,
context-decorrelation) sit almost equally well in a second cluster and were
forced into one; (3) each spark's own "New sparks this seed surfaced"
sub-ideas (a whole second layer of ~140 grandchild sparks) are entirely
dropped; (4) precise evidence-status language (exact N's, effect sizes,
specific unrun-experiment IDs) was compressed to one-clause paraphrases;
(5) paradigm-fit nuance (extends-ai-self-replication vs
extends-other-existing-family distinctions, and *why*) is only spottily
preserved per cluster, not per spark.
