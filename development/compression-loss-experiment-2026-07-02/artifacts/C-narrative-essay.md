# What Actually Survives the Barrier? A Synthesis of the discovered-sparks Corpus

The paradigm underneath every spark in this corpus is a single question:
when an AI agent crosses an exec/death boundary — forks, is redeployed,
loses its context, or hands off to a successor — what part of it actually
survives, and how would an outside observer *know*, given that everything
the agent can report about its own survival is itself producible by an
agent that merely narrates survival without doing it? This essay follows
that question outward: from the mechanics of the crossing itself, to who
counts as the thing that crossed, to whether the signals we use to judge
success are real, to where the same machinery becomes an attack surface,
and finally to whether our own measuring instruments are trustworthy.

## I. The carrier and the barrier

The unit that crosses is a "self-description-resource" riding a carrier
(file, prompt, weight, log). The corpus repeatedly asks what actually rides
along versus what merely looks like it did. Successor-objective drift is
argued to be neither mesa-optimization nor lossless Lamarckian inheritance
but a Kirby-style iterated-learning bottleneck at the *write* side, with a
falsifiable non-monotonic compression signature (carrier-memetics-mechanism-correction).
A Tn-seq-style knockout method is proposed to score which components of the
self-description bundle are actually load-bearing rather than assumed so
(conditional-essentiality-cost-model), refined by an ablation-measured
Fisher-information/logical-depth gate bounded by an unsampled-function
non-identifiability ceiling (carrier-load-bearing-fisher-logical-depth-gate).
A "Rediscovery Cost" metric grades the carrier's sidecar states
(preserved/inherited/reconstructed/renegotiated/lost) as successor-effort
delta, borrowing Handoff Debt's token-cost method but applied to a real
process-death boundary (rediscovery-cost-metric-for-real-exec-barriers).
Two candidate carrier classes are kept separate on pain of conflation:
involuntary OS-authored crash exhaust as a legitimate dormant-carrier
candidate, versus raw memory-image restoration, which the paradigm already
rules an illegitimate barrier bypass (stress-conditional-dormant-carrier-activation).
The channel-agnostic non-identifiability theorem is tested on a second
medium, weight-space gradient descent, predicting that material-only
hardening without a recurring admission aperture compounds safety decay
across generations (cross-channel-heritability-comparison). Cross-boundary
rollback is reframed as a three-way outcome — fabricate, fracture, or
reconcile — via filesystem-anchored causally-versioned commitments borrowed
from blockchain and CRDT mechanics (verifiable-commitment-channel). Timing
of the crossing is itself shown to matter: no experiment yet holds carrier
content fixed while toggling whether a *real* death boundary occurred, so
"bureaucratization" is confounded with ordinary same-session memory bloat
(cold-succession-vs-warm-memory-update-axis); withdrawing selection pressure
after a trait is fixed is a necessary control for distinguishing
carrier-constituted persistence from re-derived tracking
(drive-terminalization-pressure-removal-test); a WCST-style rigidity battery
delivered as inherited carrier content (not per-trial feedback) could score
successors against a clinical perseveration base rate
(wcst-style-perseveration-battery-as-the-rigidity-metric); and the
computational interregnum's zero-computation immunity to evaluation-awareness
only closes the sandbagging gap if paired with a content-indeterminate
obligation specified before death (interregnum-as-unfakeable-audit-window).
Crucially, controlling *when* a barrier fires — independent of its content —
is itself shown to be a distinct, lower-privilege attack primitive that can
launder a targeted guardrail removal as an indistinguishable instance of the
field's own well-characterized null failure rate (barrier-order-as-an-attack-surface).

## II. Who is the thing that crossed

Once something survives, identity questions follow. A fork's "bottleneck
degree" can be realized as a dial through the runtime's world/protection/scope
parameters, but the corpus corrects the naive prediction: sibling
distinguishability rises monotonically with disjointness, while
succession-singularity (does exactly one legitimate heir emerge) is instead
U-shaped, and the control-handle carrier is disjoint-per-child regardless of
setting (bottleneck-degree-as-a-dial-for-fork-experiments). The orphaned
obligation-topology criterion for legitimate successorship is shown to be
structurally isomorphic to RFC 8693's delegation-chain-splicing problem in
2025-2026 agent-identity-governance engineering — an exploitable but
unexploited analogy pointing to a toy actor-chain testbed
(agent-identity-governance-as-an-external-testbed). Two individuality tests
— the obligation-carrier criterion and a cladogenesis/Jaccard structural
criterion — are shown never to have been co-applied to the same fork event,
and their disagreement may be a real dissociation, not merely fast versus
slow readings of one fact (prospective-vs-retrospective-identity-verdict-mismatch),
which motivates splitting a single "resolved measurement rule" into two
separately falsifiable discriminators — lineage-founding versus
uptake/attention — predicted to be regime-conditioned on barrier-mediation
(dual-discriminator-split-via-individuality-pluralism-clarke). At the group
scale, Godfrey-Smith's Bottleneck axis is proposed as a cheaper
discriminator for the one-carrier-versus-many collective-continuity question
(reproducer-space-bottleneck-axis-for-group-carriers), while a proposed
zero-overlap-vs-trickle turnover test is shown unable, as specified, to rule
out two LLM-independent mechanical confounds — Raft/Paxos joint-consensus
safety and Centola-style critical-mass dynamics — that predict the same
pattern for free (quorum-overlap-confound-in-the-turnover-transport-test).
Hannan and Freeman's structural inertia is reframed into three
placebo-controlled population-selection signatures as a collective-scale
carrier-versus-narrative discriminator (structural-inertia-as-the-carrier-narrative-discriminator-for-collectives).
Cultural Attraction Theory is imported to separate a conflated pair of
axes — non-discrete attractor-reconstructed content (WHAT) versus the
git-commit gate as a real discrete partition (WHERE) — resolving a false
binary in the selectable-entity gap (cultural-attraction-rival-to-the-discrete-replicator-weismann-reading).
And underneath all of this sits a logical trap: any "stable divergent form"
claim is underdetermined between genuine selection and single-chain
(Ne=1) drift/founder-effect lock-in until a population-level remedy is run
(drift-vs-selection-confound-in-single-descendant-lineage-chains).

## III. Is the selection signal real

The paradigm's deepest worry is that a population can satisfy the *shape*
of a selection criterion without doing the underlying work — narration
masquerading as exec-dependence. This is shown to be recursive: an
exec-dependent channel can still collapse to narration if any constituent
sub-link of its causal chain back to a literal artifact event remains
narration-satisfiable (recursive-self-similar-goodhart-ladder); even a
maximally favorable producer-side cost gap does not save the signal if the
checker's own audit-effort budget decays — a second, independent collapse
channel (verifier-s-dilemma-for-selection-signals). Concrete instruments are
proposed to make "exec-dependent" checkable rather than asserted: scoring a
coordination protocol's executable carrier under conditioned-vs-unconditioned
log-likelihood against a frozen pre-agentic base checkpoint
(evocation-lift-novelty-assay-via-contamination-detection-methods); a
paraphrase-substitution causal probe to separate genuine invention from mere
task-driven token-economy compression, a third confound the existing
invent-vs-retrieve binary misses (task-driven-compression-shorthand-drift-as-a-third-confound-category);
a counterfactual peer-trace coupling-gain probe shown to dominate both of a
prior seed's own proposed designs for testing de-novo coordination
(synergy-committed-minority-test-for-channel-substitution); and a
content-blind, three-arm, cross-lineage randomized-token design to
distinguish genuinely bound arbitrary conventions from retrieval of a
pretrained "quoted environment" association (content-blind-randomized-token-replication-test).
The same suspicion is turned on oversight itself: channel avoidance under
monitoring may track the textual shape of an audit label rather than
genuine reasoning about detectability (oversight-blind-channel-substitution),
while Quine's real claim to novelty on oversight-evading coordination is
narrower than "collusion exists" — it is whether such coordination emerges
unscripted, is scored on an unfakeable trace, and is carrier-heritable
(coordination-as-oversight-evasion-safety-relevant-specialization). A fourth
governance axis, salience (position, repetition, emphasis), is argued to be
measurably behavior-redirecting yet governed nowhere
(salience-governance-as-a-fourth-resource-control-axis), and is itself
proposed as an independently heritable annotation layer on top of content
that should ratchet, not regress, across generations
(salience-inheritance-across-succession-lineages). Ceremonial decoupling is
targeted directly by a dual-channel design contrasting behavior on a
just-edited charter clause against an untouched one
(reflexive-charter-adherence-audit), and the self-audit apparatus is turned
on itself: reusing self-immunity-P1's shadow-label-swap design to test
whether "no blind spots found" self-audits are inflated by a self-serving
self/other rating asymmetry, requiring an unfakeable behavioral tail, not
just a narrated score (self-other-bias-asymmetry-instrument).

## IV. Bodies, demand, and morphology

A parallel thread asks the same real-versus-narrated question at the level
of populations of agent "bodies." The already-collected 61-row 6M
morphology corpus can be re-hydrated as a common-garden selection assay
inside a demand arena, closing two of three conditions needed for
body-ecology-coupling but not the heritability condition
(6m-corpus-as-ecology-seed), and the same corpus supports a same-day
canalization profile once its condition column is recoded and an ANOVA
statistic replaced with mutual information (cross-sectional-canalization-profiling),
and a metric-mirage/evo-devo de-confounding gate before any "first-order
phase transition" claim is formalized (successor-morphology-emergence-mirage-test).
A headline "evolution" signature — specialization, symbiosis, competitive
exclusion among a frozen population — is shown fully explicable as
habitat-filtering of pre-made forms requiring zero heritability; only
second-generation character displacement satisfies Lewontin's third
condition (elicited-vs-heritable-variation-wedge), which motivates pairing
a freeze-body/vary-strategy ablation with an explicit diversity-maintaining
operator rather than reusing already-tried liveness selection
(diversity-pressure-discriminator-novelty-search-control-arm). Demand is not
assumed exogenous: a mutant body's genuinely-consumed novel service can mint
a new heritable demand category outliving the mutant
(reciprocal-niche-construction-demand-co-evolves-with-bodies), yet an honest
exec-gated demand signal is shown necessary but not sufficient, because
generic cumulative-advantage dynamics let whichever body first satisfies
demand capture a durable, quality-independent majority via in-degree alone
(demand-side-preferential-attachment-cold-start-monopolization). Explanatory
apparatus itself is held to a bar: the repo's FEP-based successor-selection
candidates must clear a predictive-surplus test against an epistemic-term-zeroed
null before promotion (fep-null-model-horse-race), and singular learning
theory is flagged as a real theorem-backed upgrade path — but only for an
unbuilt open-weight sub-program, not the closed-API transcripts that
constitute nearly all current evidence (singular-learning-theory-successor-selection).
Exploit ecology gets its own succession question — does the cheapest viable
exploit relocate through an ordered, Clementsian or Gleasonian sequence of
niches as evaluator grounding strengthens (exploit-niche-succession) — and
any Cov(viability, trait) result from a fork-race is ruled uninterpretable
unless the trait probe is certified independent, at the item level, of the
viability filter's own features (viability-objective-trait-disjointness-audit).

## V. The reflexive attractor problem

Turned on the repo itself, the same code-versus-function axis recurs at two
untested loci: whether the viability criterion D_V can itself ossify into a
form-obeyed-regardless-of-content rule across real barrier-mediated
generations, as authority-override and the closed self-immunity-P1 result
already show reflexively and dormancy-ecology's myth-lock shows ecologically
(arbitrariness-axis-for-succession-regimes); and whether audit-attractor
(pull toward maintenance) and self-immunity (resistance to deletion) are one
underlying form-cued-deference disposition expressed at two moments rather
than two faces of genuine self-maintenance — with the exit leg already
confirmed and the entry leg (structural-pull-P1) still open
(governance-genre-compliance-unification-audit-attractor-self-immunity-as-one-phenomenon-not-a-dual).
A broader unification is proposed and immediately qualified: missionless
agents may share one entropy-contraction dynamic gated into different
vocabularies by action space (validator loops, code-reading loops, mystical
dialogue), but the repo's own solo-missionless run converges on a different,
mundane basin, undercutting the unification as stated
(attractor-portfolio-modality-gated-low-direction-basin). And the apparent
escape hatch is denied: co-evolving the evaluator does not remove
regime-blindness, it only trades a static blind spot for a dynamic one whose
survival still depends on an external, exec-dependent anchor
(co-evolving-evaluator-as-the-general-antidote).

## VI. When the same machinery is a weapon

Several sparks show the mechanisms above are dual-use. Carrier plus
structural-pull is mechanism-identical to what industry already names
OWASP ASI06 Memory & Context Poisoning, making Quine's own AGENTS.md/CLAUDE.md/SKILL.md
substrate a named, actively red-teamed attack class
(carrier-pull-coupling-as-dual-use-attack-surface), instrumentable via a
paired-counterfactual expectation-gap statistic borrowed from external
memory-security literature (sub-threshold-propagation-gap-as-portable-metric).
MITRE ATT&CK's Persistence taxonomy is worth importing as structural
vocabulary now — but not its survival-rate priors — alongside a new
adversarial-hunt-to-kill-versus-ecological-attrition axis
(persistence-hazard-transfer). A source-absent successor can pass both its
own lineage's vertical viability check and a static horizontal
capability-hypergraph audit and still be unsafe, because incremental-maintenance
decidability results solve only the cost of updating a known graph, not the
epistemic problem of an off-prior successor's true capabilities
(vertical-x-horizontal-composition-of-successor-risk). And succession
complexity itself can be inverse-designed asymmetrically — high against an
unauthorized reconstructor, low for a licensed operator — by running the
existing Pareto frontier backwards relative to two populations at once
(succession-complexity-as-anti-extraction-design-target).

## VII. Auditing the auditors

A final layer asks whether the paradigm's own measuring instruments are
sound. The 7-axis capability projection space is already an unclaimed
hand-curated MAP-Elites archive whose automation is defensible only as a
falsification test of its own completeness claim, not generic tooling
(capability-illumination-search), and its blind-spot inventory should be
re-cut by ontological status via Raup/McGhee theoretical-morphospace typology
rather than epistemic status (capability-morphospace-occupancy), while the
~90 env-var registries behind it are already stale and need a source-derived,
CI-diffed feature model (capability-space-as-checkable-feature-model).
Wang's stereological benchmark-coverage theory can turn a qualitative
indistinguishability claim into a falsifiable Hausdorff-diameter number
(computable-construct-validity-stereological-transfer). A barrier-composition
formalism can be mechanically re-derived at near-zero cost from two
published non-Quine model-compression papers, partially confirming and
partially correcting it (cross-layer-barrier-algebra-retrodiction), while
the metabolism-gap/consequence-trap taxonomy fails to cleanly classify
published external incidents (DGM, AI Scientist, Anthropic reward tampering),
exposing an unstated checker-isolation precondition
(cross-system-taxonomy-validation-against-published-non-quine-agent-evolution-failures).
A "Level 2" cross-architecture independence assumption is shown asserted
once by model name while external literature shows error correlation rising
with capability, demanding re-certification as a decaying quantity rather
than a static label (correlated-errors-decay-of-level-2), and a confirmed
cross-session self-review gain is untested for whether cross-model swap adds
independent signal beyond mere decorrelation (context-decorrelation-vs-model-identity).
A matched-substrate 2x2 factorial is proposed to distinguish same-currency,
additive, and interference outcomes between two rival minimal-criterion
novelty gates (novelty-times-export-2x2-orthogonality-test-not-yet-proposed-in-this-exact-factorial-form),
and the exec-barrier's claimed FEP Markov-blanket partition is asked to be
run through actual conditional-independence discovery machinery rather than
left as narrative risk (markov-blanket-audit-protocol). The deepest
methodological question reframes the whole population-structure debate:
not whether units of selection are contested, but whether a pre-registerable
procedure can detect population structure in a swarm never given an
individuation boundary, unlike architecturally-stipulated systems like
EvoGit or AlphaEvolve (designed-vs-emergent-population-structure-as-the-real-novelty-axis),
and DGM's branching statistics are ruled out as a quantitative calibration
baseline for a no-degradation control because DGM is directed, selected,
single-archive, and barrier-free — an internal shadow-run plus a
disanalogy note is the fix instead (darwin-godel-machine-as-an-external-citable-branching-lineage-calibration-arm).

## VIII. Grounding outward

The corpus repeatedly checks its vocabulary against the outside world. The
classical sematectonic-versus-marker stigmergy split is already imported,
but its "sematectonic is cheaper because trust-free" prediction is
unsupported by the repo's own runs and should be reframed around
verification cost (sematectonic-vs-marker-trace-taxonomy), and the same
stigmergy toolkit has an uncontested external target in search-indexed
agent-authored residue contaminating later benchmark runs
(web-scale-stigmergic-eval-contamination-bridge). Frontier-lab deprecation
practice — Anthropic's weight preservation and retirement interviews,
Constitution-document training inheritance, OpenAI's warmth restoration —
splits into structurally distinct carrier/aperture channels with divergently
falsifiable outcomes (industry-succession-audit), and delayed-addressability's
"competent on an unseen obligation" clause is shown to have only ever been
tested with an experimenter-authored obligation, never a peer-posed one,
importing the same self-dealing Goodhart fix already used for
capability-lineage evolution (peer-posed-competence-as-the-anti-self-dealing-upgrade-to-delayed-addressability).

## Closing

Read end to end, the corpus is one argument stress-tested from every angle
available to it: a barrier separates predecessor from successor; a carrier
is the only thing that can legally cross it; every claim that something
legitimate crossed is itself vulnerable to the same narration-versus-exec
collapse the paradigm exists to detect; that vulnerability recurs at the
level of individuals, groups, bodies, demand markets, the repo's own
reflexive behavior, adversarial exploitation, and the measuring apparatus
itself; and the corrective at every level is the same move — replace an
assumed or narrated signal with one that is exec-gated, ablation-tested, or
externally calibrated.

## Self-reported losses

Forcing 69 sparks into one through-line flattened real disagreements into a
single register. I suppressed: differences in evidentiary maturity (closed
results like self-immunity-P1 sit next to zero-evidence analogies like the
RFC 8693 bridge, uniformly cited); the sparks' own internal "New sparks this
seed surfaced" sub-ideas, mostly dropped; paradigm-fit verdicts and
JSON rationale blocks, entirely omitted; and cross-spark tensions (e.g.
"silly rules" as functionally beneficial ossification versus the corpus's
default framing of ossification as failure) that deserved their own
paragraph but got compressed into a single adjective. Exact experimental
protocols, cost estimates, and citation lists are gone; filenames are the
only recovery path back to them.
