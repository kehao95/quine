## 3. Uniquely preserved

The single-narrative form is uniquely good at surfacing the corpus's genuine emergent property: the **same corrective move** (replace narrated signal with exec-gated/ablation-tested/externally-calibrated) recurring at every scale — individual, group, body, demand-market, reflexive, adversarial, instrument. Flat linked-objects, thematic clusters, or canonical+diff cards would preserve each object but lose this "one argument stress-tested from every angle" insight, which only appears in synthesis. It also preserves inter-spark bridges (e.g. `peer-posed-competence` importing the dorm-08 fix into `delayed-addressability`; `dual-discriminator` sharpening `selectable-entity`) that consequence-class or cluster schemes would scatter. Filenames-as-citations keep the recovery path open.
