# Loss Audit — Scheme B (flat objects + typed links)

> artifact. Verbatim record, 2026-07-02.

Setup check: the 52 objects partition all 69 sparks cleanly (verified: every `from:` file exists, none dropped, none duplicated). Loss is therefore entirely intensive (within-object compression), not extensive.

## 1. Folded dimensions

**Merges that fused opposed or distinct-question claims** (worse than mere omission — the reader cannot even tell two ideas were present):
- `demand-dynamics-cumulative-vs-reciprocal` fuses a *pathology* (cumulative-advantage monopolization can **fake** a selection signal — `demand-side-preferential-attachment`) with a *generative opportunity* (a mutant's service **mints** a heritable niche — `reciprocal-niche-construction`). The artifact's "demand co-evolves with bodies" erases that one is a risk and the other a mechanism.
- `attractor-unification-disposition` pastes `attractor-portfolio`'s generic entropy-contraction basin onto `governance-genre-compliance`'s "form-cued deference to governance **text**." The artifact implies mystical-loop attractors are governance-genre deference; the source says they arise with **zero** governance text present.
- `contamination-detection-as-instrument` implies one log-likelihood instrument (Min-K%++/ReCaLL) scores both target gaps; `web-scale-stigmergic` proposes an unrelated causal-ablation protocol and never mentions Min-K%++.
- `designed-vs-emergent-population-structure` presents `structural-inertia` as "one candidate discriminator" for `designed-vs-emergent`, but they answer different questions (is structure *detectable* vs. is it *carrier or narrative*).
- Also `capability-blind-spot-typology-and-search`, `bottleneck-degree-dial`, `salience-as-independent-heritable-axis`, `fep-formalization-gate`, `carrier-content-cost-metrics` each fuse two proposals with distinct scope/measurement loci.

**Motivating context lost:** the "why is this worth stating" framing is routinely cut, leaving claims parseable only by someone who already holds the repo's other concepts (e.g. `oversight-blind-channel-substitution`, `novelty-times-export-factorial`).

**Argument chains flattened:** dropped counter-data and self-corrections are the sharpest loss. `arbitrariness-axis-calcification` drops the 6M.03 "source is material, not destiny" counter-datum the claim must be scoped around. `exploit-niche-succession-order` drops that its own premise (regime-decomposition-P3's D4 theorem) was **contradicted** one day prior. `elicited-vs-heritable-variation-wedge` drops the H6-omission argument that is the file's actual teeth. Concrete numbers, base rates, and named alternatives vanish throughout (e.g. F1 scores in `labeled-independence`, ASR=0.73 in carrier-pull, order-effect nats in `barrier-algebra`).

**Links are largely load-bearing but systematically pruned** to 2–3 headline targets, and the dropped edge is consistently *the single most load-bearing one* — the actual gap/brief being extended: `cross-channel-heritability-test` keeps 2 of 6 links dropping the reused apparatus; `elicited-wedge` drops `11-diversification` (the brief it critiques); `wcst-battery` drops `14-bureaucratization` (the brief it replaces). Several kept links are decorative substitutions: `barrier-algebra-retrodiction` and `dgm-not-a-calibration-baseline` both link only `regime-decomposition`, which appears nowhere in their sources — the real edges (`10-multi-barrier`, `dd-07`) aren't representable because those aren't artifact objects.

## 2. Distortions introduced

- **Confidence inflation from unrun→asserted.** `co-evolving-evaluator-not-antidote` states as flat fact what the source calls "a well-grounded but unrun correction, not a finding." `stereological-benchmark-coverage` "turns...into a falsifiable number" vs. source's "zero direct evidence either way." Similar in `succession-complexity-anti-extraction`, `industry-succession-audit`, `carrier-content-cost-metrics`, `verifiable-commitment-channel`.
- **Relation-label over-precision.** `refines:`/`depends-on:`/`contradicts:` read one notch harder than the sources' hedges. `verifiers-dilemma` "depends-on" is a *refinement* in source; `verifiable-commitment-channel` "refines: fabricate-vs-fracture dichotomy" — source says it extends the open gap `revisable-time-semantics`; `viability-trait-disjointness` "contradicts" is really "assumes-not-certifies."
- **Dangling contradicts-targets:** `succession-generated-diversity`, `fabricate-vs-fracture` are named as contradicted/refined but are not objects in the artifact — unresolvable to an artifact-only reader.
- **Dropped hedges become overclaims:** `recursive-goodhart-ladder` loses its explicit "asymptotic, not terminating" (Rice's theorem) caveat, reading as more totalizing than intended.

## 3. Uniquely preserved

The scheme keeps what a narrative essay or thematic-cluster digest would lose: the **dialectical stance of each spark toward repo theory** — the `refines`/`contradicts`/`depends-on` edges preserve that these are *corrective moves on existing claims*, not free-floating ideas. Repeated link targets (`selection-signal-collapse`, `carrier`, `self-immunity-P1`) form a de-facto cross-cutting web that survives even though no cluster is named — a canonical+diffs or essay form would bury which sparks attack the same object. The one-object-per-idea granularity also preserves *count and separability* of the research program's open fronts better than prose would.
