# Cycle-9 Compression

## Decision

Promote one concept now:
[`sequence-carrier-topology`](../../../Paper/theory/objects/sequence-carrier-topology.md).

Retain all raw growth and seed files. Do not merge near-repeats. Cycle 9 produced
near-repeat clusters rather than exact filename repeats, and those clusters are
the useful common-refinement evidence.

## Why This Promotion

`sequence-carrier-topology` survived all three projections:

- Geometry: noncommuting repair paths can reveal a missing coordinate, curvature,
  confluence class, or generated direction.
- Autopoiesis: repair order changes membrane opening, reclosure, intermediate
  viability, scar formation, and identity succession.
- Repository practice: validator timing, ownership handoff, navigation windows,
  and closure order make sequence operationally visible.

The promoted object is intentionally narrow. It does not absorb
`commutator-friction`, `reclosure-holonomy`, `path-viability-equivalence`, or the
mechanism seeds. It only gives the primary child dimension a stable address.

## Ordered Next Moves

1. Derive a discovery-loop prediction from `sequence-carrier-assay` or
   `path-viability-equivalence-vector`.
2. Use a real maintenance case with two valid repair orders to test whether the
   residue appears in endpoint diff, validator state, ownership topology,
   future-agent reachability, or intermediate viability.
3. Promote `path-viability-equivalence` only if multiple assays need the same
   equivalence-vector vocabulary.
4. Promote `reclosure-holonomy` only if path-dependent closure residue recurs
   outside this cycle's metaphor space.

## Non-Promotions

- `commutator-friction` remains the generator seed, not yet a standalone object.
  The cycle showed it is productive, but the child dimension is currently more
  precise than the parent signal.
- `reclosure-holonomy` remains a secondary seed because it may overfit the
  geometry/autopoiesis overlap.
- `path-viability-equivalence` remains a seed until an assay needs its vector
  vocabulary.

## Closure

Cycle 9 closes as `SEED` work. It produced one promoted theory object, three raw
growth lineages, one collision synthesis, 36 retained seed files, and a curation
index. Scientific validation remains downstream.
