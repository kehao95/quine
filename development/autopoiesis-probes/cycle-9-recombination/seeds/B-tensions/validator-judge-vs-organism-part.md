# validator-judge-vs-organism-part

## Tension

A red validator can be treated as law judging content, or as organism tissue that
may itself be malformed. Fixing content first and fixing the sensor first can
produce different futures, even if both end green.

The conflict is between automation as external judgment and automation as a
mutable part of the repair apparatus.

## What it would take to resolve later

Resolution would require a critical-pair assay for red checks: evidence that the
sensor's scope, trigger, and metric are correct before content is reshaped around
it, or evidence that the content really violates a valid law.
