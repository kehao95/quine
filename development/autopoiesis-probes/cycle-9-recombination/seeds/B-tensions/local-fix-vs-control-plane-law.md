# local-fix-vs-control-plane-law

## Tension

Fixing a malformed local artifact before changing the control plane can erase the
anomaly that would have justified a durable rule. Changing the control plane
first can overgeneralize from a local accident and pull future work into a false
law.

The paradox is that both patchwork avoidance and rule inflation are real failure
modes, and the correct sequence may not be visible until one path has destroyed
or amplified the evidence.

## What it would take to resolve later

A later assay would need to preserve the anomaly long enough to test recurrence,
scope, and future-agent relevance before deciding whether the repair belongs in
the artifact, a local contract, a validator, or repo-wide law.
