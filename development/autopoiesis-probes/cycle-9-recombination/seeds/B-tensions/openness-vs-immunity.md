# openness-vs-immunity

## Tension

A permissive seed surface can create the tolerance needed for a later repair or
theory direction to enter. The same permissiveness can admit infection: malformed
variation, unowned residue, or endless seed accumulation.

The conflict is between immune strength and generative openness. Too much immune
response sterilizes the garden; too little dissolves the boundary of self.

## What it would take to resolve later

Resolution would require observing which early exposures make later variation
more competent, transportable, or self-maintaining, versus which exposures merely
increase unresolved residue and navigation burden.
