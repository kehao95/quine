# scar-debt-vs-memory

## Tension

Residual clauses, duplicated routes, or extra control-plane edges after an
order-sensitive repair may be cleanup debt. They may also be protective scar
memory: structure that keeps the system from reopening an old wound.

The paradox is that closure wants completed repair to simplify the repository,
while identity preservation may require visible memory of past injury.

## What it would take to resolve later

A later classifier would need to test whether the residue enables future action:
preventing repeated failure, improving sensing, or preserving boundary knowledge.
If it only increases navigation cost without changing future behavior, it is
debt rather than memory.
