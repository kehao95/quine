# repair-vs-reproduction

## Tension

Nonzero commutator residue can look like repair debt: a sign that two valid
operations should be represented so they compose cleanly. The same residue can
also be reproductive material: the alternating path exposes a child direction no
single repair would have generated.

The unresolved paradox is that reducing friction may erase the signal by which
the system grows, while preserving friction may leave genuine entropy in place.

## What it would take to resolve later

A later assay would need to distinguish residues that recur as basis-like
generators from residues that only consume maintenance energy. The deciding
evidence would be whether preservation produces transportable theory or future
repair capacity rather than just more exceptions.
