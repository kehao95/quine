# intermediate-viability-signal

**What:**

A signal for order-sensitive repair where both orders reach acceptable endpoints, but one path passes through a state that would be non-viable for a fresh agent. Non-viability can mean broken navigation, missing owner, red sensor with no route, ambiguous scope, or a surface that cannot explain its next action.

The candidate signal is not final failure but continuity loss. If A-then-B requires temporary blindness and B-then-A preserves sensing throughout, the residue belongs to path viability rather than content correctness.
