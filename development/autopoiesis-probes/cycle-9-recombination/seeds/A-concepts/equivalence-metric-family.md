# equivalence-metric-family

Commutator-friction depends on the metric used to compare repair orders. A residue can be large under file diff, small under theory-graph topology, invisible to validators, and decisive for fresh-agent reachability.

This seed names the family of comparison metrics rather than a single score. It leaves room for both refinement and relaxation: sometimes the representation needs a new coordinate, and sometimes the comparison is too fine for the question being asked.
