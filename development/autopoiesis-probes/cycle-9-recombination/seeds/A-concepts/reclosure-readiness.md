# reclosure-readiness

Closure can be coherent but unreceptive. A repair may leave the system green, indexed, and locally consistent while making the next valid perturbation harder to admit, harder to sense, or more likely to be misrouted.

This seed treats readiness as part of closure quality rather than an external convenience. It preserves the question of whether some noncommutators are caused by a system closing too tightly or too early for the next operation.
