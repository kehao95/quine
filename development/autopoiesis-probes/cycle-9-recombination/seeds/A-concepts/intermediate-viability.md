# intermediate-viability

A repair path can preserve or break the repository's ability to act before it reaches the final endpoint. A-then-B and B-then-A may leave similar final files while differing in whether a fresh agent, validator loop, or ownership route could survive each intermediate state.

The concept keeps endpoint coherence from absorbing all path information. It treats temporary incoherence as potentially meaningful, but leaves open whether every intermediate break is identity-relevant or merely acceptable construction scaffolding.
