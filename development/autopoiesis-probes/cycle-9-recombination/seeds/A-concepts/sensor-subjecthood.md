# sensor-subjecthood

A validator or readout can act as judge in one repair order and as repair subject in another. When content repair and sensor repair fail to commute, the residue may reveal whether the system treated sensing as external law or as mutable organism tissue.

This seed keeps the validator inside the sequence-carrier field. It does not decide whether a specific red check is right or wrong; it names the conceptual shift that occurs when sensing infrastructure participates in the repair it observes.
