# reclosure-holonomy

A repair loop can return to a coherent endpoint while leaving a twist in identity, readiness, routing, or memory. The residue is not necessarily a content delta; it may be the accumulated effect of having opened and reclosed through a particular path.

This concept borrows the shape of holonomy without requiring a formal geometry yet. It asks whether the repository carries closure history in ways that become visible only when opposite repair orders are compared.
