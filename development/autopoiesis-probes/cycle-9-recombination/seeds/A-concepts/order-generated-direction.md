# order-generated-direction

Alternating two valid operations can generate a direction that neither operation names alone. The residue is then not just failed composition; it is the first visible trace of a child dimension made reachable by the sequence.

This concept keeps generative noncommutation available as a theory-growth mechanism. It asks when friction should be reduced immediately and when it should be preserved long enough to reveal the new direction it is producing.
