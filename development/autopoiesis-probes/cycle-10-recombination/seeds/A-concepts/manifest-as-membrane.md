# Manifest As Membrane

A manifest can make projection siblings discoverable, but that same route surface can become the mouth that pre-digests them. If it summarizes too much, future agents inherit the manifest as the concept and stop reopening the lineages.

As membrane, the manifest would regulate permeability rather than provide consensus. It would carry identity, routes, statuses, tension labels, and non-flattening warnings, while refusing to own the interpretation that belongs inside projection files.

The unresolved tension is thickness. A membrane too thin becomes an ignored index; a membrane too thick becomes an authority wall. The manifest has to bind projections to one latent object without making membership equivalent to conceptual subordination.
