# Quotient Digestion Debt

Compression is necessary, but every compression is also a quotient operation: many projection readings are identified under a smaller residue. Digestion is necessary too, but every digestion transforms the projection-specific nutrients that made the lineage valuable.

Debt therefore cannot be discharged by source links alone. A backpointer can preserve provenance while losing the kind of divergence that was transformed. The debt is to state what tension was quotiented, what remains actionable, what was lost, and when the raw lineage must be reopened rather than cited decoratively.

## What it would take to resolve later

Resolve by distinguishing at least four obligations: source backpointer, lost divergence profile, transformed nutrient, and future reopening condition. A promoted residue that satisfies only the first is still in debt.
