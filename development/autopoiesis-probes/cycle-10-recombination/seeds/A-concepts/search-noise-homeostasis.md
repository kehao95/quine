# Search Noise Homeostasis

Positive overload is useful during divergent collision because many projection deposits preserve recombination potential. The same overload becomes entropy when fresh agents cannot distinguish live cover from accidental duplication.

Search-noise homeostasis names the threshold problem. The system needs enough ordering surface to route action without making that ordering surface the new consensus. Too little ordering creates avoidance; too much ordering recreates the one-file authority through an index.

## What it would take to resolve later

Resolve by identifying ordering forms that reduce navigation burden while preserving repeated names, projection-specific tension, and raw lineage reopenability. The success condition is not fewer files; it is less pressure to canonicalize prematurely.
