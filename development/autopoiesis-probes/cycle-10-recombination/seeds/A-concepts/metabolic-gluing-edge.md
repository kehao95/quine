# Metabolic Gluing Edge

A gluing edge is not a label between two projection files. It is a transition function that tells future agents how to traverse partial readings while preserving their non-identity.

The edge must be strong enough to make movement possible and weak enough not to digest the projections into one consensus. Too little structure leaves an unindexed cover; too much structure turns young divergence into compliance with an ontology chosen before the collision matured.

The concept asks whether relation types can carry digestion thresholds. `overlaps`, `conflicts-with`, `obstructs`, `refines`, and `is-quotient-of` are not schema proposals yet; they name different kinds of permitted motion through divergence.

## What it would take to resolve later

Resolve by showing at least one edge form that changes later-agent behavior without becoming a closed relation vocabulary. The test is not whether the edge is well named, but whether it lets an agent act through a tension that would otherwise be flattened or ignored.
