# Metabolic Transition Topology

A projection-bearing substrate may not be made of projection nodes at all. Its live unit is the transition: the route by which a later agent moves from one partial reading to another without pretending they are already the same reading.

That route has to carry three jobs at once. It is topological because it names overlap, conflict, obstruction, refinement, quotient, or failed gluing. It is metabolic because it says whether the relation is raw deposit, fermenting tension, digestible residue, over-digested summary, or sediment. It is governmental because it tells a fresh agent what action is safe: extend, collide, decompose, test, compress, preserve, or retire.

The tension is that each job can swallow the others. A graph edge without metabolism becomes inert metadata; a metabolic state without gluing becomes vague digestion language; a governance affordance without non-collapse becomes a route around the tension instead of through it.
