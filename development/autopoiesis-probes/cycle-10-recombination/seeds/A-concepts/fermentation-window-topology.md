# Fermentation Window Topology

Absorption lag is not automatically failure. A projection can remain partly undigested because its difference has not yet found the sibling, anomaly, or repair path that makes it useful.

But lag has shape, not just duration. A projection remains live only while it is both non-collapsed and traversable. Openness without route structure becomes sediment; route structure without openness becomes digestion. The fermentation window is the interval where divergence is still available as recombinable energy.
