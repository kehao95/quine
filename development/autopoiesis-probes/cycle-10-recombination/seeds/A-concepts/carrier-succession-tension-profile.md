# Carrier Succession Tension Profile

Projection material changes carriers: growth file, decomposed seed, typed edge, prediction, theory object, compression index. Each carrier makes some work easier while hiding some of the original tension.

The tension profile is what must survive those transitions. It is not only provenance or summary. It includes the lens-specific partiality, the sibling conflict, the obstruction or overlap that mattered, and the kind of future action the older carrier made possible but the newer carrier may no longer display.
