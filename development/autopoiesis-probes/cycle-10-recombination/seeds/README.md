# Cycle-10 Seed Curation Index

## Role

This is the compression layer over the 32 raw cycle-10 seed files. It orders the
material without flattening the three decomposition projections that generated
it.

The raw directories are not duplicates to clean up. They are independent
projection files:

- `A-concepts/`: 10 concept seeds
- `B-tensions/`: 10 tension/paradox seeds
- `C-mechanisms/`: 12 mechanism, signal, prediction, and experiment seeds

Total retained seeds: 32. Unique seed IDs by filename: 18. Exact repeated IDs:
10.

## Compression Rule

Do not merge repeated raw seed files unless a later promoted object explicitly
absorbs their obligations. In cycle 10, robustness appears as exact repeats plus
near-repeat mechanism variants: the same dimension is visible as a concept, a
preserved tension, and an assay path.

Promotion order:

1. Exact-repeat clusters connecting concept + tension + mechanism.
2. Near-repeat mechanism variants that could become discovery-loop predictions or
   fresh-agent assays.
3. Single-projection seeds retained as searchable material until reuse,
   friction, or transport pressure makes them promotion candidates.
