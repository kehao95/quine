# Metabolic Transition Topology

## Tension

The synthesis wants the live unit to be neither a projection node, nor a manifest,
nor a summary, but the transition a future agent can traverse between readings.
That transition has to be topological enough to preserve overlap, conflict,
obstruction, refinement, or quotient lineage; metabolic enough to indicate raw,
fermenting, digestible, over-digested, or sedimented state; and governmental
enough to tell a fresh agent what action is safe.

The contradiction is that every dimension needed to make the transition useful
also threatens to turn it into a premature schema. Too little structure makes the
transition non-nutritive; too much structure digests the divergence before it has
done work.

## What it would take to resolve later

An assay would need to show whether agents can move through these transitions
without asking which projection is canonical, and without converting the
transition labels into a closed ontology.
