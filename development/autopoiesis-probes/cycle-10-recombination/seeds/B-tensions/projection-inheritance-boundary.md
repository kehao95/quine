# Projection Inheritance Boundary

## Tension

A projection must inherit enough identity to remain part of one latent concept:
address, lineage, sibling comparability, and boundary conditions. But if it
inherits the parent's claims, the projection is contaminated before it can
diverge, and lineage becomes conceptual authority.

The graph lens wants weak identity, the metabolic lens wants membrane membership,
and the governance lens wants route identity. Each is necessary; each can become
the failure it prevents.

## What it would take to resolve later

Resolution would require a distinction between inherited lineage obligations and
inherited claims, with a test for when identity has started acting as authority.
