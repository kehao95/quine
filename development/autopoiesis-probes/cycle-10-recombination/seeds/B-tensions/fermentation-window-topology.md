# Fermentation Window Topology

## Tension

Absorption lag can be a fermentation chamber: a place where partial readings stay
alive until later lineages, anomalies, or repairs make their difference useful.
But the same lag can become sediment when projections remain findable only to the
session that created them or burdensome to fresh agents who need a route through
them.

The tension is not simply time. A projection remains live only while it is both
non-collapsed and traversable. Openness without gluing becomes residue; gluing
without openness becomes digestion.

## What it would take to resolve later

A later pass would need markers for when lag is still producing recombination
potential versus when it has crossed into search burden or cleanup debt.
