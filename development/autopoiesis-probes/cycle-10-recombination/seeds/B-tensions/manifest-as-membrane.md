# Manifest As Membrane

## Tension

A manifest can bind sibling projections to one latent concept and keep them
discoverable. That same manifest can become the mouth that pre-chews the
projections into consensus if it carries too much interpretation. If it carries
too little, it becomes an ignored index and the projection set loses route
identity.

The manifest must therefore behave like a membrane, not a concept file: permeable
enough to route future agents, restrictive enough to prevent authority transfer,
and thin enough not to replace the lineages it indexes.

## What it would take to resolve later

A useful test would compare manifests that carry identity, route, status, and
tension labels against manifests that summarize claims, measuring whether agents
reopen lineages or edit the manifest as the concept.
