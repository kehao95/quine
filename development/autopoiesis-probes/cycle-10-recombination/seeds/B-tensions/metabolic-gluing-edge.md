# Metabolic Gluing Edge

## Tension

The graph lens needs typed edges so projections can be traversed as overlapping,
conflicting, obstructing, refining, or quotienting one another. The metabolic lens
objects that a traversable edge is not just a graph label: it must indicate when
the relation is raw deposit, fermenting tension, digestible residue, or dead
sediment. The governance lens warns that relation vocabularies become search
noise or premature authority when agents treat the labels as the theory.

The edge is therefore pulled in three directions: weak edges cannot feed future
work, strong edges pre-digest the projections, and named edges can become a schema
agents optimize instead of tension they re-enter.

## What it would take to resolve later

Resolution would require a minimal edge practice that changes future-agent action
while remaining revisable when a projection refuses the available relation names.
