# Carrier Succession Tension Profile

## Tension

Projection material changes carriers: growth file, seed, edge, prediction,
compression surface, theory object. Each carrier increases some form of
reachability while losing another form of context. Raw growth preserves richness;
seeds preserve hooks; edges preserve transitions; theory objects preserve
canonical visibility and intensify the flattening enzyme.

The tension profile that made the projection valuable can disappear during a
successful carrier transition. The organism may think it digested the lineage
while actually preserving only its nutrient mass and losing its reproductive
code.

## What it would take to resolve later

Later work would need a way to record what tension each carrier can no longer
display, so succession does not become lineage amnesia.
