# Fresh-Agent Transition Assay

## Tension

The practical criterion is not whether projection files exist, or whether their
manifest is well linked. The criterion is whether a later agent acts through the
projection transitions: finding the relevant reading, preserving its partiality,
moving to sibling tensions, and recombining or decomposing without asking which
file is canonical.

The contradiction is that the assay itself may become a governance shortcut. If
it only checks presence, status, or route compliance, it can certify inert cover
as living substrate. If it tries to judge interpretation, it may flatten the
divergence it is supposed to preserve.

## What it would take to resolve later

Run fresh-agent probes across competing carriers and compare behavior: canonical
file, in-file sections, separate projection files plus manifest, and raw seeds
plus compression index.
