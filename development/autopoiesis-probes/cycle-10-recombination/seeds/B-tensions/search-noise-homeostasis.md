# Search Noise Homeostasis

## Tension

Positive overload is the point of divergent collision: many deposits preserve
dimensions a single lineage would erase. The same overload creates fresh-agent
burden. Without an ordering surface, agents may ignore the deposits or recreate
one authoritative concept file just to make navigation tolerable.

The unresolved contradiction is that ordering is both the cure and the risk. A
route surface can keep overload metabolically available, but it can also become a
summary surface whose apparent clarity displaces the raw projections.

## What it would take to resolve later

Resolution would require an ordering practice that routes agents to disagreement
and permitted next actions without ranking projections into a de facto consensus.
