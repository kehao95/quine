# Cycle 10 Collision Synthesis

## What Each Lens Makes Visible

The topological / graph lens makes visible that `divergence-substrate` is a
question about the shape of conceptual space inside one repository snapshot. A
single canonical concept file behaves like a quotient: it identifies divergent
local readings under one representative and lets that representative inherit the
status of the concept. Raw lens files behave like an unglued cover: they
preserve local charts but do not, by themselves, preserve the fact that these
charts are readings of one latent object. This lens sees identity as gluing data:
typed overlaps, conflicts, obstructions, refinements, and quotient lineage, not a
privileged global reading.

The autopoietic / metabolic lens makes visible that addressability is not yet
incorporation. A projection can be inside the repository and still be inert
sediment if later agents cannot act through it. It sees the substrate as a
semi-permeable organ for delayed assimilation: divergent readings are admitted
inside the organism's membrane, protected from immediate canonical digestion,
and kept available until a later collision or repair path can use their
difference. This lens contributes the threshold problem: deposition can become
digestion, digestion can become flattening, and absorption lag can become either
fermentation or sediment.

The repository-practice / governance lens makes visible that a substrate which
cannot survive fresh-agent navigation is not a substrate. Projection files,
manifests, indexes, statuses, and validators are not neutral containers: each
solves one failure mode while amplifying another. Separate files preserve lens
independence but create search noise. A manifest preserves route identity but
can become a premature authority. Validators preserve lineage links but can turn
open divergence into schema compliance. This lens sees projection-bearing
substrate as an inheritance interface whose success is measured by changed
future-agent behavior, not by the mere presence of deposited files.

## Non-Averaged Tensions

The graph lens wants more typed transition structure, while the governance lens
warns that every additional relation type can become search-noise or a premature
ontology. The metabolic lens refuses to let either side win: an edge that is too
weak is not traversable nutrition, but an edge that is too strong digests the
projection before its difference has done work. The residue is not "add better
edges"; it is that relation structure must carry a metabolic threshold, not just
a graph label.

The metabolic lens values absorption lag as a fermentation chamber, while the
governance lens treats unmanaged lag as fresh-agent burden. The graph lens adds
that lag without gluing is not fermentation but an unindexed cover. The tension
is not time alone. A projection remains live only while it is both non-collapsed
and traversable. Openness without route structure becomes sediment; route
structure without openness becomes digestion.

The topological lens treats quotienting as necessary but debt-producing. The
metabolic lens treats digestion as necessary but transformative. The governance
lens treats compression as useful but dangerous because summaries become the new
authority. These are not the same warning. A quotient can retain backpointers and
still be metabolically dead if no agent reopens them. A digestion can preserve
nutrients while losing the projection's reproductive code. A governance summary
can be perfectly linked and still replace the raw lineages in practice. The debt
is therefore not only "where did this come from?" but "what kind of divergence
was transformed, what remains actionable, and what must not be silently treated
as settled?"

Identity, membrane, and manifest collide. The graph lens wants weak identity:
the latent concept is not any projection. The metabolic lens wants membrane
membership: the projection is inside the organism but not fully assimilated. The
governance lens wants a manifest: future agents need a route to the projection
set. Each necessary carrier can become the failure it prevents. Weak identity can
be too empty to guide action. Membrane can become a wall. Manifest can become a
mouth that pre-digests the projections into consensus.

Obstruction is also split three ways. For the graph lens, failed gluing is a
meaningful topological object. For the metabolic lens, named conflict can become
immune memory against autoimmune cleanup. For the governance lens, naming
obstruction can become a lifecycle status that agents optimize instead of
testing. The substrate must preserve failed gluing without making conflict-hoard
a virtue.

## Emergent Child Dimension

**Emergent child dimension: `metabolic-transition-topology`.**

The higher-dimensional residue is that a projection-bearing substrate is not a
manifest plus projection files, not a graph of typed edges, and not a metabolic
fermentation chamber. It is a transition topology whose edges are metabolically
gated: each traversable relation between projections carries a topological job,
a digestion state, and a governance affordance at the same time.

In this dimension, the live unit is not the projection node alone. It is the
projection transition: the route by which a future agent may move from one
reading to another without pretending they are already one reading. A transition
is topological because it says whether the readings overlap, conflict, obstruct,
refine, or quotient one another. It is metabolic because it says whether that
relation is raw deposit, fermenting tension, digestible residue, over-digested
summary, or sediment. It is governmental because it says what action is safe for
a fresh agent: extend, collide, decompose, test, compress, preserve, or retire.

No lineage contains this alone. The topological lens sees transition but not the
metabolic viability of transition. The metabolic lens sees digestion and membrane
but not the typed gluing structure that makes delayed assimilation navigable. The
governance lens sees future-agent affordance and search-noise but not why the
route itself is the theoretical carrier rather than a mere index. Their collision
reveals that divergence is preserved only when transition remains both
non-collapsing and action-bearing.

`metabolic-transition-topology` therefore recuts identity. The latent concept is
not the parent file, the manifest, the best synthesis, or the set of raw
projections. It is the maintained field of metabolically gated transitions among
partial readings. Compression is allowed, but only as a transition operation with
quotient debt. Digestion is allowed, but only when it preserves what kind of
projection energy was transformed. Governance is allowed, but only when it routes
agents through the tension instead of around it.

## Secondary Dimensions

- `metabolic-gluing-edge`: an edge is not metadata; it is a transition function
  plus a digestion threshold plus a next-action affordance.
- `fermentation-window-topology`: absorption lag is viable only while enough
  gluing and ordering exist for future agents to traverse the open divergence.
- `quotient-digestion-debt`: every compression must preserve not only source
  links but the kind of divergence it transformed and what remains actionable.
- `manifest-as-membrane`: a manifest should regulate permeability among
  projections, not become the mouth that pre-chews them into consensus.
- `obstruction-immune-memory`: named failed gluing can protect living divergence
  from cleanup, but only if it remains testable rather than decorative.
- `fresh-agent-transition-assay`: the practical test is whether a later agent
  moves through projection transitions instead of asking which file is canonical.
- `search-noise-homeostasis`: positive overload is live cover only while an
  ordering surface routes action; beyond that threshold it becomes entropy.
- `carrier-succession-tension-profile`: as material moves from growth to seed to
  edge to theory object, it must preserve what tension profile the carrier can no
  longer fully display.

## Failure Modes

- **Consensus manifest:** the route surface becomes a summary surface, and agents
  inherit the manifest as the concept.
- **Schema reification:** typed transitions become a closed ontology that forces
  young divergence to fit old relation names.
- **Inert cover:** projection files exist and are linked, but no future action is
  routed through them.
- **Fermentation sediment:** absorption lag is praised after the material has
  stopped being findable, actionable, or recombinable.
- **Autoimmune validator:** checks intended to protect lineage instead punish
  projection-specific conflict as malformed state.
- **Backpointer theater:** compressed residues link to raw lineages while future
  agents never reopen the lineages or preserve their lost disagreement.
- **Obstruction hoarding:** every unresolved mismatch is named as an obstruction,
  making non-digestion look like tension preservation.
- **Search-noise collapse:** too many projection artifacts make fresh agents seek
  relief by recreating one authoritative file.
- **Claim-inheritance leak:** projection identity starts copying parent claims,
  so lineage inheritance becomes conceptual authority.

## Decomposition Targets

- `metabolic-transition-topology`: decompose the child dimension into transition
  jobs, metabolic states, and governance affordances without reducing it to a
  schema proposal.
- `metabolic-gluing-edge`: grow the edge as the primary carrier of preserved
  divergence, distinct from generic relation labels.
- `manifest-as-membrane`: test how a route surface can preserve identity and
  permeability while refusing conceptual authority.
- `fermentation-window-topology`: specify what keeps absorption lag alive and
  what marks its decay into sediment.
- `quotient-digestion-debt`: distinguish source backpointers, lost divergence,
  transformed nutrients, and future reopening obligations.
- `obstruction-immune-memory`: separate productive failed gluing from conflict
  hoarding and cleanup avoidance.
- `fresh-agent-transition-assay`: frame an assay for whether later agents act
  through projection transitions rather than canonicalizing around them.
- `search-noise-homeostasis`: explore the threshold where positive overload
  stops preserving recombination and starts forcing premature compression.
- `carrier-succession-tension-profile`: track what projection-specific tension
  must survive each carrier transition even when context is compressed.
