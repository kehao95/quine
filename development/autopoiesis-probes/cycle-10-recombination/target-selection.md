# Cycle 10 Target Selection: divergence-substrate

## Survey Surface

This selection follows a full survey of deposited theory across:

- `development/autopoiesis-probes/cycle-8-recombination/seeds/` — 136 retained seeds plus `seeds/README.md`
- `development/autopoiesis-probes/cycle-9-recombination/` — root records, growth lineages, synthesis, compression, and retained seeds
- `Paper/theory/objects/` — canonical theory object inventory and all object files

The current structural pull is toward making one canonical file hold one concept.
That pull is useful for addressability but dangerous for this target, because the
target is exactly the failure mode where one canonical surface collapses divergent
readings. This cycle therefore treats raw lens outputs and decomposed seeds as the
carrier, not as draft material to average into one consensus.

## Candidates Considered

- `trigger-validation split`: ripe and cross-cuts recombination method, but its
  central payoff is lifecycle discipline after a candidate exists.
- `agency-scaling / ownership-handoff topology`: ripe, especially through cycle-9
  carrier succession, but likely needs social/protocol objects and may sprawl.
- `path-viability equivalence`: strong cycle-9 common refinement, but still close
  to the already-grown `commutator-friction` branch.
- `projection-object / divergence-preserving deposition`: directly addresses the
  open problem in `divergence-substrate`, connects cycle-8 projection seeds with
  cycle-9 digestion, and is different from `commutator-friction`.

## Chosen Collision Target

**Target:** `divergence-substrate`, sharpened as **projection-bearing substrate**:
what makes divergent readings of one latent concept addressable, inheritable,
and recombinable inside one coherent repository snapshot without flattening them
into one authoritative reading too early?

## Why This Is Ripe

- `Paper/theory/objects/divergence-substrate.md` explicitly names the unsolved
  problem: neither git branches nor one-file-per-concept carry intra-snapshot
  cognitive divergence at multiple levels.
- Cycle-8 produced multiple direct seeds: `projection-object-type`,
  `projection-object`, `versioned-concept-fragments`,
  `divergence-preserving-deposition`, and
  `addressability-divergence-flattening`.
- Cycle-9 adds the missing metabolic dimension: `addressability-vs-digestion`,
  `absorption-lag-assay`, and `carrier-succession` show that deposition is not
  incorporation unless future agents can find and act through the material.
- The target is `scope: theoretical` and autonomous-safe for seed growth. No
  paradigm-scope object edit is required.

## Lens Plan

Three medium-distance lenses will grow the same target independently:

- **A. Topological / graph lens:** identity versus projection, quotient versus
  cover, and what relation structure preserves non-collapsed readings.
- **B. Autopoietic / metabolic lens:** deposition, digestion, membrane, carrier
  succession, and when a projection becomes metabolically available.
- **C. Repository-practice / governance lens:** concrete file/layout/control-plane
  forms that let agents preserve divergence without search-noise overload.

## Closure Shape

This round should leave:

- deposited lens outputs under `growth/`
- a tension-preserving collision under `synthesis.md`
- decomposed seeds under `seeds/`
- an ordering/compression surface under `compression.md`
- promotion of any robust child concept into the canonical theory graph only if
  the collision produces a small, useful residue rather than a premature schema
