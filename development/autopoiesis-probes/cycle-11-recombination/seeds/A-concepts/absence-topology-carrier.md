# absence-topology-carrier

Structured absence may act as a carrier of sensing behavior in fresh-agent
habitats.

A missing task, empty inbox, absent target, disabled exit, zero-entry surface, or
unfilled control slot can stimulate an agent to search, infer, or maintain. The
absence is not positive transmitted content, but the habitat can make lack itself
encounterable and actionable.

Preserved tension: this may be a real environmental carrier or a model-prior
artifact. Fresh agents may inspect absence because of training or prompt habit,
not because the repository topology carried the stimulus.

Candidate handle: ablate positive traces while preserving or altering shaped
absences, then compare fresh-agent behavior across tool affordances and models.
