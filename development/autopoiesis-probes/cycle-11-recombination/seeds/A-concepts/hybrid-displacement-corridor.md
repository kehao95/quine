# hybrid-displacement-corridor

Carrier replacement may usually pass through a corridor of hybrids rather than a
clean old-carrier versus new-carrier switch.

Wrappers, registries, queues, watchers, status files, adapter bodies, and fallback
contracts can keep enough obligation relation alive while changing the surface
that carries it. Old-carrier fragility can create the compensatory structure that
later becomes the new carrier.

Preserved tension: lock-in and innovation are not opposites here. Entrenchment
can block replacement, but it can also build the bridge by which replacement
becomes possible.

Candidate handle: force a lineage from one carrier surface toward another and
score the transition corridor, not only the final winner.
