# sensor-manufactured-carrierhood

Sensors and validators can manufacture carrierhood by turning passive residue
into a blocking or salient future obligation.

A stale fact, missing field, public note, status line, or latent drift may not
change behavior until an executable sensor reports it. The carrier may therefore
be residue plus sensor plus activation schedule, not residue alone.

Preserved tension: governance both discriminates and selects. It can prevent
false carriers by filtering noise, but it can also erase exaptable weak traces or
create the salience that makes one trace causal.

Candidate handle: plant equivalent residue with and without an activated sensor,
then measure whether future behavior follows the residue, the report, or neither.
