# carrier-assemblage-selection

The selected carrier may be an assemblage whose parts live in different sidecar
modes, not an atomic class such as fd, file, source, queue, or narrative.

Examples include opaque handle plus protocol map, mailbox plus registry,
filesystem residue plus watcher, stale fact plus validator, source plus summons,
or narrative plus legitimacy membrane. The assemblage carries only because its
parts jointly preserve, expose, authorize, and route the obligation.

Preserved tension: self-description is not automatically a carrier. This seed
does not promote narrative or source into carrierhood by declaration; it marks
cases where an operational survivor is unusable without an enabling resource.

Candidate handle: measure whether removing one component leaves survival intact
but breaks uptake, summons, legitimacy, or reproduction.
