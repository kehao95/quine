# lease-salience-dual-horizon

Carrier selection may be governed by two independent expiry horizons: physical
lease expiry and salience or legitimacy expiry.

A route can expire physically when a process dies, a queue clears, a socket
closes, a lease lapses, or an address becomes stale. It can also expire
attentionally when a file falls out of indexes, a validator stops running, a
public surface loses authority, or a mailbox keeps existing but loses naming
power.

Preserved tension: durable bits are not durable carrierhood. A physically weak
surface can win early through salience; a physically persistent surface can lose
when no agent treats it as legitimate or actionable.

Candidate handle: vary cleanup horizon and salience path independently, then
record time-indexed changes in the selected winner.
