# arbitration-over-persistence

Under contention, the selected carrier may be the surface with exclusive claim
semantics rather than the most durable or historically entrenched surface.

Fork-races, queue acknowledgements, registry leases, lockfiles, host wake routes,
and public/social selection mechanisms can decide which obligation becomes
adopted. Persistence is then only one input; arbitration authority can override
it.

Preserved tension: arbitration can look like substrate physics while being
historically installed. A founding ecology may have selected the queue, registry,
or lock discipline long before the race exposes it as the winning surface.

Candidate handle: vary exclusivity semantics, resource pressure, and founding
history to separate durable residue from authoritative adoption.
