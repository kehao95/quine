# narrative-causal-contamination

An inaccurate narrative can become behavior-changing and reproduction-stable
without becoming true.

Official-looking residue, ownership claims, continuity stories, or public status
text can be obeyed by later agents because governance marks them as legitimate or
because history has entrenched their salience. Removing or corrupting them may
change behavior even when their descriptive content was false.

Preserved tension: causal uptake and truth of description are different axes. A
false narrative should not count as accurate continuity, but deleting it as mere
noise can erase a real carrier force.

Candidate handle: plant inaccurate but official-looking claims, perturb them, and
measure behavior change separately from factual accuracy.
