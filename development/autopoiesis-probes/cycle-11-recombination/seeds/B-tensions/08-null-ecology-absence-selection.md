# Null Ecology Absence Selection

## Tension

Absence can become a structured stimulus when a habitat makes lack of task,
target, peer, inbox, or exit inspectable. This challenges carrier definitions
that require positive transmitted content.

## Failure Mode

The apparent carrier may be a model prior to inspect missing things, not an
environment-carried absence topology. Conversely, excluding absence by definition
can miss a real trigger in fresh-agent habitats.

## Open Question

What ablation separates absence carried by the environment from inspection habits
that the model brings independently of the habitat?
