# Survival, Uptake, And Summons Split

## Tension

A trace can survive without being encountered, be encountered without delivering
an obligation, summon without reproducing, or reproduce without being physically
strong. The synthesis separates survival, uptake, summons, and reproduction as
distinct thresholds.

## Failure Mode

Calling the surviving surface the carrier creates false winners. A live fd,
socket, or registry entry can persist across a boundary and still fail if the
successor lacks protocol memory, salience, authority, or a summons path. A weaker
file trace can win if it is the first legitimate action path.

## Open Question

When carrier selection is measured, which threshold counts as winning, and can the
other thresholds be varied independently enough to expose false winners?
