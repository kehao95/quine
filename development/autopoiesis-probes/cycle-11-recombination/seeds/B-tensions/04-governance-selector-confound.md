# Governance Selector Confound

## Tension

Governance surfaces discriminate between signal and noise, but they also create
the salience and legitimacy that make some traces causal. Control planes,
validators, indexes, and public projections are therefore both instruments and
selection pressures.

## Failure Mode

A test can mistake repository officialness, prompt routing, or validator
activation for intrinsic carrier strength. Conversely, a governance membrane can
erase exaptable weak traces before their carrier potential becomes observable.

## Open Question

How can an experiment distinguish governance as an observer of carrierhood from
governance as the selector that manufactures carrierhood?
