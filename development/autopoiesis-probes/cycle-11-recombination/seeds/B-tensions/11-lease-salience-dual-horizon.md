# Lease And Salience Dual Horizon

## Tension

A carrier can expire physically through fd loss, queue expiry, stale registration,
lease expiration, or dependency drift. It can also expire attentionally when a file
drops out of indexes, a validator stops running, a public surface loses trust, or a
mailbox loses naming authority.

## Failure Mode

Bits that persist indefinitely can be misread as stable carriers after their
salience or legitimacy horizon has expired. Conversely, short-lived operational
routes can dominate if they retain authoritative summons during their lease.

## Open Question

Does carrier selection follow the earlier of physical lease expiry and salience /
legitimacy expiry, or can one horizon reactivate the other?
