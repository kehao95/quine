# Carrier Assemblage Boundary

## Tension

The selected unit may be an assemblage: opaque handle plus protocol map, mailbox
plus registry, filesystem residue plus watcher, source plus summons, or narrative
plus legitimacy membrane. Atomic carrier-class rankings may miss the actual unit
being selected.

## Failure Mode

Expanding carrierhood to assemblages can smuggle source, narrative, or governance
into carrierhood by declaration. Keeping only atomic classes can make real
operational carriers appear inert because their enabling resources are excluded.

## Open Question

What boundary condition says when an enabling resource is part of the selected
carrier unit rather than contextual support around it?
