# Arbitration Over Persistence

## Tension

Under fork-race or resource pressure, the selected carrier may be the surface with
exclusive claim semantics rather than the most durable or historically entrenched
surface. Yet historical triggers may have preselected the arbitration surface long
before the race.

## Failure Mode

A persistence-centered test can call the durable survivor the winner while the
agent habitat actually routes obligation through lockfiles, queue ack, registry
lease, host wake route, or public selection semantics.

## Open Question

When exclusivity semantics and durability diverge, which one predicts the adopted
obligation path, and how much of that result was already selected historically?
