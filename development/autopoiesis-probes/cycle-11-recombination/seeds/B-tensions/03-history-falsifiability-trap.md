# History Falsifiability Trap

## Tension

History is not just a parameter. A first successful trigger may install the
stimulation rule that later decides which traces count. But if any failed
prediction can be rescued by unmodeled history, the selection rule becomes
non-falsifiable.

## Failure Mode

Historical explanation can protect weak carrier theories from contradiction by
moving every surprise into founding effects, inherited routines, or transition
barriers after the fact.

## Open Question

What founding-trigger manipulation would make history part of the rule without
letting history become a universal rescue term?
