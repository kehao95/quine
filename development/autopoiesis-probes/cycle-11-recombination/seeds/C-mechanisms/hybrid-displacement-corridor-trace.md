# hybrid-displacement-corridor-trace

**What:** Carrier replacement may pass through hybrid corridors instead of
switching directly from old carrier to new carrier.

Trace transitions where an old surface becomes fragile and compensatory wrappers,
registries, queues, watchers, status files, or adapter bodies appear. The
mechanism predicts that the transition corridor can be the selected unit for a
period, with parts in different sidecar modes.

Trigger != validation. Seeing a wrapper or registry appear after fragility is a
candidate displacement trigger, not proof that it carried the obligation.
Validation requires showing that removing the hybrid corridor disrupts obligation
continuity even when old and final surfaces remain available.
