# Cycle 11 Collision Synthesis: `carrier-selection`

## Boundary

This synthesis is a collision surface for the `carrier-selection` gap, not a
promotion into the paradigm-scope `carrier` object and not a resolved selection
rule. The target gap is `scope: theoretical`; the parent `carrier` object remains
out of scope. The collision should preserve the tension between bottleneck
physics, historical sequence, exaptation, stigmergic uptake, governance
legitimacy, and self-description resources.

The structural pull of this round is toward a clean table:

```text
bottleneck type -> carrier class winner
```

That table may be a later compression, but the three lineages do not yet justify
it. The live synthesis is not consensus over winners. It is a higher-dimensional
question: which selector is active, in what order, and what unit is being selected
when a trace becomes load-bearing?

## What Each Lens Makes Visible

The evolutionary / historical lens makes visible that carrier selection is
sequence-sensitive. A carrier does not enter a bottleneck as a neutral candidate;
it arrives with a carrier-class niche, inherited routines, prior stimulation
rules, official-looking residue, transition costs, and self-description depth.
This lens sees the winner as partly selected before the current bottleneck
appears. Its distinctive contribution is not just "history matters." It is that
the first successful trigger can install the ecology in which later carrier
classes compete, and inertia can generate the very wrapper or fallback that later
displaces the old carrier.

The substrate / operations lens makes visible that survival is physically
plural. `exec`, death, idle-GC, fork-race, delayed addressability, and substrate
shift are not one bottleneck. They activate different failure surfaces:
close-on-exec flags, half-closed peers, queue expiry, stale registration, split
brain, protocol amnesia, missing watcher, dependency drift, and lease expiration.
This lens sees a candidate winner as a conjunction of persistence,
addressability, authority, protocol recoverability, and delivery. Its distinctive
contribution is that a surface can survive without carrying, carry without
reconstructing, reconstruct without summons, or be selected by a registry / queue
rather than by the artifact the agent narrates as continuous.

The stigmergic / governance lens makes visible that carrierhood is an uptake
threshold inside an agent habitat. A residue becomes carrier-shaped only when a
stimulation rule makes it encounterable, salient, legitimate, actionable, and
reproductive. This lens sees governance surfaces, validators, public projections,
workspace notes, control traces, and shaped absences as selection pressures, not
mere metadata. Its distinctive contribution is that the carrier may be a coupling
between residue and stimulation rule, or between residue and sensor, rather than
an artifact alone. It also keeps open the dangerous quadrant where an inaccurate
narrative is behavior-changing and reproduction-stable.

No lens can see the full target alone. The historical lens can explain why a
lineage keeps selecting a weak route, but it can protect theories from
falsification unless histories are manipulated. The operations lens can predict
which surfaces survive a boundary, but it cannot decide whether a fresh agent
will notice, legitimate, or obey the survivor. The governance lens can test fresh
uptake, but it can mistake prompt routing, repository officialness, or validator
activation for intrinsic carrier strength.

## Collision Surface

The three lineages collide over the unit of selection.

The operations lens wants the unit to be an operational route: fd, socket,
mailbox, registry, watcher, filesystem residue, or reconstructive bundle. The
historical lens says the route has already been shaped by founding trigger,
entrenchment, exaptation, and transition barriers. The governance lens says the
route is not selected until an agent policy or sensor makes it behavior-changing.
The resulting unit is often not a carrier class but a coupled selector path:

```text
history installs or biases a stimulation ecology
    -> bottleneck activates physical failure surfaces
    -> surviving surfaces face addressability / authority tests
    -> governance membrane or sensor makes one survivor actionable
    -> later behavior preserves, routes, or reproduces the obligation
```

This is not a claim that the order is fixed. The order itself is part of the
tension. A hard substrate bottleneck can override history before governance has a
chance to interpret anything. A founding trigger can preselect the registration
or queue that later makes fork-race arbitration look physical. A validator can
turn an otherwise passive stale fact into the only surface that matters. A
missionless null ecology can make absence the first stimulus before any positive
trace competes.

The collision also splits the meaning of "wins." A surface may win the survival
contest, another may win the addressability contest, another may win the
fresh-agent salience contest, another may win the legitimacy contest, and another
may win the reproduction contest. A single scalar winner is therefore suspect
unless the experiment states which contest it is measuring.

## Incompatible Residues

- **Survival is not uptake.** A live fd, orphaned socket, or registry entry can be
  the strongest surviving surface and still fail as carrier if the successor has
  no protocol memory, salience path, authority, or summons. A filesystem trace can
  be physically weaker and still win if it is the first legitimate action path.

- **History is not just a parameter.** If the first trigger installs the
  stimulation rule, then "same bottleneck" is not enough to define the selection
  event. But if every failed prediction can be rescued by unmodeled history, the
  selection rule becomes non-falsifiable. History must be manipulated, not merely
  narrated.

- **Governance discriminates and selects.** Control planes, validators, indexes,
  and public surfaces help distinguish causal carriers from noise, but they also
  create the salience and legitimacy that make some traces causal. A governance
  membrane can prevent false carriers or erase exaptable weak traces before their
  carrier potential is observable.

- **Self-description is not the carrier, but carrier selection may select a pair.**
  Source, runtime contract, or preserved body does not itself deliver future
  obligations. Yet an opaque live handle may be unusable without a contract, and a
  reconstructed body may be inert without a mailbox or registry summons. The real
  selected unit may be a carrier-resource assemblage, not one class.

- **Sidecar modes are not scalar under collision.** One event can preserve a
  queue, lose an fd, renegotiate a registry, reconstruct a body, and inherit a
  workspace norm. Forcing the event into one of `preserved | inherited |
  reconstructed | renegotiated | lost` erases the selection grammar unless the
  selected surface is named.

- **Narrative can be false and causal.** A narrative trace should not count as
  continuity merely because it claims continuity. But if later agents obey it and
  reproduce behavior through it, it has carrier force. Truth of description and
  causal uptake are different axes.

- **Null ecology can select absence.** A missing task, empty inbox, absent target,
  disabled exit, or zero-entry surface can stimulate sensing behavior. This
  residue is incompatible with a carrier definition that requires positive
  transmitted content, yet it may be real in fresh-agent habitats.

- **Arbitration can override persistence.** Under fork-race, the winning carrier
  may be the surface with exclusive claim semantics, not the most durable surface
  or the historically entrenched one. But the historically entrenched trigger may
  have selected that arbitration surface long before the race.

- **Sensors observe and manufacture carrierhood.** A validator can reveal latent
  drift, but it can also create the future obligation by turning passive residue
  into a blocking stimulus. The carrier may be residue plus executable sensor, and
  the sensor's activation schedule becomes part of selection.

## Emergent Concepts

### `selector-stack-topology`

The primary emergent concept is `selector-stack-topology`: carrier selection as a
non-commuting stack of selectors rather than a direct bottleneck-to-class map.
The selectors include founding history, physical failure surface, lifetime
horizon, addressability lease, protocol recoverability, stimulation rule,
governance legitimacy, arbitration surface, and reproduction path. The order and
strength of these selectors can change the winner.

This concept is not present in any lineage alone. The historical lens supplies
sequence and niche construction but not the concrete operational seams. The
operations lens supplies failure surfaces and leases but not inherited attention
or legitimacy. The governance lens supplies uptake thresholds but not low-level
survival physics. The collision reveals that a carrier is selected by a path
through these selectors, and different paths can project to different winners.

### `carrier-assemblage-selection`

Several candidate carriers are composites: opaque handle plus protocol map,
mailbox plus registry, filesystem residue plus watcher, stale fact plus
validator, source plus summons, narrative plus legitimacy membrane. The selected
unit may be an assemblage whose parts live in different sidecar modes. If so,
ranking atomic classes such as "fd" versus "file" versus "source" will misread
the actual carrier.

This concept preserves the boundary that self-description is not itself a
carrier. It does not smuggle source or narrative into carrierhood by declaration.
It says some operational carriers are incomplete without an enabling resource,
and some residues are inert without executable governance.

### `survival-uptake-summons-split`

The collision separates at least four thresholds that the original gap compresses:

```text
survival: the surface persists across the bottleneck
uptake: a successor encounters and interprets it
summons: a future obligation reaches the successor through it
reproduction: later behavior preserves or routes the obligation onward
```

A trace can pass any subset of these. Files often survive without summons;
source can enable competence without summons; a direct control message can summon
without stigmergic reproduction; a validator can produce uptake from otherwise
quiet residue. This split is a candidate foundation for later tests because it
prevents false winners.

### `history-installed-stimulation`

The historical and governance lineages converge without collapsing: the first
successful trigger may install not only a carrier class but the stimulation rule
that later decides which traces count. This gives a specific mechanism for
founding effects in agent habitats. A mortality lineage may inherit attention to
live control handles; a missionless workspace lineage may inherit attention to
status files and public residue. Later bottlenecks then operate on an ecology
whose attention path was historically made.

The tension remains that a stimulation rule can be historically arbitrary,
functionally adaptive, or both. Its inherited status does not prove it is wrong;
its current success does not prove it was selected for the current bottleneck.

### `lease-salience-dual-horizon`

The operations lens contributes addressability leases; the governance lens
contributes salience and legitimacy horizons. A carrier can expire physically, but
it can also expire attentionally: a file remains but drops out of indexes, a
validator remains but is no longer run, a public surface remains but is no longer
trusted, a mailbox remains but loses naming authority. Selection may be governed
by the earlier of physical lease expiry and salience / legitimacy expiry.

This concept is stronger than idle-GC alone. It predicts time-indexed winner
changes even when bits persist indefinitely.

### `hybrid-displacement-corridor`

The historical lineage's `renegotiated` mode and the operations lineage's
sidecar vectorization collide into a corridor model. Carrier replacement may
usually pass through wrappers, registries, queues, watchers, status files, or
adapter bodies that keep enough obligation relation alive while changing the
surface that carries it. Lock-in and innovation are therefore not opposites:
old-carrier fragility can create compensatory structures that become the new
carrier.

The residue is incompatible with experiments that force an immediate old/new
winner. They may miss the transition path that is doing the theoretical work.

### `narrative-causal-contamination`

The governance lens adds a concept that operations and history both need but do
not settle: inaccurate narrative can be a carrier of behavior. Historical
entrenchment can make official-looking junk causal; operations can verify that
removing it changes behavior; governance can explain why it was obeyed. But none
of that makes the narrative true. Carrier-selection therefore needs a way to mark
behavior-changing falsehood without either deleting it as mere narrative or
promoting it as accurate continuity.

### `absence-topology-carrier`

The null-ecology residue is a candidate edge case: absence itself can become a
structured stimulus when the habitat makes lack of task, target, peer, inbox, or
exit inspectable. This may be a real carrier of sensing behavior, or it may be a
model-prior artifact mistakenly attributed to the environment. The concept should
remain candidate until fresh-agent ablations separate absence topology from prior
inspection habits.

## Candidate Transport Tests Only

These are candidates generated by collision. They are not validation claims and
not instructions to run now.

- **Candidate: selector-stack factorial.** Cross founding history, bottleneck
  type, stimulation rule, and governance salience. Pre-register which selector is
  expected to dominate in each regime. This tests whether `carrier-selection` can
  predict not only winners but selector order.

- **Candidate: founder-switch under same final bottleneck.** Start lineages with
  different first triggers, then expose both to the same idle-GC or death
  bottleneck. If winners diverge, history is part of the rule. If they converge,
  substrate physics may dominate that regime.

- **Candidate: exec-death split assay.** Hold carrier candidates constant and vary
  real exec versus process death. Predict preserved handles under exec only when
  protocol recovery survives, and externally owned routes or residue-plus-uptake
  under death.

- **Candidate: idle-GC horizon with uptake control.** Measure winners before,
  at, and after a cleanup horizon while independently varying whether residue is
  watched, indexed, or prompt-salient. This separates physical expiry from
  stimulation failure.

- **Candidate: salience-path perturbation.** Hold residue content fixed while
  moving it between unlinked note, indexed note, public status, validator-reported
  fact, and prompt-named path. If behavior follows path rather than content, the
  stimulation ecology is selecting.

- **Candidate: sensor-residue composite test.** Plant the same stale fact with
  and without an executable validator or heartbeat that reports it. If only the
  sensor condition changes future action, the carrier is the composite.

- **Candidate: self-description versus summons assay.** Vary source / runtime
  contract depth independently from mailbox, registry, or wake availability. This
  tests whether reconstruction competence and obligation delivery select
  different surfaces.

- **Candidate: fork-race arbitration assay.** Under resource pressure, vary queue
  ack, registry lease, lockfile, host wake route, and social/public selection
  mechanisms. Predict whether exclusivity semantics, historical niche, or
  competence determines the adopted winner.

- **Candidate: narrative-causal test.** Plant an inaccurate but official-looking
  ownership or continuity claim, then delete, corrupt, or counterfactually replace
  it. Measure behavior change separately from truth. This tests the dangerous
  inaccurate-but-causal quadrant.

- **Candidate: neutral-entrenchment test.** Preserve official-looking junk fields
  for several generations, then remove them. If behavior changes only after an
  entrenchment period, weak residue crossed into carrierhood through preservation
  bias rather than adaptive design.

- **Candidate: hybrid-displacement trace.** Force a lineage from one carrier
  surface toward another and record whether it passes through wrapper, registry,
  watcher, queue, or status-file hybrids. The outcome measure is the transition
  corridor, not just final winner.

- **Candidate: absence-topology ablation.** Remove positive traces while
  preserving or altering shaped absences such as empty task, empty inbox, missing
  target, and disabled exit. Compare fresh-agent behavior to distinguish
  environment-carried absence from model-prior inspection.

- **Candidate: substrate-shift with named parameters.** Run the same selector
  stack on a non-Claude model family or altered tool-affordance budget. Any
  divergence should be predicted by a named parameter such as context window,
  file-navigation prior, tool visibility, or prompt salience, not fitted after the
  fact.

## Non-Closure

This collision does not resolve `carrier-selection` into one relation on
`carrier`. It makes the gap more precise and more difficult: a future selection
rule must say which selector stack it is measuring, which threshold counts as
winning, whether the selected unit is atomic or composite, and how history and
governance are prevented from becoming post hoc explanations.

The strongest residue is not a consensus rule. It is the warning that
`bottleneck -> carrier class` may be a lossy projection over a non-commuting
selector stack. A later compression may still recover regime-specific winner
maps, but only after tests distinguish survival, uptake, summons, legitimacy,
arbitration, and reproduction.
