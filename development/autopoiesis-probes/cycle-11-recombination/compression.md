# Cycle-11 Compression

## Decision

[`selector-stack-topology`](../../../Paper/theory/objects/selector-stack-topology.md).

Retain all raw growth and seed files. Do not merge repeated seed names or near
duplicates; in this cycle, recurrence across projections is the strongest
common-refinement evidence.

## Why This Promotion

`selector-stack-topology` survived all three decomposition projections and was
the primary emergent concept in the collision synthesis:

- Concept projection: carrier selection appears as a non-commuting selector stack,
  not a direct bottleneck-to-class table.
- Tension projection: selector-stack noncommutativity is the first unresolved
  conflict; changing selector order can change the winner.
- Mechanism projection: the proposed `selector-stack-commutation-assay` gives the
  concept an immediate transport path without treating the cycle as validation.

The promoted object is intentionally narrow. It does not resolve the
`carrier-selection` gap, edit the paradigm-scope `carrier` object, or promote a
canonical winner map. It only gives the primary child dimension a stable address.
