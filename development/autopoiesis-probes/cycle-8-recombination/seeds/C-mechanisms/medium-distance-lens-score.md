# medium-distance-lens-score

**What:** Recombination yield peaks at medium lens distance.

Build this as a probe portfolio selector by scoring how far two lenses are apart in vocabulary, ontology, and evidence base. Very near lenses may duplicate; very far lenses may not integrate; medium distance should produce the richest collision.

It proves useful if medium-distance pairings outperform near and far pairings. It fails if yield is monotonic with distance or dominated by agent skill rather than lens distance.
