# collision-record

**What:** A durable record preserves parents, non-overlap, disagreement, child axis, and validation plan.

Build this as the standard carrier for recombination material before synthesis. Each collision record states what each parent sees, what the others miss, where they conflict, what child axis becomes possible, and how the child will be tested.

It proves that collisions can be made inspectable rather than reduced to narrative summary. It fails if later agents cannot reconstruct why the child relation exists or what would invalidate it.
