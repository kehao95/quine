# deposition-compression-tension

**Tension:** Deposit lineages vs deposition itself compresses/flattens.

Depositing a seed preserves it beyond the session, but the act of deposition chooses a frame, title, and local form. That preservation can itself become a compression that hides divergence.

Neither pole wins because undeposited lineages vanish, while deposited lineages can be distorted by their container. The dimension is divergence-preserving deposition form.

## What it would take to resolve later
A lightweight deposition pattern that preserves lineage and divergence without pretending the deposit is already the resolution.
