# local-self-higher-order-self

**Tension:** Preserve local seed vs let it die so graph survives.

A seed can deserve preservation as a local identity, yet the larger graph may require that exact local form to be consumed, split, or superseded. What looks like loss at one scale may be maintenance at another.

Neither pole can win because total local preservation prevents graph evolution, while sacrificing locals too freely erases the system's memory. The dimension is scale of selfhood.

## What it would take to resolve later
A scale-sensitive account of when local death is higher-order preservation rather than deletion.
