# permeability-noise

**Tension:** Open membranes admit variation vs boundaries prevent overload.

Permeability lets new material, probes, and collisions enter the system. Boundaries keep that influx from overwhelming addressability and coherence.

Neither pole can win because sealed systems cannot evolve, while fully open systems cannot maintain themselves. The dimension is controlled permeability.

## What it would take to resolve later
A membrane model that specifies which variation may enter, at what rate, and under what containment.
