# fitness-scalar-viability-addressability

**Tension:** External scalar fitness vs internal viability + addressability.

Scalar fitness makes selection simple and comparable, but self-maintaining systems may depend on viability, addressability, and continued participation in the graph. A single external number may miss what lets the system live.

Neither pole wins because evaluation needs pressure, while self-maintenance needs richer survival criteria. The dimension is fitness for self-maintaining systems.
