# openness-recombinability

**Tension:** Leave ideas open vs make them bounded/labeled/heritable.

Open ideas remain generative because they resist premature closure. Recombination, however, needs bounded and addressable material that can be selected, inherited, and collided.

Neither pole wins because openness preserves possibility, while heritable form enables later operators. The dimension is genotype conditions.

## What it would take to resolve later
A genotype condition for when an open idea is sufficiently bounded to recombine without being over-closed.
