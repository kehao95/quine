# selection-shadow

**Tension:** Audit/gates maintain discipline vs early selection suppresses novelty.

Gates and audits protect the system from overclaiming, drift, and incoherence. Applied too early, those same mechanisms can select against weak but fertile novelty before it has a chance to recombine.

Neither pole wins because discipline is necessary for truth, while early discipline can become anti-discovery. The dimension is phase ordering.

## What it would take to resolve later
A phase-ordering rule for when selection pressure should be delayed, softened, or applied.
