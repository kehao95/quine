# compression-branching-potential

**Tension:** Simplicity now vs evolvability later.

Compression earns its force by reducing present structure, but early removal can erase branches that would become valuable under a later collision. Branching potential is not noise just because it has not yet compressed.

Neither pole can win globally: pure simplicity closes options too early, while preserving every option dissolves the pressure that makes understanding usable. The hidden dimension is temporal option value.

## What it would take to resolve later
An account of temporal option value: when an apparently non-compressive branch should be retained because it increases later recombination capacity.
