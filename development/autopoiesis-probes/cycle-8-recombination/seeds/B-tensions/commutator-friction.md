# commutator-friction

**Tension:** A-then-B vs B-then-A.

If applying two operations in different orders yields different results, the order is carrying information. That noncommutativity can indicate a missing dimension that the current representation treats as irrelevant.

Neither pole wins because some operations should commute under a good abstraction, while failures to commute can be discovery signal. The dimension is noncommutativity as missing-dimension evidence.

## What it would take to resolve later
A way to interpret order-dependence as either abstraction failure or legitimate structural information.
