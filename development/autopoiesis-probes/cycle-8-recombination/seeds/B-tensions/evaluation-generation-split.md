# evaluation-generation-split

**Tension:** MDL scoring vs recombination candidate-generation.

MDL can rank or compress candidates, but recombination may generate the candidates that MDL alone would not search toward. Treating evaluation as the whole process loses the operator that changes the candidate population.

Neither pole can win because generation without evaluation becomes drift, while evaluation without generation becomes local optimization over an impoverished set. The dimension carried here is population-level transition operators.

## What it would take to resolve later
A model that separates scoring from population transition while showing how both participate in discovery.
