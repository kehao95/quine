# maintenance-generation

**Tension:** Cleanup/coherence repair vs reproductive variation.

Maintenance removes drift and restores coherence, but generation requires variation that can initially look like disorder. Cleanup can accidentally sterilize the reproductive substrate.

Neither pole wins because the system cannot live without coherence, and it cannot grow without variation. The dimension is mode boundary.

## What it would take to resolve later
A mode boundary that distinguishes clutter needing cleanup from variation needing preservation.
