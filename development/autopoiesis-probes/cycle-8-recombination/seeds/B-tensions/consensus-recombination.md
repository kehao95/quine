# consensus-recombination

**Tension:** Keep common part vs preserve conflict for latent dimension.

Consensus safely extracts what parents share, but recombination may depend on the unresolved conflict that consensus would smooth away. The conflict can be the pointer to a dimension neither parent names alone.

Neither pole can win because commonality gives inheritance, while preserved conflict gives discovery. The dimension is integration as enzyme rather than averaging.

## What it would take to resolve later
An integration account that preserves conflict when it is generative and compresses it only when it is merely redundant.
