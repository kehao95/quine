# redundancy-complementarity

**Tension:** Compression values shared structure vs recombination values complementary difference.

Shared structure makes compression possible, but complementary difference supplies the material that lets recombination produce something not already present in either parent. Too much sameness collapses into consensus; too much difference prevents inheritance.

Neither pole wins because useful parents must be similar enough to couple and different enough to generate. The dimension is parent-selection geometry, especially the Goldilocks zone.

## What it would take to resolve later
A parent-selection geometry that identifies when redundancy supports recombination and when complementarity becomes unbridgeable.
