# artifact-gap-boundary-gap

**Tension:** Missing template vs undefined semantic boundary.

A failure may appear as a missing artifact or template, but the deeper issue may be that the semantic boundary of the thing is undefined. Adding the artifact can hide the boundary gap.

Neither pole wins because concrete artifacts are needed, while templates cannot substitute for ontology. The dimension is spec gaps as dual failures.

## What it would take to resolve later
A diagnostic that distinguishes artifact absence from an undefined semantic boundary before repair chooses a form.
