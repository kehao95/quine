# single-candidate-search-ecology

**Tension:** Optimize one hypothesis vs maintain population.

Single-candidate search gives pressure, direction, and measurable improvement. Population maintenance protects diversity, latent complements, and alternative future closures.

Neither pole can dominate because one hypothesis can become a trap, while an unmanaged population can become incoherent. The tension carries the unit of optimization.

## What it would take to resolve later
A criterion for when the search object is a single candidate and when it is an ecology of candidates.
