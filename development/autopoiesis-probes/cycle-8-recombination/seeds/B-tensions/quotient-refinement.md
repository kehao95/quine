# quotient-refinement

**Tension:** Compression discards distinctions vs recombination recovers them.

Compression forms a quotient by treating some differences as irrelevant. Recombination can later reveal that a discarded distinction was actually a hidden axis shared only across certain collisions.

Neither pole can win because compression needs equivalence classes, while discovery sometimes comes from reopening the kernel of a prior quotient. The dimension is kernel intersection and hidden-dimension recovery.

## What it would take to resolve later
