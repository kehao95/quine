# conflict-radius

**Tension:** Keep changes narrow vs honest new instance requires many surfaces to change.

Narrow changes reduce risk and preserve reviewability. But a real new instance can be distributed, requiring multiple surfaces to move together for the change to be honest.

Neither pole wins because excessive radius becomes churn, while too-small radius creates partial truth. The dimension is constraint topology.

## What it would take to resolve later
A topology-aware notion of change radius that distinguishes scope creep from necessary distributed repair.
