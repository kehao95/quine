# identity-preservation-liquidity

**Tension:** Maintain boundary vs loosen to re-close higher.

Identity needs a boundary to remain addressable, but recombination may require loosening that boundary so a higher-level form can close. Too-rigid identity blocks transformation; too-liquid identity loses continuity.

Neither pole wins because self-maintenance needs both closure and controlled boundary movement. The dimension is identity level as movable.

## What it would take to resolve later
A criterion for when identity boundaries may loosen without dissolving the lineage they are meant to preserve.
