# delete-overclaim-preserve

**Tension:** Delete unsupported vs promote beyond evidence vs preserve as open.

Unsupported material is risky if treated as claim, and overpromotion damages evidence discipline. But deletion can destroy a live seed before it has a chance to mature.

Neither pole wins because the real third move is often preservation as open rather than deletion or promotion. The dimension is demotion as third epistemic move.

## What it would take to resolve later
A demotion practice that preserves unsupported but fertile material without letting it masquerade as validated claim.
