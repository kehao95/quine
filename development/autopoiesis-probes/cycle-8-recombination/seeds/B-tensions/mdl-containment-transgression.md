# mdl-containment-transgression

**Tension:** Recombination as higher-scale MDL vs temporary anti-compression.

Recombination can look wasteful because it proliferates variants, unresolved collisions, and awkward hybrids. At another horizon, that same proliferation may be the move that allows a later, stronger compression.

Neither pole can win without specifying the accounting window. Immediate MDL discipline protects against clutter, but higher-scale MDL may require a temporary transgression of local compression. The hidden dimension is the time horizon of compression accounting.

## What it would take to resolve later
A horizon-sensitive compression account that distinguishes local anti-compression from higher-scale compression work.
