# retention-compression-metabolism

**Tension:** Retain every branch vs compress after enough instances.

Branch retention preserves diversity and future recombination material. Compression after enough instances prevents the system from becoming a junk drawer of unabsorbed variation.

Neither pole wins because metabolism needs both storage and absorption. The dimension is absorption threshold.

## What it would take to resolve later
An absorption threshold for when retained branches should stay separate, compress into a pattern, or be deleted.
