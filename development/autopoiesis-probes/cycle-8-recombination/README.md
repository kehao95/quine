# Cycle-8 Recombination Probe

## What this directory is

Retained output from the cycle-8 divergent-collision recombination experiment.
The experiment grew [`evolutionary-recombination`](../../../Paper/theory/objects/evolutionary-recombination.md)
through three lenses, collided the lineages, and decomposed the collision into
addressable seeds.

Status: mechanism-level validation of the workflow, not scientific validation of
the theory claims. The raw lineages and seeds are retained as reproductive
material; promotion requires later transport, prediction, or functional-use
evidence.

Read in this order when reconstructing the experiment:

1. [`../../../Paper/theory/objects/evolutionary-recombination.md`](../../../Paper/theory/objects/evolutionary-recombination.md) - seed object and owning theory surface

- [`seeds/B-tensions/`](./seeds/B-tensions/) - tension/paradox seeds
- [`seeds/C-mechanisms/`](./seeds/C-mechanisms/) - mechanism, signal, prediction, and experiment seeds

## Compression decision

Do not merge or delete same-named raw seed files during the first compression
pass. Duplicate names across projections are evidence of common refinement, not
redundant clutter. The compression layer is the curation index, plus any later
promotion into `Paper/theory/objects/`, `Paper/theory/gaps/`, or
`Paper/predictions/` after reuse or transport pressure.

Cycle-8's immediate absorption is:

- [`reproductive-code-topology`](../../../Paper/theory/objects/reproductive-code-topology.md) - SEED theory object for the child dimension exposed by the collision
- [`seeds/README.md`](./seeds/README.md) - ordered common-refinement map for future promotion
- [`development/contracts/divergent-collision.md`](../../contracts/divergent-collision.md) - method updated with the required post-overload compression pass

## Projection maintenance

- source owner: raw outputs in this directory and the owning theory objects under `Paper/theory/objects/`
- projection role: navigation and compression index for one recombination probe cycle
- freshness trigger: adding/removing seed files, promoting a seed to an object/gap/prediction, or changing the divergent-collision procedure
- drift check: compare counts and promotion targets in [`seeds/README.md`](./seeds/README.md) with files under `seeds/*/*.md` and current theory objects
- absorption owner: stable theory lands in `Paper/theory/objects/`, testable claims land in `Paper/predictions/`, and raw probe artifacts remain here as lineage evidence
