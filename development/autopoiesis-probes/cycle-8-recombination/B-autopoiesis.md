# Cycle-8 Divergent Growth — Autopoiesis / Identity-Boundary Lens

## Core Growth — recombination is a boundary event

For a self-maintaining system, identity is normally maintained by keeping a boundary
coherent under entropy. Recombination says: sometimes the system must loosen, split,
or dissolve that boundary to escape a local identity basin. The paradox:

> A self-maintaining system may preserve itself only by regulatedly ceasing to be
> itself at one level, so that a higher-level self can re-close.

Candidate concept: **recombinant autopoiesis** — a system that maintains identity not
by preserving one uninterrupted form, but by controlling when and how identity
boundaries open, exchange material, and re-close around a viable organization.

## Where the Biological Analogy Holds / Breaks

Autopoiesis (Maturana/Varela) emphasizes operational closure, boundary production,
self-producing organization. The analogy breaks if we ask sexual recombination to
preserve the same *individual*. In biology, sexual reproduction preserves
lineage/population/species continuity while producing a new individual. The gametes
are not the parent; the zygote is neither parent.

For this repo, the "same self" is usually not the individual agent session or seed.
The higher-order self is **the repository as maintained research organism**.
Divergent seed lineages can dissolve as local selves while enriching the repo-level self.

## Identity Scale — fusion addressability

Existing continuity theory says strong same-lineage claims require carrier-indexed
continuity. Recombination asks the **many-to-one fusion** version of `branching-identity`:
when two lineages merge, whose identity survives?

Candidate: **fusion addressability** — a recombined object earns continuity with
parent lineages only if it preserves their obligation basins, not merely their
vocabulary. Otherwise it is only a narrative hybrid.

## Boundary Paradox — the regulated membrane

The boundary is not a wall; it is a regulated membrane.

- Too rigid: preserves current identity, blocks hybridization, traps in local minima,
  turns "smallest coherent repair" into basin lock-in.
- Too porous: admits every narrative as self, collapses carrier/function into prose,
  creates addressability noise, risks narrative complexity masquerading as functional complexity.
- Best case: selectively porous; variation deposited but labeled seed; relations explicit
  for future recombination; promotion gates fire only when material becomes claim-bearing;
  compression after divergence.

**Branching potential is not simple openness — it is controlled permeability.**
Biological sex requires boundaries (gametes, meiosis, species barriers). Repository
recombination needs object files, relation links, seed labels, addressability. Without
those, there is no recombination, only chat residue.

## Minimal Modification Upgraded

Layer 0's graded resolution = local homeostasis. Layer 3's hybridization = dimension
escape. The synthesis: recombination is the deeper minimality.

> The smallest coherent repair inside the current code may be maladaptive when the
> current code is the local minimum. Recombination is the smallest repair after
> lifting the system into a larger code space.

Biology does this constantly: recombination doesn't invent every gene from scratch;
it preserves functioning modules and changes their combination.

## Fitness Without External Fitness

For a self-maintaining system, fitness is not a scalar from outside. The repo's
`viability-pressure` already decomposes survival into mortality risk, persistence
opportunity, maintenance demand.

Fitness reframed: the ability of an organization to remain inside its viability
boundary while preserving enough future addressability to meet obligations it cannot
yet predict. A hybrid is "fit" if it preserves future addressability, remains
competent across parent obligation basins, reduces entropy without killing branch
potential, and creates a better surface for future repair.

## The Strongest New Tension — identity liquidity

> Autopoietic maturity is not maximal boundary stability. It is the capacity to
> decide which boundary level may dissolve without losing the higher-order
> organization that re-closes the system.

This connects to **evolutionary transitions in individuality**: when recombination
succeeds, individuality may move upward. The old unit stops being the true self; the
higher-order organization becomes the self that owns mortality, persistence, maintenance.

In repo terms: the individual seed may die, the theory graph survives; or the graph
reorganizes, the research program survives. The self is wherever carrier-indexed
obligations continue to route and be competently handled.

## The Repo As Recombination Engine

The Reflexive Instance section is directly relevant:
- divergent agents = variation generators
- seed files = deposited genetic material
- relation links = recombination handles
- Human/integrator collision = recombination enzyme
- future agents = offspring environment
- promotion = selection
- validators/dossiers = reproductive barriers

**Addressability debt is fatal here.** Undeposited insight cannot recombine. A
brilliant branch trapped in chat is biologically equivalent to a mutation that never
enters the germline.

The cycle needed: `deposit branch → preserve addressability → collide → test
obligation coverage → compress only after recombination yield appears`.

## Structural Pull Risk on Recombination Itself

Recombination workflows are vulnerable to capture:
- audit-shaped integration erases divergence
- premature "best synthesis" averages away minority alleles
- promotion gates firing at intake sterilize seeds
- strong maintenance default collapses generation into cleanup

The SEED contract is not a softness loophole; it is the **reproductive niche** where
variation remains viable long enough to become useful.

## 5 Experiments Proposed

1. **Fusion-addressability test:** merge two divergent seed branches, later ask fresh agents to act on obligations from each parent. If both route competently, fusion holds.
2. **Porosity A/B:** rigid-gated vs fully-open vs seed-labeled semi-permeable surfaces. Measure diversity, recombination yield, noise burden.
3. **Addressability-debt test:** hold one branch in chat, deposit another. Later integrators recombine. Prediction: undeposited contributes little.
4. **Narrative-hybrid control:** prose summary vs functional relation graph with parent obligations. Agents perform better from the graph.
5. **Many-to-one identity test:** extend branching-identity from fission to fusion.

## Distilled Claim

> **Recombination is autopoiesis with a regulated wound.**

The self opens its boundary, admits divergent organization, lets local identity
become unstable, then re-closes at a higher-dimensional organization. If it cannot
re-close, recombination was dissolution. If it never opens, self-maintenance becomes
ossification. The living system metabolizes boundary instability into renewed identity.
