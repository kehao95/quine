## Scope / out of registry

- **Excluded (not governing control plane):** historical *design* docs, now all
  under `Paper/_design/migrations/` — frozen design records, not standing rules;
  the learning/history layer (`EVOLUTION.md`, `evolution/periods/*`); and the
  theory net (`Paper/theory/objects|views|gaps`) and exploratory philosophical
  models such as computational epistemology and the mutual-encoding network.
  Frequently used philosophical perspectives, working vocabulary, and
  procedures are included even when their directory is philosophy or theory.
- **Threshold rationale:** 200 lines is the "consider compress/split" pain line,
  not a hard cap. `AGENTS.md`'s own growth analysis (it reached 941 lines by
  additive amendments outpacing pruning) is the motivating failure mode.

## Total budget (advisory health indicator)

The `budget_lines:` frontmatter above serves as an **advisory reading-cost
indicator on the summed line count of all registered files**, tracked by
`./scripts/check-control-plane-size.sh`. Rationale: the per-file pain line alone
incentivizes splitting (which resets each file's counter without shrinking the
surface); the total budget provides a holistic view of the governing surface.

- **Advisory health signal:** Size monitoring surfaces bloat as an advisory
  indicator rather than a blocking commit tripwire, avoiding zero-sum editing friction
  while preserving cognitive visibility.
- **Interpretation:** Line count is a surface-size proxy. It is not semantic
  compression, total reading cost, organizational closure, or scientific value.
  Compare changes using the same file population; changing registry membership
  does not constitute compression.
- **Registering a new file** adds its lines to the total: creating governing
  surface remains transparent and tracked.
