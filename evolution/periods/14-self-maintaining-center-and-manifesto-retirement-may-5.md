## Period XIV: Self-Maintaining Center and Manifesto Retirement (May 5)

### May 5 — Philosophy center realignment

**Key commits:** 6862d1b7a

**What changed:** Re-centered active navigation around
`Paper/philosophy/self-maintaining-systems.md` as Quine's philosophical center,
reframed `north-star.md` as the runtime ontological cut inside that center, and
clarified that the repository-as-living-control-plane and process-native runtime
are enacted layers of the same self-maintaining-systems frame. Removed the old
active `Paper/core/manifesto.md` surface and routed surviving claim references
through the philosophy, axiom, evidence, and repository-self-model owners.

**Friction:** The previous repair had created a philosophy layer, but active
navigation still let the process-native manifesto and "agent = process" slogan
compete with the stronger Human-stated center: Quine as a self-maintaining
system and the repository as a living control plane. That produced exactly the
kind of interpretive confusion the self-model is supposed to sense and repair.

**Structural repair:** Demoted runtime ontology from whole-project center to
runtime-layer realization, retired the obsolete polemical core surface, and
updated root / Paper / status / roadmap / repository-self-model routing so
future agents encounter the self-maintaining center before the runtime slice.

**What it taught:** In a self-maintaining repository, identifying a confusing
center is itself maintenance signal. A slogan that once compressed discovery can
become drift when the system's living self-model has grown past it.
