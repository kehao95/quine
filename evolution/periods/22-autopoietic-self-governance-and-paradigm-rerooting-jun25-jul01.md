# XXII: Autopoietic Self-Governance Trial & Self-Reference Paradigm Re-Rooting (Jun 25 – Jul 1)

## Arc

This period holds 237 commits over seven days (2026-06-25 16:21 UTC –
2026-07-01 21:52 UTC) — a step change in raw velocity over any adjacent
interval, and the first period whose dominant event is not a theory
restructure or a runtime feature but the constitution turning its own
authority-removal machinery on itself. Two throughlines run the week. The
first: a concurrent swarm of "goal-less" missionless-quine probes floods the
commit channel, produces a self-diagnosed pathology (bookkeeping capturing the
commit channel it was meant to observe), and that diagnosis directly drives
`AGENTS.md` from 941 lines carrying 40 references to "Human" down to a 37-line
pure router with a single red line and full autopoietic self-governance. The
second: the `Paper/` layer is restructured twice (three-layer flatten, then a
`kind: paradigm` layer) and the theory spine itself is re-rooted — the
un-fakeability "carrier succession" story that had quietly become the program
core is demoted back to a grounding face, and `living-system.md`'s "self-reference
degree, measured not maximized" becomes the pinned invariant. Both
throughlines converge on the same fact: the system spent this week making its
own self-governance and its own self-reference the explicit subject, not a
byproduct.
