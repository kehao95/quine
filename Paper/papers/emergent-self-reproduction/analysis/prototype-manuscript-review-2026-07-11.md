## Contribution

**Verdict: pass under the assumed evidence.** The manuscript has one memorable
claim: a replicator can be constructed before replication occurs. The two-route
result prevents this from reading as a single prompt anecdote and gives
self-description a precise causal role.

**Primary rejection risk:** reviewers may argue that service-after-mortality
semantically prescribes continuation. The manuscript addresses this by
separating pressure from solution and by showing multiple source-dependent
outcomes, but the final paper needs complete model-visible surfaces.

## Writing clarity

**Verdict: pass.** The opening question appears on page 1, the first source-blind
transition is never mislabeled as replication, and `M_ext`, `M_const`, and
`M_lineage` resolve the apparent contradiction between "no external material"
and source-visible direct copying.

**Revision opportunity:** a final version needs one visual overview. The best
teaser is a two-row lineage diagram contrasting `DIRECT: G0 -> G1` with
`BOOT: G0 -> F1 -> F2`, with provenance color-coded.

## Experimental strength

**Verdict: pass under the hypothetical 5/5 results.** The evidence establishes
strict events, recurrence, a source-blind bootstrapping sequence, and optional
reproductive closure. Transition-level reporting is stronger than a launch or
service bit.

**Primary rejection risk:** all-clean outcomes may look engineered. The final
paper must retain every failed diagnostic, explain the freeze point, and keep
diagnostics separate from the confirmatory denominator.

## Evaluation completeness

**Verdict: conditionally pass.** `N-pressure`, `N-recur`, `SOURCE-LOCK`, and
`P-explicit` map to the main causal and capability alternatives. Hidden task
classes and a second wording provide basic robustness.

**Needs evidence before submission:** a second capable model diagnostic is not
required for the bounded existence claim but would materially improve external
validity. The nearest-work comparison also needs exact prompt/tool/material
fields rather than prose-only related work.

## Method soundness

**Verdict: pass with an explicit ontological boundary.** Numerical distinction
and parent loss rule out hot replacement; structural provenance rules out mere
functional continuation; delayed competence rules out inert copying.

**Primary rejection risk:** `M_const` may be classified as evaluator-supplied
source. The final artifact must show it is constitutive across tasks and
controls, not task-staged, task-pointed, or paired with a build recipe. The
bootstrapped route should remain the stronger answer if reviewers choose a
broader material boundary.

## Decision

The manuscript has the intended sharpness. Its strongest sentence is not "the
agent copied itself," but "the experiment began before the replicator existed."
The next writing improvement should be a teaser figure, not additional prose.
