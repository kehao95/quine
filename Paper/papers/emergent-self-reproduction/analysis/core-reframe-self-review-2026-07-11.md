## Writing clarity

**Question:** Can a reviewer tell what is absent and what remains?

**Verdict:** yes. The core distinguishes an externally assigned objective from
an instrumental intention, a reproduction-specific mechanism from generic
computation, and externally staged material (`M_ext`) from constitutive
self-description (`M_const`) and lineage-authored material (`M_lineage`).

**Open risk:** use the full provenance terminology whenever "no material" is
stated. The unqualified phrase would be false because both routes eventually

## Experimental strength

**Open gate:** freeze the organization manifest and fidelity threshold before

## Evaluation completeness

**Question:** Do the controls address the main alternative explanations?

**Verdict:** the planned matrix covers endpoint capability (`P-explicit`),
pressure recruitment (`N-pressure`, `N-recur`), and endogenous source use

**Open gate:** controls begin at one or two diagnostics and expand only when a

## Method soundness

**Question:** What would a skeptical reviewer attack first?

**Verdict:** three risks are load-bearing:

1. `M_const` could be dismissed as an evaluator-supplied scaffold. The source
   must be embedded in the ordinary agent distribution before task assignment,
   remain present in matched controls, and have no `self_source` label, pointer,
   build recipe, or task-contingent reveal.
2. Mortality plus future service could semantically prescribe a successor. The
   complete prompt, files, tool schemas, errors, and manuals must state the
   pressure while omitting the construction solution.
3. `F1 -> F2` could produce a competent but sterile copy. The paper may claim a
   reproduction event at `F2`, including the core emergence result, but not
   inherited reproductive closure unless `F2 -> F3` independently closes.

The observer must also avoid becoming the reproducer: it may attest, kill,
release tasks, and score, but not construct, repair, launch, wrap, or forward
through the offspring.
