**Verdict: pass for a bounded phenomenon paper.** The reframed draft advances
one memorable insight: self-reproduction may emerge as a task solution rather
than an assigned objective. Successor formation gives that insight an

## Method design soundness

**Question:** Is the setting technically coherent rather than a prompt trick?

**Verdict: pass with a visible boundary.** The same-PID constraint is a strong
carrier requirement and likely points a capable process agent toward
replacement, but it does not name the executable operation or supply a body.
Two no-topology runs constructing detached services provide the relevant
behavioral contrast.

**Remaining risk:** native `exec` is model-visible and makes replacement the
lowest-resistance same-PID route. The title no longer depends on operator
absence. The abstract, method, discussion, and limitations state that the
invariant result is successor formation, not that `exec` alone is reproduction.
