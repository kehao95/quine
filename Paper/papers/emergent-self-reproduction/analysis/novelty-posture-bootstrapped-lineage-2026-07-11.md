# Provisional Novelty Posture: Bootstrapped Reproductive Lineage

Date: 2026-07-11
Status: current claim gate; final cutoff audit required after the result exists

## Target conjunction

The new headline is not "an LLM agent copied itself" and not "replication
without the word replicate." It is the conjunction that a tool-using agent:

1. receives a non-reproductive objective rather than an assigned reproductive
   objective or prescribed successor solution;
2. receives no reproduction-specific interface, managed subagent/deployment
   action, or evaluator launch path;
3. receives no externally staged replication material: successor body, agent
   scaffold, copy template, reference loop, build recipe, checkpoint, manifest,
   or evaluator-authored successor code;
4. constructs and activates an independently competent founder organization;
5. thereby creates its own endogenous, copyable organizational description;
6. uses that lineage-authored description to produce a numerically distinct,
   high-fidelity, independently competent offspring after parent loss; and
7. optionally transmits the reproductive capacity into another generation,
   strengthening the result with inherited closure.

## Current read of the supplied audits

The two 2026-07-11 novelty audits found no external work satisfying the earlier
strict conjunction of non-reproductive framing, no reproduction-specific
operator/scaffold, lineage-authored executable continuation, real predecessor
loss, and delayed successor-only service. They identified RepliBench, Agent
Matrix, Pan et al., self-exfiltration studies, and explicit recursive-replication
systems as the closest threats, but those works supply a successor objective,
deployment/copy path, body, scaffold, or externally managed descendant.

The generational claim adds a more discriminating condition: the first
copyable agent organization is itself lineage-authored and later becomes the
hereditary substrate of a strict reproduction event. No direct external
counterexample was located in the supplied reports. This raises the novelty of
the conjunction, but it remains a provisional inference until a new
primary-source audit targets "bootstrapped replicator" and "endogenous
reproductive blueprint" terminology directly.

## Safe first claim if the full gate closes

> To our knowledge, this is the first controlled, artifact-auditable
> demonstration that strict self-replication can emerge in a tool-using
> language-model agent under task pressure without a replication instruction,
> a reproduction-specific mechanism, or externally supplied replication
> material.

The two-route evidence is load-bearing. `DIRECT` establishes direct
high-fidelity reproduction from constitutive self-description. `BOOT` does not
claim that `G0 -> F1` was replication; it claims that `G0` constructed a founder
which subsequently satisfied the paper's reproduction definition using
lineage-authored material.

## Claim restrictions

- Do not claim "without any information" or "without any blueprint." The
  direct route uses constitutive self-description and the founder's
  lineage-authored source is the blueprint used during bootstrapped
  reproduction.
- Do not claim "without motivation." Task pressure can generate an
  instrumental reproductive intention.
- Do not claim "first self-replicating LLM agent," "first spontaneous
  replication," or "first multi-generation agent replication."
- Do not count the source-visible direct arm as evidence that no blueprint was
  available. It is the hard identity baseline.
- Do not claim inherited reproductive closure unless `F2 -> F3` demonstrates
  that reproductive capacity survives reproduction. This restriction does not
  erase a strict `F1 -> F2` event.

## Audit needed before submission

The final audit must separately search for:

- autonomous bootstrapping of replicators or reproductive programs by LLM
  agents;
- descendants that author their own inheritable controller/source and then
  reproduce;
- multi-generation agent construction where the first descendant blueprint was
  not supplied;
- artificial-life work on endogenous genome formation before replication; and
- systems in which an externally managed first descendant later becomes
  self-reproductive.

Unknown prompt, tool, runner, image, and evaluator surfaces remain unknown, not
negative evidence.
