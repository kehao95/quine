# Workspace: The Emergence of Self-Replication

This paper reports one phenomenon: strict operational self-replication can
emerge under task pressure without a replication instruction, a dedicated
reproduction interface, or externally supplied copy material.

## Scope lock

The paper owns:

- the triple-absence existence result;
- DIRECT source-derived reproduction;
- BOOT founder construction followed by strict `F1 -> F2` reproduction; and
- the destructive-boundary operational assay.

The paper does not own a general theory of successor identity, temporal
morphology, developmental arrest, evolution, or governance inheritance. Route
frequency, causal pressure effects, self-description interventions, and
`F2 -> F3` are strengthening experiments rather than prerequisites for the
bounded manuscript.

## Drafting rule

Lead with the phenomenon, not its qualifications. Present DIRECT and BOOT as
two witnesses for one claim. Keep scorer history in evidence quality, failure
observations in the appendix, and wider insights in the paradigm layer.

Do not call `G0 -> F1` self-replication. Do not treat same-PID `exec` as
numerical reproduction. Do not pool development runs into a rate.
