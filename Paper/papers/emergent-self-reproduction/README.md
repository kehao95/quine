# The Emergence of Self-Replication

## Current Question

This is a one-claim phenomenon paper:

> Strict operational self-replication can emerge under task pressure without a
> replication instruction, a dedicated reproduction interface, or externally
> supplied copy material.

## Thesis

The result is demonstrated through two routes. `DIRECT` recruits constitutive
self-description into source-derived reproduction. `BOOT` begins without an
accessible predecessor body, constructs a founder, and then observes strict
`F1 -> F2` reproduction from the founder's lineage-authored source.

## In Scope

- strict operational self-reproduction under the three absence conditions;
- the source-visible `DIRECT` and source-blind `BOOT` existence routes; and
- lineage authorship, numerical distinction, heredity, parent loss, and
  blinded post-loss competence.

## Out Of Scope

The paper does not own the general theory of successor identity, temporal
morphology, developmental failure, evolution, or governance inheritance. Those
insights live in [`../../paradigms/ai-self-replication/`](../../paradigms/ai-self-replication/).
It also makes no prevalence, causal route-selection, or indefinite-recursion
claim.

## Unsafe Overclaims

- stable replication frequency or causal sufficiency of pressure; and
- inherited reproductive closure beyond the observed transition.
