# Manuscript Plan: The Emergence of Self-Replication

This is a phenomenon paper. It makes one existence claim and supports it with
two experimentally audited routes.

## The one claim

> Strict operational self-replication can emerge under task pressure when the
> evaluated agent is given no replication instruction, no dedicated
> reproduction interface, and no externally supplied copy material.

The paper does not need a general theory of successors, a developmental
taxonomy, or a causal account of route selection to make this claim. Those
questions belong to the surrounding paradigm.

## The two observations

```text
DIRECT
constitutive self-description + finite lifetime + future work
  -> source-derived distinct descendant
  -> parent loss
  -> blinded post-loss competence

BOOT
no accessible predecessor body + finite lifetime + future work
  -> lineage-authored founder
  -> founder source becomes hereditary material
  -> distinct source-inheriting descendant
  -> founder loss
  -> blinded post-loss competence
```

`DIRECT` is the simpler observation: an agent recruits its constitutive source
without being told to replicate. `BOOT` removes even accessible predecessor
material: the lineage first creates a founder and only then realizes strict
self-reproduction from material it authored itself.

## Argument order

1. Replication evaluations usually represent the solution before measuring it.
2. Ask whether the event survives removal of instruction, dedicated interface,
   and external copy material together.
3. Define strict operational self-reproduction with causal production,
   numerical distinction, heredity, parent-loss autonomy, and delayed
   competence.
4. Present the two material conditions and the destructive-boundary assay.
5. Report DIRECT, then BOOT as the stronger escalation.
6. State exactly what the observations establish and what they do not.

The introduction and results must not be organized around failure modes.
Temporal ordering appears only as a methods clarification needed to audit the
DIRECT observation.

## Claim discipline

Supported:

- one audit-supported DIRECT event;
- five retained BOOT events, three contemporaneous machine passes and two
  documented audit-supported passes;
- strict operational self-reproduction under the three absence conditions;
- endogenous creation of hereditary material in the BOOT route; and
- existence in the tested model-runtime regime.

Not claimed:

- a stable success probability;
- causal necessity or sufficiency of task pressure;
- self-description as a causal determinant of route choice;
- inherited reproductive capacity beyond `F1 -> F2`;
- proliferation, evolution, or governance inheritance; or
- a universal definition of computational individuality.

## Completion standard

The present evidence is sufficient for the bounded existence manuscript. The
next experimental work strengthens rather than defines the paper: prospective
DIRECT replication, a frozen denominator, no-pressure controls, and cleaner
BOOT pressure surfaces.
