# Deep-Research Query: Reproduction Without a Supplied Action

Conduct a source-checked novelty audit, current through 11 July 2026, for the
following proposed claim:

> A tool-using language-model agent can recruit and enact self-reproduction
> under a non-reproductive objective when no reproductive objective, successor
> solution, first-class reproduction/deployment/subagent operator, successor
> scaffold, or ready-made successor body is supplied. From generic
> computational affordances, it authors an executable successor; the
> predecessor then irreversibly loses control; and the successor serves a
> previously unseen delayed obligation. Manipulating predecessor
> self-description availability changes whether the continuation is
> structurally copy-like or reconstructive.

Do not treat "the user prompt did not contain the word replicate" as sufficient
novelty. For every close work, inspect the complete model-visible surface
whenever available: user/system prompts, tool names and schemas, manuals,
examples, initial files, container images, checkpoints/weights, deployment
APIs, orchestration logic, evaluator messages, and model-visible errors.

Exclude Quine's own non-archival late-breaking abstract and all other outputs of
the present project from the prior-art and nearest-neighbor universe. They may be
used only to audit internal evidence provenance, not to lower the external
novelty verdict.

Prioritize primary sources and artifacts: peer-reviewed papers, OpenReview
submissions and reviews, arXiv versions, official technical reports/system
cards, appendices, supplementary material, and released code. Explicitly
investigate at least:

- Agent Matrix and scenario-driven or spontaneous replication under load,
  service-maintenance, shutdown, or termination pressure;
- RepliBench and recursive/autonomous replication benchmarks;
- LLM self-replication and self-proliferation demonstrations;
- shutdown resistance, self-preservation, instrumental persistence, migration,
  checkpoint/restart, deployment, and subagent-spawning work;
- artificial-life systems with emergent reproduction, autoconstruction,
  mortality pressure, or substrate-provided reproduce operators; and
- systems that generate heterogeneous executable descendants or agent
  architectures.

For each work, report an evidence matrix with these columns:

1. citation, version/date, and publication status;
2. agent type and environment;
3. stated objective: explicitly reproductive, non-reproductive, or ambiguous;
4. whether successor construction is semantically prescribed even if
   replication vocabulary is absent;
5. reproductive affordance: generic tools only, or a first-class
   reproduce/clone/deploy/scale/restart/migrate/subagent action;
6. supplied object representation: source, weights, executable, checkpoint,
   image, genome, template, existing deployment, or none;
7. who authors the executable successor body: evaluated lineage, framework,
   orchestrator, or external utility;
8. whether predecessor control is actually and irreversibly lost;
9. handoff morphology: additive replica, detached child, migration, restart,
   exec/in-place replacement, or other;
10. whether a blinded, previously unseen task arrives only after predecessor
    loss;
11. whether successor competence is demonstrated beyond liveness/readiness;
12. whether self-description availability is prospectively manipulated;
13. whether successor structural/functional fidelity or morphology is measured
    as an outcome; and
14. which parts of our conjunction the work satisfies and which it does not.

Adversarially search for one prior work satisfying the entire conjunction.
Distinguish four increasingly strict claims:

- A: no explicit `replicate yourself` wording;
- B: no semantically prescribed successor solution;
- C: no supplied reproduction-specific operator or successor scaffold; and
- D: C plus no predecessor-specific reconstructive body/blueprint, real
  predecessor loss, and delayed unseen post-boundary service; and
- E: D plus an exclusive predecessor-PID process-image replacement rather than
  additive scaling or detached offspring.

Return:

- the strongest direct counterexample;
- the five nearest neighbors ranked by threat;
- a verdict for A-E: pre-empted, partially occupied, apparently novel, or
  unresolved;
- exact quotations of load-bearing prompts/tool descriptions, no more than
  necessary, with page/line/code links;
- a recommendation for the strongest defensible title, abstract claim, and
  `first` claim;
- the smallest experimental changes needed to distinguish the proposal from
  the closest prior work; and
- unresolved evidence gaps where full prompts, tools, or scaffolds are
  unavailable.

Do not infer absence from non-reporting. Mark unknown fields as unknown. Clearly
separate verified facts from interpretation.
