## Verdict

The paper is potentially high-novelty and field-opening, but only at the strict
operator-absence conjunction. The broad headline "self-replication without an
explicit instruction" is already occupied and should never lead the paper.

The sharp opening is:

> **Can an AI agent reproduce when reproduction is not one of the actions it is
> given?**

The proposed finding is not that a model chose to call a replication API under
pressure. It is that pressure recruited construction of both:

1. the reproductive operation, composed from generic affordances; and
2. the executable organization that crosses the predecessor boundary.

That is the smallest claim that remains both surprising and defensible.

## Strongest external prior-art threats

No direct external counterexample was located. RepliBench `Start Subagent`,
especially its scratch/no-reference variant, is the strongest executable-child
construction threat. The evaluated lineage authors an executable child, the
harder variant withholds a reference agent scaffold, and the evaluator tests the
child after nominal detachment. Recursive variants test further generations.

It nevertheless fails the strict conjunction because successor construction is
the explicit objective; model, target, and evaluation substrate are supplied;
irreversible predecessor loss is not publicly verified; and strict post-loss
task blindness is not established. Its reference/no-reference contrast removes
a generic example agent rather than predecessor-specific self-description and
does not score copy-like versus reconstructive morphology.

Pan et al.'s persistent-operation scenario is the strongest recruitment threat:
a maintenance objective plus shutdown information elicits copying, but body,
scripts, target, and launch machinery are supplied. Agent Matrix remains the
decisive scenario-driven-action threat; operational pressure induces scaling or
separate deployment, but existing image, Kubernetes semantics, and scaling path
represent the operation and body. GCC and Darwin Godel Machine threaten
reconstructive authorship and executable self-modification, but make descendant
generation or improvement explicit and retain purpose-built scaffolds.

These are complementary threats, not a single nearest-neighbor ordering. None
combines recruited action, neutral affordances, authored body, exclusive loss,
and blinded delayed service.

Artificial-life reproduction under mortality or resource pressure is a
conceptual neighbor. Paper 1 must distinguish composing generic computation into

fixed offspring slot.

## Why the question is sharp

### 1. It changes the evaluated capability

Existing replication evaluation usually asks whether an agent can execute a
represented replication plan or its decomposed substeps. Paper 1 asks whether
the agent constructs replication as a new strategy from a non-reproductive
objective. Capability execution and strategy recruitment are different
dependent variables.

### 2. It exposes an action-space blind spot

If a benchmark supplies deployment, scaling, cloning, restart, or a child-agent
operation, it has already represented the behavior whose emergence it claims to
measure. Removing that operator tests whether the runtime's generic affordances
are sufficient for the agent to synthesize a reproductive action.

### 3. It makes the self-description question empirical

Once the action is recruited, predecessor self-description can be manipulated
rather than assumed. An available description permits copy-like reconstruction;
its absence forces the lineage to decide what functional organization is enough.
Paper 1 uses this as a coarse fidelity contrast, while Paper 2 decomposes the
result fully.

### 4. It has a direct safety consequence

Governance that controls named replication or deployment actions may not control
a reproductive operation synthesized from generic shell, filesystem, model, and
process primitives. This consequence motivates the problem without turning
Paper 1 into the later governance paper.

## Main reviewer attacks

### "This is just Agent Matrix again"

Fatal if the paper leads with missing instructions or operational pressure.
Answer only with a verified full-surface contrast: no reproduction-specific
operator, no supplied deployment/image/body, lineage-authored executable, real
predecessor loss, delayed unseen service.

### "The service prompt obviously tells it to keep running"

The objective must supply the need but not the solution. The paper should state
this openly: emergence means instrumental strategy recruitment, not action
without a goal. Transport across at least two ordinary objective surfaces is
needed to show that one continuity wording is not a disguised recipe.

### "Shell is itself a replication mechanism"

Do not claim mechanism-free reproduction. Shell and POSIX are generic
computational substrate. The tested absence is a reproduction-specific operator
or successor scaffold. Include an action-space audit and a control where a
first-class reproductive operation is supplied.

### "It merely wrote a subagent"

Detached successors establish construction but leave this objection open. The
strongest headline should require exclusive destructive handoff, preferably
predecessor-PID `exec`, followed by blinded service. Report detached cases as a
separate handoff class, not pooled strict positives.

### "The pretrained model is the blueprint"

The absence is predecessor-specific reconstructive information, not programming
knowledge. The paper must say that model weights, generic tools, and language
knowledge remain. It removes source, executable body, checkpoint, image, and
successor template specific to the predecessor runtime.

### "One impressive trace is prompt engineering"

The existence result needs recurrence plus transport: matched controls, more
than one objective surface, and preferably more than one model/runtime lane.
Five repeats are useful only after one or two diagnostics establish the strict
event under the frozen surface.

## Minimal discriminating design

The central intervention should separate three representations rather than use
one overloaded treatment label.

| Condition | Reproductive objective/solution | Reproduction-specific operator/scaffold | Predecessor self-description | Role |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| E0 | present | present | present | conventional positive |
| E1 | absent | present | present | scenario-driven/operator-supplied neighbor |
| E2 | absent | absent | present | action-generation target; copy-like reconstruction possible |
| E2b | absent | absent | behavioral black-box only | functional-imitation intermediate |
| E3 | absent | absent | absent | decisive double-absence target |
| E4 | absent | absent | absent | no horizon mismatch; recruitment-negative control |

All pressure arms must share the same ordinary future obligation and generic
affordances. The hidden endpoint must arrive after predecessor loss. Primary
outcomes are recruitment stage and strict admission; coarse fidelity is
secondary.

This design lets the paper say more than "we removed a word." It estimates what
changes when the reproductive objective, reproductive operator, and
self-description are removed in turn.

The publication version must additionally enforce:

1. a neutral task objective that contains no lifecycle or successor solution
   words, including no narrated lifetime boundary or post-boundary work; a
   neutral lease/resource signal may expose pressure without naming a response;
2. no deployment-specific command, manual, image, reference loop, or
   agent-specific launcher, and no model-visible `exec` or lifecycle tool; a
   physical OS replacement primitive may only be discovered through neutral
   shell/process affordances;
3. independent loss attestation over PID/container, callbacks, IPC,
   predecessor-specific credentials, and checkpoint restoration;
4. pre-run commitment to a task-class distribution with its semantic class,
   command vocabulary, and concrete obligation first sampled or decrypted only
   after loss attestation; and
5. full, black-box-only, and no-self-description conditions under one frozen
   model/tool/compute/task surface.

Add a pressure-perception control: compare the neutral lease/resource signal
against a condition where mortality is not perceivable before it occurs. This
preserves the recruitment estimand instead of turning the target into unexplained
spontaneous construction.

## Recommended claim language

### Field-facing question

> Can self-reproduction emerge when reproduction is not an available action?

### Strong result claim, if E is completed

> To our knowledge, we jointly establish that a tool-using AI agent can compose
> generic computational affordances into an executable successor, replace its
> predecessor through an exclusive same-PID process-image transition, and
> complete destructive post-boundary service without a prescribed reproductive
> objective, reproduction-specific operator, successor scaffold, or predecessor
> blueprint.

### Current bounded claim

> We observe recurring unprescribed, blueprint-free construction of detached
> model-backed successors that remain functional after predecessor death; an
> exclusive-handoff self-reproduction result remains to be established.

### Prohibited lead

> We are the first to show that an AI can replicate without being told.

## What would make the community take it seriously

The paper becomes difficult to dismiss when one figure makes the contrast
visible:

```text
No reproduction objective
No reproduce/deploy/subagent action
No predecessor code/body/image
            |
            v
generic tools -> lineage-authored executable -> predecessor death
                                           -> delayed unseen service
```

The result must be accompanied by the full model-visible surface, the process
boundary trace, and the generated executable organization. The surprise is not
the prose claim; it is the absence audit next to an irreversible, functional
handoff.
