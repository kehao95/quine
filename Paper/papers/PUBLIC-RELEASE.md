# Public paper dossiers

This is a research-workbench snapshot from private source `f4d1b6414`.
Planning, drafting, rejected, and published dossiers retain their recorded
status. Published reproduction packages remain under `public/`.

Only `Paper/papers/` is included from the source `Paper/` directory.
Links to other Paper paths, theory, experiments, and newer development records
may be unavailable in this public snapshot. These links are retained as
provenance, not promises that all referenced evidence can be downloaded.

Twenty files replace the original machine home prefixes with `/REDACTED_HOME`.
Other content, including historical source digests, is retained; a digest in a
redacted receipt still identifies its original input, not the edited receipt.
Literature URLs and experimental examples of incorrect paths are unchanged.

The copied ALIFE review record `minimal-perceptual-prerequisites/reviews/alife-2026-fp283s1.md`
and the two Escher JPEGs under `the-autopoietic-repository/images/` are omitted
from this source snapshot. References to omitted material may be unresolved.

Vendored acmart class/bibliography files and totpages style files in the two
`support/acmart/` directories are omitted. Historical TeX sources use acmart
v2.16 and totpages from an external TeX installation. Retained ALIFE styles keep
their upstream notices. This release does not establish that every historical
manuscript builds on a clean machine or that every draft claim is validated.
