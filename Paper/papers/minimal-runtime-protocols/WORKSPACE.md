## Directory Role

This workspace keeps the manuscript skeleton, paper-local literature surface,
and evidence backlog for a systems-facing empirical paper on protocol-layer
emergence over minimal runtime transport.

Claim-boundary, evidence-backbone, and next-step decisions live in the dossier,
not only here.
