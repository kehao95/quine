   `full-paper-directions.md`.

   expert probability distributions" — an unrelated statistics/forecasting

   object about the repository's own reflexive self-model, in the
   `reflexive-repository` family. Not the same claim as this dossier's
   frozen-agent/environment claim, but close enough in name to trip up a
   reader skimming both.
