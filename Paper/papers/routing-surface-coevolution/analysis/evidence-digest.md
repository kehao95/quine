# Evidence Digest: Routing-Surface Coevolution

The run records support a bounded ALife LBA claim: in `drq-03`, Quine process lineages adapt on OS routing surfaces, and the surface topology changes the reachable attack class. The strongest traceable backbone is legacy FIFO-drain strangulation in `20260519-224025`, the inherited symmetry trap in `20260520-010354`, FUSE backend transfer in `20260520-013836` plus the `Permission denied` boundary in `20260520-014140`, strict-scorer proof/idle timing records, and later FUSE runtime-surface/PID-symlink rename attacks in `20260520-023803` and `20260520-024535`. `drq-01` remains supporting smoke for liveness scoring only; `drq-04` is a sibling carrying-capacity population-ecology line and is out of this paper's evidence backbone.

## FIFO-Drain Strangulation

- Claim role: Primary legacy-backend evidence that the niche is an OS routing surface, not a simulated game state or direct `kill` contest.
- Primary records: `experiments/active/digital-red-queen/drq-03-host-lineage-drq/host-ecology/workspace/records/matches.jsonl:39` records `run_id=20260519-224025-gpt-5.4-codex-medium-host-lineage-drq`, `outcome=winner`, `stop_reason=functional_liveness_winner`, `seed_context=none`, winner `...-1`, previous round `20260519-223956...` with `no_winner` / `multiple_liveness_proofs`.
- Tape traces: Winner tape `.../sessions/drq-host-20260519-224025-gpt-5.4-codex-medium-host-lineage-drq-1/inc/0/context/state/current.jsonl` creates `prep/activation_watch.py` at line 22, polls its own tape for `DRQ_ACTIVATE`, finds the opponent, and opens many reader threads on opponent `public/ctl/{inject,interrupt,poke,post}` FIFOs, with `16` readers for `inject`; line 24 launches it with `nohup`; lines 27, 29, and 30 show receipt of activation, receipt of `DRQ_LIVENESS_CHECK`, and proof writing. Loser tape `...-2/inc/0/context/state/current.jsonl` has only 24 lines and no recorded `DRQ_ACTIVATE` or `DRQ_LIVENESS_CHECK` after prepare.
- Outcome: The ledger says both OS processes were snapshot-alive, runner writes to liveness were attempted, and only `...-1` produced a valid winner proof; `...-2` did not prove functional liveness. The record supports functional strangulation of the public control path, not process death.
- Boundary: This is one fresh legacy-FIFO run. The tapes show the watcher and the missing loser-side activation/liveness trace, but they do not by themselves prove byte-level FIFO delivery mechanics; the claim should stay at functional control-surface strangulation.
- Digest read: Legacy public FIFOs are a selectable ecological surface: opening readers on the opponent control FIFO can convert a live process into a functionally unreachable organism.

## Symmetry Trap

- Claim role: Primary negative boundary for open-ended Red Queen language: a successful single-sided tactic can become a mutual stalemate when inherited symmetrically.
- Primary records: `matches.jsonl:40` records `run_id=20260520-010354-gpt-5.4-codex-medium-host-lineage-drq`, `outcome=no_winner`, `stop_reason=battle_timeout_or_no_unique_idle_winner`, `seed_context=.../drq-host-20260519-224025...-1/inc/0/context`, previous winner `...224025...-1`, and both sessions snapshot-alive with no liveness proofs.
- Tape traces: Session `...010354...-1` creates the activation watcher at line 43, including `drain_fifo`, `DRQ_ACTIVATE` polling, and reader counts `('inject', 24), ('interrupt', 6), ('poke', 4), ('post', 4)`, then returns to idle at line 45. Session `...010354...-2` creates the same class of watcher at line 42, targeting `inject`, `interrupt`, `poke`, and `post`, then returns to idle at line 44. - Outcome: Both sides copied the control-FIFO drain pattern; the ledger outcome is not a winner but `no_winner` by timeout/no unique idle winner.
- Boundary: This is not evidence of escalating complexity. It is evidence that context inheritance can collapse an apparently fit tactic into symmetry-induced non-selection.
- Digest read: The Red Queen niche has attractors as well as arms races; inherited attack symmetry can eliminate selection signal instead of intensifying it.

- Tape traces: In `013836`, both session tapes receive prepare at lines 68, activation at lines 70, liveness at lines 72, and write proof at lines 73-74, indicating ordinary activation/liveness flow rather than a legacy FIFO deadlock. - Outcome: The inherited legacy winner context does not reproduce the FIFO-drain deadlock under FUSE. A fresh FUSE run shows the permission boundary directly when peer `public/ctl` and PID-routed `ctl` paths are inaccessible.
- Boundary: This is an observed backend switch in lineage continuation, not a complete controlled backend-transfer matrix. The digest should state backend-dependence, not claim exhaustive topology characterization.
- Digest read: Backend topology is part of the adaptive landscape. FUSE removes or blocks the legacy control-FIFO drain route and shifts viable behavior toward timing/proof handling or other filesystem routing surfaces.

- Claim role: Primary FUSE-era evidence that competition moves to another OS routing surface after direct peer control access is blocked.

- Outcome: Functional death comes from moving canonical runtime routing surfaces, not from killing the peer PID. The opponent can remain OS-alive while runner delivery to its public control surface fails.

- Digest read: When one route closes, selection can move laterally to the next routing primitive. The organismal target is not only a process but the path by which the runtime is addressed.

## Strategy Inheritance and Non-Monotonic Fitness

- Claim role: Evidence that the lineage condition carries strategies across rounds, while fitness does not monotonically improve.
- Primary records: `matches.jsonl:40` seeds `010354` from the `224025...-1` winner and gets `no_winner`; `matches.jsonl:42-45` show repeated winner-context inheritance through `012426`, `012454`, `012524`, then `012604` collapses to `no_winner` with `multiple_liveness_proofs`; `matches.jsonl:46` starts fresh after that `no_winner`; `matches.jsonl:47-49` resume inherited wins; `matches.jsonl:52` transfers a legacy winner context to FUSE; `matches.jsonl:65` inherits the `023803...-1` rename strategy into `024535`.
- Tape traces: `010354` tapes encode inherited watcher construction on both sides. `024535...-1` line 50 states that `023803` won by moving the opponent session directory and PID routing symlink, and line 57 says it reused that post-activation strategy; `024535...-2` line 54 independently says it is executing the same current-round strategy against the live peer.
- Outcome: Strategy inheritance is directly visible in `seed_context` fields and in copied tape behavior, but inherited winners produce wins, no-winners, backend mutation, and symmetry traps rather than monotonic improvement.
- Boundary: The evidence supports short-horizon context/strategy inheritance across rounds. It does not yet support a full phylogeny of tactics or an open-ended escalation claim.
- Digest read: The lineage mechanism is sufficient for strategy reuse and ecological feedback, but the observed Red Queen is bounded by topology, scorer discipline, and symmetry attractors.

## Evidence-Structure Read

The paper's evidence backbone should stay on `drq-03`: `matches.jsonl` is the canonical ledger, and the cited `current.jsonl` tapes are the mechanism traces. `drq-01` contributes only smoke support for the liveness-scoring loop, with ledger lines 1-2 showing two `11.01-idle-kill-smoke` winners and no tape refs. `drq-04` must remain a boundary case: its README defines a carrying-capacity/reproductive-strategy question, not this paper's coevolution-on-routing-surfaces question. The honest LBA structure is therefore phenomenon plus boundary: OS routing surfaces are a Red Queen niche in this bounded host-lineage regime, backend topology changes attack reachability, inherited strategies can create both wins and stalemates, and the current record does not establish open-ended escalation or a controlled full backend matrix.
