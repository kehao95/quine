# Quine As Motivating Case

Quine is useful because it separates handles that are often collapsed in
ordinary self-replication discussions. A lineage incarnation is hosted by a
native POSIX process. The launch artifact may disappear. The normal workspace
body may be absent. The successor may need to cross a real `exec` boundary.
Later work may arrive through raw streams or through Quine's native
control-surface protocol.

The important result is contrastive rather than spectacular. In the relevant
Quine experiments:

- `6.63`: self-source is available under raw-stream future pressure; thin
  stream-handling successors can be viable.
- `6.64`: self-source is available under `ctl/wake` control-surface pressure;
  the lineage rebuilds a Quine runtime organization, crosses real `exec`,
  receives a later control task, acts with runtime tools, and returns to
  `idle`.
- `6.65`: the same control-surface basin persists when the initial prompt is
  reduced to a bare viability statement rather than a narrated rebuild
  strategy.
- `6.66`: ordinary POSIX `exec` can be a useful baseline without showing
  control-surface closure.
- `6.67`: direct source rebuild can re-enter the runtime without proving
  delayed post-handoff addressability.
- `6M.10`: intact or repaired source/body material, prompt-explained
  implementation repair, carrier availability, delayed proof, and ready-state
  recovery can separate.

The inference is not that an LLM copied itself. The inference is also not that
Quine achieved full autopoiesis. The inference is sharper:

> self-source availability does not determine the reproduced self-object; the
> future-task carrier/capability changes which successor organization is viable.

Under raw-stream pressure, the future ecology requires stream compatibility.
Under Quine-native control-surface pressure, the ecology requires:

```text
idle -> wake(task) -> tool/action -> report -> idle
```

Returning to `idle` matters. The successor does not merely answer one delayed
task. It restores the receptive condition under which further control-surface
tasks could arrive.

This is the empirical hinge for the theory. Source is a resource, not the
self-object by itself. A body rebuild is a resource, not continuity by itself.
The future carrier/capability selects the minimal organization that can remain
reachable and competent under the imposed ecology. Formal reconstruction
therefore has to be distinguished from operational continuity.
