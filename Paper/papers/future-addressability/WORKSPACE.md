## Directory Role

Use this directory for the paper-sized theory object:

> computational individuality is indexed by future addressability.

This is not the active LBA surface and not the evidence-heavy Quine boundary
paper. It is the place to develop the general theory that the Quine
control-surface result revealed.

## Current Work Mode

Current default:

- preserve the anchor thesis and vocabulary
- keep Quine as the motivating case, not the whole paper
- compare across computational media where continuity is carrier-mediated
- avoid widening into a full theory of life, agency, or open-ended evolution

## Paper Boundary

This paper asks:

- when is a computational successor the same continuing individual?
- how does the future-task carrier define the relevant self-object?
- how do different media make different continuity objects salient?
- what does post-handoff addressability add beyond source identity, process
  identity, memory continuity, endpoint naming, or artifact copying?

It should not become:

- another telling of the `p6` evidence ladder
- a general AI self-replication benchmark survey
- a full autopoiesis proof
- a systems implementation paper about rebuilding Quine
