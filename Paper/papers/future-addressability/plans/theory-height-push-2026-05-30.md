# Theory Height Push: Future Addressability

This note records the next conceptual move for the future-addressability paper.
It is paper-local planning material, not manuscript prose.

## Purpose

Raise the paper from a Quine continuity criterion to a general theory candidate
for computational individuality.

The current dossier already has the right phrase:

```text
computational individuality is indexed by future addressability
```

The theory-height move is to treat the future as an active selector of the
self-object. A boundary event creates multiple possible identity handles:
source, binary body, process, state, endpoint, mailbox, capability, and
observable behavior. The future-task carrier determines which organization
must be preserved or reconstructed for the lineage to remain obligation-bearing.

## Stronger Framing

Do not frame the paper as only:

```text
which successor can still be reached?
```

Frame it as:

```text
when identity handles diverge, future obligations select the computational
organization that counts as the continuing individual.
```

This is what gives the theory more height than a Quine-specific handoff result.
It turns `future addressability` into a selection criterion over candidate
self-objects.

## Three-Level Contribution

1. **Diagnostic:** source, body, process, state, endpoint, mailbox, and behavior
   are identity handles. None is a sufficient continuity criterion once a real
   handoff boundary separates them.
2. **Criterion:** in obligation-bearing computational lineages, continuity is
   carrier-indexed: `obligation + carrier + competent successor organization +
   receptive-state recovery`.
3. **Experimental program:** perturb the future carrier and observe which
   successor organization becomes viable. This connects future addressability
   to successor morphology instead of leaving it as abstract vocabulary.

## Core Theoretical Contrast

Most neighboring theories are retrospective or structural:

- source and quine theories ask what description is copied
- checkpoint and migration ask what state survives
- actors and services ask what address or role remains routable
- process calculi ask what behavior is observationally equivalent
- capabilities ask what authority-bearing references remain valid
- autopoiesis asks what organization produces and maintains itself

Future addressability asks a prospective question:

```text
what future obligation can still bind this lineage, through which carrier, and
what organization must exist for that obligation to be acted on after boundary B?
```

That is the paper's theory edge.

## Connection To Successor Morphology

Future addressability and computational morphogenesis should be coupled, not
merged.

Future addressability owns the individuation criterion:

```text
which successor organization counts as the continuing individual?
```

Computational morphogenesis owns the form-space question:

```text
what successor organizations appear under different resource, carrier, and
viability-depth pressures?
```

The bridge claim is:

```text
future carriers select individuality criteria; resource and viability pressures
shape the morphology that can satisfy those criteria.
```

This gives Quine a stronger theoretical stack:

- individuation: future addressability
- morphology: successor morphospace
- mechanism: runtime replacement / handoff physics
- evidence: P6/P6F/P6M contrasts
