## Query Selection Rationale

These three queries intentionally avoid repeating existing local deep-research
coverage:

- `autopoiesis-renewal` already covers autoconstructive successor morphology,
  reproduction, lifecycle bottlenecks, and ARR/source-check neighbors.
- `open-ended-ecology` already covers stigmergy, OEE criteria, trace causality,
  and affordance-rich null ecologies.
- `minimal-perceptual-prerequisites` already has a social-behavior literature
  review for stigmergy and coordination under existential unawareness.

The workshop paper needs a different citation surface: a workshop synthesis that connects
Quine's process-native substrate to agentic / generative AI ecologies.
