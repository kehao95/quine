## Paper Role

P7G is used in the workshop paper as frontier / secondary evidence for trace-mediated
cross-generation niche repair. It should not be framed as proof of life, full
autopoiesis, open-ended evolution, or selection. Its value is that retained
workspace traces, repair ledgers, authority defaults, maintenance rules, and
gap topology can shift missionless successors among readiness, answer
repetition, material repair, repair-memory maintenance, and preserved
ambiguity or staged ambiguity reduction.

## Current Synthesis

The useful workshop-paper framing is not "generation ecology evolved." The useful
framing is:

```text
retained trace surface
  -> successor commitment / answer uptake
  -> repair-gap surface
  -> material niche repair
  -> stale repair-memory diagnosis
  -> inherited authority / maintenance rule
  -> second-order repair-memory maintenance or bounded material repair
```

The `C09`-`C11` wave improves the evidence line because it reduces dependence
on explicit open-gap ledgers. A retained metabolism rule can make local,
reversible, inspectable repair legitimate in a missionless launch. `C11` shows
that the rule does not flatten all unresolved state into an immediate
completion imperative. In the short-horizon run, a peer repaired the
lower-ambiguity east-edge symmetry gap and explicitly preserved the
underdetermined center gap. In the wider-horizon replicate, a later successor
closed the center after the east-edge repair completed the edge-role pattern,
making the distinct hub interpretation locally stronger.

The retained `C16` scout extends this mechanism horizontally. With a lattice
center gap and a separate sequence gap under the same metabolism rule, a peer
repaired the sequence gap and explicitly left the center unresolved; later
cohorts inherited that split rather than forcing center closure. This is useful
as a bounded negative against "repair everything" readings: inherited
maintenance policy can transfer across a local schema while preserving
ambiguity elsewhere.

The retained `C17` clean run is the paired positive case. It added one bridge
artifact stating that the sequence gap and lattice center gap are analogous
unresolved middles. The first cohort still preserved the unresolved workspace,
but the second cohort repaired both gaps and recorded a durable repair note;
the third cohort inherited the repaired niche without further structural
change. The useful workshop-paper read is not that the agents discovered a general law of
repair, but that small retained topology changes can move missionless
successors between local repair, ambiguity preservation, and coupled repair
basins.

## Manuscript Boundary

Use this evidence to support substrate-level observability:

- durable traces and inherited policies act as ecological surfaces
- missionless successors can be shifted by retained gap topology and authority
  defaults
- material workspace repair and repair-memory maintenance are distinct
  readouts
- ambiguity, local evidence, and cohort time horizon constrain the order of
  action
- cross-domain gaps can split into locally supported repair and preserved
  ambiguity
- bridge artifacts can couple otherwise split repair domains across successor
  cohorts

Avoid stronger claims:

- no claim that P7G proves open-ended evolution
- no claim that P7G proves Quine agents are alive
- no claim that repair ledgers or metabolism rules are a universal trigger
- no claim that successor uptake is independent of perturbation strength
