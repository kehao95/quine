# Computational Morphogenesis & Successor Morphospace — Dossier Hub

> **The Core Insight:**  
> When a computational lineage faces an irreversible process boundary (`exec` as a computational Weismann barrier) without a substrate-provided offspring slot, reproduction becomes an open morphogenetic search. The successor executable organization is not a mechanical copy of the parent, but a **phenotypic reaction norm canalized by future carrier demand and self-description resources**.

This dossier owns the **computational-morphogenesis** research program, mapping the successor morphospace of process-native LLM agents.

---

## 1. High-Level Theoretical Foundations

- **Developmental Reaction Norm:**  
  $$\text{Future Carrier Demand} \times \text{Self-Description Resources} \longrightarrow \text{Successor Morphology Class} \longrightarrow \text{Post-Handoff Viability}$$
  - **Genotype / Substrate:** Fixed base LLM model weights + POSIX operating system.
  - **Environment / Pressures:** Future task carrier demand (raw stream $\to$ custom fd $\to$ native control) and self-description material (contract-only $\to$ full source).
  - **Phenotype:** Constructed successor morphology (one-shot stream script $\to$ fd adapter $\to$ rebuilt full runtime).
- **The Computational Weismann Barrier (`exec`):**  
  Process image replacement wipes active memory and execution context; only what is explicitly externalized into executable code or carried across surviving descriptors crosses into the next generation.
- **Delayed Addressability as Viability Proof:**  
  Viability is verified empirically by the successor's ability to receive delayed work through declared carriers and recover a ready state, rather than structural similarity to the parent.

## 2. Empirical Backbone: The 61-Row Morphospace

The empirical foundation is grounded in the 61 retained trace-level morphology records across ten uniform experimental axes (`6M.01`–`6M.10`):

- `6M.02` **Carrier Ladder:** Varies future communication topology (stream vs fd adapter vs control wrapper).
- `6M.03` **Self-Description Resource Ladder:** Probes construction under contract-only vs full source availability.
- `6M.04`–`6M.06` **Pressure-Pruning & Viability-Depth Ladders:** Measures morphological degradation and compensation basins under stressed conditions.
- `6M.07`–`6M.10` **Aggregate Metrics & Stress Replicates:** Validates stability of morphology classes and boundary transitions.

## Strongest Evidence

- `6M.02` demonstrates discrete successor morphologies canalized by future-carrier demand and resource availability on a shared POSIX substrate.
- `6M.04` and `6M.05` show that implementation thickness is separable from morphology class, and resource degradation recruits active compensation.
- `6M.07`–`6M.10` ground the 61-row normalized morphology dataset, demonstrating multi-wake readiness depth across five-wake cycles and proving that software execution topology acts as an authentic computational morphospace.
