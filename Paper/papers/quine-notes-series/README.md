# Quine Notes

## Current Question

Which narrow systems claims should leave the flagship papers and become short one-primitive / one-claim notes instead?

## Thesis

Some systems claims are better published as short, one-primitive / one-claim notes than as sections inside the flagship papers.

## In Scope

- compact notes on one primitive and one consequence
- self-contained extensions that depend on Quine's core mapping
- publication or refinement of local semantic results without reopening the whole system paper

## Out Of Scope

- replacing the flagship papers
- absorbing broad future architecture into a note series
- experiment-heavy programs that need their own full paper
