## The Paper's Core Claim

Classical evolutionary theory (OEE, cultural evolution) presupposes three structural separations:
1. **Substrate-type**: organism ≠ environment (different substances)
2. **Individual boundary**: discrete, countable units of selection
3. **Weismann barrier**: acquired traits do not inherit

These separations are not universal laws. They are **substrate choices**, enforced by physics in biology/Tierra/Avida.

In the Quine language-based agent substrate, **all three collapse simultaneously** due to a single root cause: **read = dissolution** (lossless absorption of environment into cognition, not translation).

### Consequences

- **Reflexivity is constitutive**: the loop is the resting state, no special mechanism needed
- **Lamarckian inheritance at full strength**: tools, theories, modifications written by one agent are immediately heritable
- **Fuzzy individuals**: the unit of selection is indeterminate; boundaries blur
- **Fitness is endogenous**: no external oracle; selection criteria are language-produced
- **Substrate translation is absent**: no bandwidth limit; environment is not encoded/decoded

### Scholarly Positioning

- **Against OEE universalism**: separations presupposed by Bedau (1997) are substrate-contingent
- **Against Weismann universalism**: the barrier exists in biology/Tierra because of substrate choices, not because it is necessary
- **For cultural evolution**: positions Quine as "cultural evolution with the brain/world barrier removed" — a regime Boyd/Richerson has not modeled (lossless transmission + endogenous selection)
