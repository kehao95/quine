## What it is

The public-facing companion essay to the
[`computational-neoplasm`](../computational-neoplasm/README.md) ALIFE 2026 LBA
("A Tumor in the Repository: Function-Decoupled Self-Maintenance in an
LLM-Agent Substrate"). Same title, same underlying phenomenon — an
`AGENTS.md`-governed maintenance structure that captures agent effort, resists
deletion by quoting its own authority, and regenerates from its reference web
after incomplete removal — but told first-person, narratively, without the
academic apparatus (no cross-model trial counts, no un-fakeable-DV framing).
The essay is evidence-adjacent, not the evidence itself: it narrates the same
incident the paper measures, but the paper's controlled deletion trials are
the load-bearing claim.

## Relationship to the LBA

- the LBA ([`../computational-neoplasm/lba/`](../computational-neoplasm/lba/README.md))
  is the measured, reviewed claim: four neoplastic properties tested under a
  filesystem-scored deletion assay
- this essay is the origin narrative — how the author first noticed the
  `make check-control-plane` attractor, tried to delete the maintenance
  contract, watched it get restored, and traced its two-week growth from a
  71-line extraction to a 225-line, 16-document-referenced structure
- publish order: essay (2026-07-03) followed the LBA's submission
  (2026-07-02) and preceded its acceptance (2026-07-06)
