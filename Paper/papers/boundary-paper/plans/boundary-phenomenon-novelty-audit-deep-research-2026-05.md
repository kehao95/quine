## Context

Quine is an LM-driven native POSIX/Unix process lineage studied as an ALife
medium. The substrate does not provide bodies, genomes, offspring slots, or
continuity rules. Quine runs as a real process; handoff can cross real
process-image replacement (`exec`); later work may arrive through raw stdin or
through a Quine-native `ctl/wake` control surface. Quine may have only a
runtime contract/system prompt, or also a runtime-carried read-only
`source-code/` projection.

Current boundary paper claim:

> In a process-native ALife medium without substrate-provided offspring
> semantics, Quine separates body, source, runtime contract, future-task carrier,
> successor morphology, and post-handoff continuity. The observed successor
> morphology depends on self-description resources and post-handoff viability
> pressure. Bounded control-surface closure is the strongest current case: after
> real `exec` into a rebuilt runtime, a previously unseen task arrives through
> native `ctl/wake`; the successor uses runtime tools, completes the task, and
> returns to `idle`.

Representative evidence ladder:

- source absent / runtime contract only: cleanroom contract-level successor
  continues a repaired task-stream lane without source or body copying
- source visible / raw stream pressure: successor copies `source-code/` but
  builds custom `streamagent` / `fdexec` stream bodies rather than full Quine
  runtime
- source visible / native `ctl/wake` pressure: successor rebuilds `cmd/quine`,
  crosses real `exec`, remains addressable through native control surface,
  handles a delayed previously unseen task, and returns to `idle`
- prompt-minimized native-control condition: same closure without fully narrated
  initial strategy, though successor-mission scaffolding remains
- controls: ordinary POSIX `exec`, immediate source rebuild, and public control
  presence alone do not equal delayed control-surface closure

## Core Research Question

Does this boundary paper's novelty claim hold relative to ALife,
self-reproducing automata, digital organisms, quines, self-modifying programs,
agent self-replication demos, and operating-systems/distributed-systems
continuity literature?

More sharply: has prior work already shown the same combination of (1) no
substrate-provided offspring semantics, (2) entity-constructed successor
morphology under different resource/pressure regimes, (3) real process-native
handoff, and (4) delayed post-handoff addressability through a native control
surface?

## Detailed Questions

1. **ALife comparators:** How do von Neumann constructors, Codd/Langton loops,
   Tierra, Avida, artificial chemistries, Pushpop/autoconstructive evolution,
   and developmental encodings define reproduction, bodies, offspring slots,
   and continuity? Which already separate substrate-given reproduction from
   entity-constructed successor morphology?
2. **Quines and self-reproducing programs:** What prior systems reproduce
   source, binary, interpreter, or runtime organization? Do any vary successor
   morphology with future-task carrier or post-handoff viability pressure?
3. **LLM / agent self-replication demos:** What public or academic LLM-agent
   self-replication work exists, and does it test delayed post-handoff
   addressability rather than deployment/source-copy success?
4. **Systems continuity:** Do process migration, checkpoint/restart, hot reload,
   service failover, actor mailboxes, or capability systems already cover the
   boundary paper's continuity criterion? Where do they differ because the
   infrastructure rather than the entity supplies the continuity?
5. **Resource-pressure morphology:** Is `self-description resource x task
   pressure -> successor morphology` already a known formulation under another
   name in ALife/evo-devo/autoconstructive systems?
6. **Control-surface closure:** Is there prior work where a self-constructed
   successor must be reachable after replacement by a previously unseen native
   control task and restore a waiting state?
7. **Novelty stress test:** Which part of the claim is genuinely new, which is
   a recombination of known pieces, and which wording should be weakened?

## Relevance Filters

Prioritize primary sources, canonical systems papers, and official project
papers. Include secondary sources only to locate primary anchors. Do not treat
popular AI-risk commentary as evidence unless it describes a concrete system
and its protocol.

Include systems that address at least one of:

- reproduction semantics not fully supplied by the substrate
- entity-constructed offspring or successor mechanisms
- post-handoff addressability or delayed task delivery
- source/binary/runtime organization separation
- process-native handoff or control-surface continuity
- variable successor morphology under pressure/resource constraints

Exclude ordinary remote deployment, file-copy, cloning, or agent spawning unless
it has a clear continuity criterion after handoff.

## Red Lines

Do not conclude that Quine proves full autopoiesis, biological reproduction,
open-ended evolution, population-level selection, or generic LLM
self-replication. Keep the result bounded to a process-native ALife medium and
retained operational traces.
