## Context

The current LBA is titled `Self-Reproduction Without Offspring Semantics:
Control-Surface Closure in Quine`. It argues that Quine operationally separates
self-description resource, future-task carrier pressure, successor organization,
and lineage continuity. The strongest case is bounded control-surface closure:
a rebuilt successor crosses real `exec`, receives a delayed previously unseen
native `ctl/wake` task, acts with runtime tools, and returns to `idle`.

The LBA is intentionally narrow. It should not become a literature survey or
claim full autopoiesis, generic LLM self-replication, population-level
selection, or biological morphogenesis.

## Core Research Question

Are the current LBA citations and boundary statements sufficient and safe for an
ALife late-breaking abstract, and which minimal primary-source citations should
be added or corrected?
