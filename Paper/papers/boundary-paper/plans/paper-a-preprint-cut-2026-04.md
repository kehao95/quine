# Paper A Preprint Cut (2026-04)

This note defines the intended `arXiv` cut for
[`../README.md`](../README.md).

It is a planning note, not manuscript prose.

The preprint is the **first public form of Paper A**, not a separate `P0`.

## Role

`A-preprint` should:

- establish the object publicly
- define the paper's decomposition and non-claims
- show one real empirical backbone strong enough to justify the decomposition
- create narrative priority without exhausting the full archival expression

`A-preprint` should not:

- become a freestanding position paper
- absorb the whole broader family theory
- try to pre-solve Paper B's framework task

## Minimum Must-Keep Content

The preprint should keep:

- the core question: what is reproduced, and when does successor production
  fail to become lineage continuity?
- the two-axis decomposition in a compact but explicit form, with the five
  self-production objects presented as vocabulary organized by those axes
- the two-act structure: `what is reproduced?` and `does it continue?`
- the stable empirical backbone:
  - `6.1`
  - `6.2`
  - `6.3`
- one compact frontier line showing bounded movement beyond self-copy:
  - `6.26` as the safest current positive
- one explicit continuity boundary:
  - `6.53` as the named carrier boundary
  - `6.58` as the repair comparator
  - `6.59` as a cleanroom autonomous-closure result on the repaired lane
- the current safest title-level center:
  - bounded organizational closure
  - precarious self-maintenance
  - reproduced scaffolded interpretive organization
  - handoff-conditioned continuity
- explicit non-claims:
  - not full autopoiesis
  - not pure endogenous teleology
  - not generic AI self-replication

## Keep Out Or Hold Back

The preprint should hold back:

- full venue-specific optimization
- extended philosophy-of-identity expansion
- every adjacent literature lane at maximal breadth
- unqualified title-level translation; if `6.59` enters, present it as
  repaired-lane autonomous closure while keeping full autopoiesis as a
  non-claim
- large Paper-B-style regime and protocol expansion
- any attempt to sound like a broad benchmark or risk survey

## Title Posture

Current default:

- `Bounded Organizational Closure in a Mortal Computational Lineage`

Use the stronger title:

- `Toward Bounded Cognitive Autopoiesis in a Mortal Computational Lineage`

only if the preprint itself immediately makes the ceiling posture explicit and
the frontier evidence has genuinely moved upward enough to support that
translation.

## Minimum Honest Preprint Shape

The preprint should read as:

- a complete boundary paper
- with a smaller but still real evidence backbone
- and a deliberately incomplete archival horizon

It should be strong enough to stand alone as a citable public reference.
It should still leave the archival version room for:

- tighter evidence freezing
- fuller citation layer
- cleaner figures and tables
- more mature frontier placement
- venue-specific framing refinement

## Release Test

`A-preprint` is ready only if:

- the paper can state its object in one sentence without leaning on chat-local
  explanation
- the five objects and the central continuity distinction are explicit
- the empirical backbone is strong enough that the paper does not read as
  conceptual speculation
- the strongest claims still remain below full autopoiesis
- the preprint does not force the archival version to be a trivial rewrap
