# Paper A Venue Fit Memo (2026-04)

This note records the current venue posture for
[`../README.md`](../README.md).

It is a planning memo, not manuscript prose.

Use it when the question is:

- where Paper A is most likely to be correctly understood
- where Paper A has the largest realistic upside
- how much reframing tax each venue family imposes

Do not use it as a stealth owner for the whole paper family. Paper B and later
framework or systems lanes need separate venue logic.

## Current Object

Paper A is currently strongest as:

- an A-Life and cognitive-autonomy hybrid
- a frontier phenomenon / boundary paper
- about bounded organizational closure, precarious self-maintenance,
  reproduced scaffolded interpretive organization, and handoff-conditioned
  continuity in a mortal computational lineage

Its core claim is not generic `AI self-replication`.

Its sharpest contribution is that:

> capable successor production and actual lineage continuity under real handoff
> are different achievements, and the Quine lineage substrate makes that
> separation empirically visible.

## Venue Identity Judgment

The paper's native identity is not yet:

- a broad-AI benchmark paper
- a pure philosophy paper
- a systems-runtime paper

Its native identity is:

- a decomposition-and-positioning paper
- anchored in one implemented lineage substrate
- with strong A-Life relevance and a real cognitive-autonomy translation layer

That creates three realistic venue families.

### 1. Native-Fit Family

Best matches when the paper remains closest to its current self:

- `Artificial Life`
- `ALIFE`
- `Adaptive Behavior`

What these venues naturally absorb:

- self-production decomposition
- bounded closure language
- renewal and viability pressure
- lineage and continuity under bounded conditions

Main advantage:

- reviewers are most likely to understand why the object is unusual without
  forcing the paper to impersonate a benchmark or pure philosophy article

Main risk:

- lower reach outside A-Life / autonomy circles

### 2. Best-Balance Family

Best matches when the paper keeps its current object but adds a broader
translation layer:

- `Cognitive Systems Research`
- `Adaptive Behavior`

What these venues naturally absorb:

- theory-rich empirical hybrids
- artificial cognition and autonomy framing
- continuity criteria for artificial systems

Main advantage:

- better interdisciplinary reach without paying the full reframing tax of
  mainstream AI journals

Main risk:

- the paper must speak more explicitly in terms of bounded autonomy,
  cognitive systems, and continuity criteria rather than assuming A-Life
  vocabulary will carry the load

### 3. Reach-Max Family

Realistic only if the paper is deliberately refactored so the decomposition
itself becomes the main product:

- `JAIR`
- `Artificial Intelligence`
- `JETAI`

What these venues would want:

- operational definitions
- a stronger AI-evaluation payoff
- a clearer critique of coarse `self-replication` claims in AI
- more explicit evidence that the proposed decomposition changes how the field
  should count self-production

Main advantage:

- highest AI-side visibility

Main risk:

- high desk-reject risk if the paper still reads like a substrate-specific
  A-Life manuscript

## Current Shortlist

For the current paper object, the honest shortlist is:

1. `Artificial Life`
2. `Adaptive Behavior`
3. `Cognitive Systems Research`
4. `JAIR` only if the decomposition is promoted above the lineage case
5. `BioSystems` only if the autopoiesis / closure argument grows faster than
   the continuity / evaluation argument

Current non-priority routes:

- `Minds and Machines` as the first archival home
- `AAMAS / JAAMAS`
- `TMLR`, `JMLR`, `Machine Learning`, `AIES`

Those are not fake future routes. They are just mismatched to the paper's
current identity.

## Two Strategy Modes

### Strategy A: Maximize Acceptance And Correct Understanding

Primary goal:

- get the paper read by people who will understand the object without forcing
  it into the wrong genre

Recommended route:

1. keep the current identity as an A-Life / cognitive-autonomy boundary paper
2. use `Bounded Organizational Closure...` as the title
3. foreground successor production vs lineage continuity as the main empirical
   separation
4. treat autopoiesis as bounded discussion-level escalation, not title-level
   triumph
5. submit first to `Artificial Life`
6. keep `Adaptive Behavior` as the strongest nearby fallback

Why this works:

- it minimizes reframing tax
- it gives the paper the best odds of being judged on its real contribution
- it creates the cleanest anchor for the later paper family

Main tradeoff:

- narrower immediate audience

### Strategy B: Maximize Influence

Primary goal:

- make Paper A legible beyond A-Life without collapsing it into Paper B

Recommended route:

1. keep the same empirical core, but raise the translation layer
2. frame the paper as a decomposition of self-production and continuity in
   artificial cognitive systems
3. move `autopoiesis` to secondary theory language unless a venue clearly
   rewards it
4. make the five objects and the continuity distinction visibly useful beyond
   the Quine substrate
5. target `Cognitive Systems Research` as the first broad-but-honest reach
   venue
6. consider `JAIR` only if the decomposition itself becomes the main product
   and the paper grows a real AI-evaluation spine

Why this works:

- it increases reader reach without paying the full distortion cost of a
  mainstream-AI first submission
- it preserves the paper's distinctiveness while widening the translation
  layer

Main tradeoff:

- more framing work and a higher burden to justify cognitive-systems language

## Decision Rule

If the optimization target is:

- `most likely to be accepted and correctly understood`:
  choose `Artificial Life`
- `best compromise between reach and honest fit`:
  choose `Cognitive Systems Research`
- `maximum possible AI-side upside`:
  attempt `JAIR` only after the decomposition is strong enough to stand
  independently of the Quine lineage case

## Current Recommendation

For the paper in its current state:

- keep `Artificial Life` as the primary archival target
- keep `Adaptive Behavior` and `Cognitive Systems Research` as the nearest
  serious alternatives
- do not let the desire for reach silently convert Paper A into Paper B

The current highest-value sequencing is:

1. stabilize the boundary paper
2. publish it where the object is most likely to be understood
3. use Paper B, not Paper A, to push harder into broader AI evaluation
   audiences
