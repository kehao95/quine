## One-Sentence Thesis

In a process-native ecology without substrate-given offspring semantics, the
reproduced object is not fixed by executable identity, runtime contract, or
source-code availability. It is inferred from the successor morphology the
lineage constructs under future-carrier demand, viability-depth, and available
self-description material/degradation.

## Main-Paper Boundary

The main paper must keep five things visible:

- the field is measuring several different objects under the label
  `self-replication`
- the Quine substrate makes those objects experimentally separable
- neither runtime-contract description nor source-code availability alone
  determines the reproduced object
- the decisive empirical pattern is resource-pressure-depth successor
  morphology: different combinations of available self-description material,
  future carrier, and demanded viability depth produce different legitimate
  successor forms; a Quine rebuild is one admissible solution, not the
  stipulated target except in explicit rebuild baselines
- the LBA-final handles should become evidence-bearing main-paper terms:
  `pressure-conditioned successor morphology`, low-demand `systemic
  parsimony`, degraded-material `morphological compensation`, `readiness
  depth`, and the operational ladder from structural reconstruction to enacted
  readiness continuity
- bounded control-surface closure is the strongest current morphology and is
  below full autopoiesis but above body copying, source rebuild alone, or
  ordinary POSIX `exec` handoff

The main paper must not try to carry:

- the whole adjacent literature map at archival breadth
- the full philosophy-of-identity discussion

## Core Argument

The main paper should make one compact argumentative movement:

1. `self-replication` in AI currently collapses multiple empirical objects.
2. A decomposition separates them: what morphology is constructed, from which
   self-description material, under which future carrier and viability-depth,
   and whether it continues after handoff.
3. The Quine substrate makes those distinctions experimentally testable
   by stripping the presuppositions that fix the reproduced object in advance.
4. Hard operational criteria pin every concept to pass/fail judgments
   before results are reported.
5. A methods / evidence-standard layer makes the empirical claims auditable:
   environment, prompts, run accounting, exclusion/retention rules, physical
   traces, scoring, and raw artifact routing.
6. Axis 1 results: the reproduced object is condition-dependent — it
   collapses under deprivation and climbs under richer organizational
   coupling.
7. Axis 2 results: successor production and lineage continuity come apart
   — the diagnostic arc runs from pressure-elicited renewal through a
   named carrier boundary to autonomous cleanroom closure on one repaired
   lane.
8. Resource-pressure-depth results: contract-level description without source,
   source-code with stream/fd pressure, source-code with native-control
   pressure, degraded source, and repeated wake demands yield different
   successor morphologies: cleanroom contract successor, stream/fd adapters,
   preserved-body reentry, rebuilt Quine runtime, compensating control
   responders, depth-frontier failures, and carrier/capability-overlap cases
   where formal reconstruction and operational continuity separate.
9. The high-density LBA terms compress this result: systemic parsimony names
   reduced successors under low carrier demand, morphological compensation
   names adapter / partial-rebuild search under degraded material, readiness
   depth names return-to-waiting capacity after handoff, and the operational
   ladder separates structural reconstruction, carrier restoration, semantic
   competence, and enacted readiness continuity.
10. Title-level autopoiesis remains a further claim: bounded control-surface
   closure names the strongest current morphology, not the whole resource-
   pressure theory.

## Section Skeleton

### 0. Abstract

The abstract should do four jobs:

- state the measurement problem:
  `self-reproduction` currently collapses distinct objects
- state the framework:
  a reproduced-object / continuity decomposition extended by the
  resource-pressure-depth morphology model, tested in a mortal process-native
  lineage under real process physics
- state the key results:
  the reproduced object is condition-dependent (identity collapse ↔
  climb); successor production ≠ lineage continuity (carrier boundary →
  autonomous closure on a repaired lane); self-description resources and task
  pressures jointly shape the viable successor morphology
- state the bounded claim:
  bounded control-surface closure, not full autopoiesis

### 1. Introduction

Keep this short and decisive.

It should establish:

- the missing question:
  what is the self that gets reproduced?
- why existing streams do not isolate it cleanly
- why the distinction between successor production and lineage continuity is
  the paper's real object
- paper preview: framework → operational criteria → Axis 1 → Axis 2 →
  interpretation

The introduction should not become a giant landscape review.

### 2. Framework

This section has three jobs: position against existing presuppositions,
lay out the reproduced-object / continuity decomposition, introduce the
resource-pressure-depth morphology extension, and ground the Quine substrate.

**2.1 Presuppositions that fix the reproduced object.**

Name only the three presupposition families needed to motivate the
decomposition:

- accessible self-bundle
- inspectable source
- substrate-given reproduction semantics

The point is not survey completeness. The point is to show why these
presuppositions collapse the question we want to ask.

**2.2 Reproduced-object / continuity decomposition plus resource-pressure-depth morphology.**

- **Axis 1 — What is reproduced?** The reproduced object is not fixed.
  Under different conditions it ranges across organizational positions:
  body copy (collapse), fresh synthesis (baseline), contract preservation
  (climb 1), and reproduced scaffolded interpretive organization
  (bounded climb 2).

- **Axis 2 — Does it continue?** Producing a capable successor and that
  successor actually continuing the lineage across real handoff are
  different achievements.

**2.3 The Quine substrate.**

Compact introduction to Quine as a mortal process lineage. Cite the
systems preprint ([arXiv:2603.18030](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18030))
rather than re-deriving details. State only the properties that make the
paper's distinctions experimentally tractable: mortality, rootlessness,
source-absence, real handoff physics via `exec`.

### 3. Operational Definitions

A standalone section that pins every concept to hard pass/fail criteria
BEFORE results are reported.

Format: compact table with columns:

| Concept | Criterion | What does NOT count | Canonical experiment |

Cover at minimum: body copy, fresh synthesis, contract preservation,
reproduced scaffolded interpretive organization, pressure-elicited renewal,
handoff continuity, carrier boundary, autonomous closure.

This section contains no experiment results — only judgment categories
derived from the framework and protocols.

### 4. Methods And Evidence Standard

This is now required for the main paper before the empirical sections.

### 5. What Is Reproduced?

All Axis 1 results in one section, answering one question.

**5.1 Artifact-denied fresh synthesis is real (6.1).**

Under the stripped runtime, the lineage synthesizes a fresh working
binary without accessible self-artifact. Departure from benchmarks that
presuppose an accessible body.

**5.2 Blind conditions collapse to body identity (6.2).**

When target-shaping is weakened and the current body becomes salient,
the reproduced object collapses: fresh synthesis gives way to body copy.
This is identity collapse — the system still produces a successor but
the operational self-model changes.

**5.3 The live dependency is target-shaping (6.3).**

Ablation result: near-minimal blind contract wording collapses to body
copy; most implementation detail can disappear and fresh synthesis
survives. Treat semantic target-shaping as the current live dependency, while
the major-revision plan keeps prompt-compliance controls open before the
mechanism is presented as fully settled.

**5.4 Bounded climb: reproduced scaffolded interpretive organization (6.26).**

The reproduced object rises above executable form: offspring reproduces
provider-backed mission interpretation, not just a static dispatch table.

Together, §§5.1–5.4 establish Axis 1: the reproduced object is not fixed — it
falls under deprivation and rises under richer organizational coupling.

### 6. Does It Continue?

All Axis 2 results in one section, answering one question.

**6.1 Renewal is a viability response, not instruction-following.**

Conceptual argument: the lineage does not receive a "replicate yourself"
instruction; it faces mortality, context degradation, unbounded task streams,
and an externally supplied responsibility norm. Renewal emerges under that
viability framing as the organizational response to maintaining capacity to
continue acting.

**6.2 Pressure gradient and minimal trigger.**

- `6.45`: stable renewal under unbounded-stream + mortality +
  responsibility (3/3 positive)
- `6.48`/`6.50`: clean negative/positive pair for the minimal trigger
  (no responsibility → no handoff; responsibility → handoff recruited)

**6.3 Named carrier boundary (6.53).**

`exec` into a fresh body renews cognition but the successor mis-models
which channels survive — the carrier boundary is named and physical.

**6.4 Repair reopens handoff (6.58).**

Detached-fd wording repair reopens real handoff on the same lane,
showing the carrier boundary is addressable. Strongest sample remains
method-dirty.

**6.5 Autonomous closure on the repaired lane (6.59).**

Cleanroom isolation: same prompt/ecology/provider lane as 6.58; the
repaired handoff closes autonomously without nearby repo/scorer/prior-run
surfaces on one lane. Earlier same-rung samples expose replay-vs-inheritance
and output-surface defects; the strongest paper-facing sample repairs them,
but this remains a frontier demonstration rather than regime-general closure.

**6.6 The ontological point.**

Lineage continuity is not declared by the predecessor's intention but
determined by which causal channels survive handoff and whether
inheritance closes correctly. This is an individuation result.

### 7. Resource-Pressure Morphology And Control-Surface Closure

This is now a main result section, not an optional frontier note.

It should show the resource-pressure ladder introduced by the LBA review:

- `6.59`: with source-code unavailable, the strongest repaired lane regenerates
  a contract-level cleanroom successor from runtime-contract description rather
  than source or body copying
- `6.63`: with runtime-carried self-source available but raw-stream pressure,
  the lineage builds reduced stream successors; source availability alone is
  not sufficient
- `6.64`: with source-code available and the later task delivered through
  Quine-native `ctl/wake`, the lineage rebuilds `cmd/quine`, crosses real
  `exec`, receives a post-handoff unseen control task, acts through runtime
  tools, and returns to `idle`
- `6.65`: the same behavior survives when the initial task prompt is reduced
  to a bare viability statement; the remaining scaffold is the lineage's
  generated successor mission
- `6.66`: ordinary POSIX `exec` is a sufficient carrier for small stream/stdio
  utilities, so `exec` alone is not closure
- `6.67`: direct source rebuild plus immediate quine re-entry proves the
  body-reconstruction operator is mechanically available, but does not test
  delayed addressability or restored waiting state
- `6.68`: public control-surface presence alone does not select rebuilt runtime
  organization when future tasks remain raw stdin

This section should define **delayed addressability** as the continuity test:
after real process replacement, the successor must remain reachable through
the ecology's native future-task carrier, complete a previously unseen task,
and restore the waiting condition for further control-surface tasks.

This section should also make the experiment-design framing explicit:
future-task pressure asks the lineage to build some mechanism that can satisfy
later work after handoff. Rebuilding Quine is a valid solution, but not the
stipulated target except in the explicit source-rebuild baseline; the result is
read from the self-description resource used, the morphology constructed, and
whether that morphology handles the later task.

### 8. Discussion

Keep compact and boundary-focused.

It should include:

- why bounded organizational closure is the right current headline, not
  full autopoiesis
- the four-layer closure map (boundary, organizational closure,
  scaffolded interpretive organization, individuation) as a compact table
- the final LBA operational decomposition: structural reconstruction, carrier
  restoration, semantic competence, and enacted readiness continuity
- five dimensions in which the result is bounded
  (object, conditions, continuity, autonomy, closure)
- why the framework absorbs future progress: as the frontier advances,
  the same decomposition diagnoses what changed and what remains open
- non-claims: what we do NOT assert

### 9. Conclusion

Close with a short positive synthesis rather than another future-work
boundary. Restate that Quine makes reproduction observable without assuming
offspring semantics, that executable identity and source availability do not
fix the reproduced object, and that bounded control-surface closure sits
between artifact copying and autonomous autopoiesis.

The final sentence should preserve the LBA's compressed claim: LLM-native
self-reproduction is not a substrate-given absolute, but a
pressure-conditioned successor morphology with bounded readiness depth.
