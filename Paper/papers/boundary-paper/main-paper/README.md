now treated as the canonical main-paper home rather than a separate
public-first cut.

## Venue Posture

This configuration targets
[`artificial-life-journal`](../../../venues/objects/artificial-life-journal.md)
under the [`full-journal`](../../../venues/envelopes/full-journal.md)
format envelope. `Artificial Life` is the default journal-scale home because
the configuration expands the ALife-facing LBA object into the full evidence
and theory burden. `Adaptive Behavior` and `Cognitive Systems Research` remain
alternate shortlist readings only if the later manuscript's center of gravity
moves away from the ALife-native journal frame.

In the current three-paper split, this is the **evidence** paper:

```text
LBA = announcement
main Paper A = evidence
future-addressability = generalization
```

It should make the Quine ladder auditable and bounded. It should not become
the theory paper on computational identity, and it should not push the LBA's
two-page compression into full literature or trace accounting.

Its job is to:

- expand the LBA's `ALIFE` object into a full boundary paper
- expand the LBA's final compressed terms into evidence-bearing main-paper
  concepts rather than treating them as new unsupported theory
- preserve the reproduced-object / continuity decomposition while making
  resource-pressure-depth successor morphology the full-paper interpretation of
  the late evidence
- distinguish self-description material layers: runtime contract / system prompt,
  preserved body, runtime-carried source-code, and degraded source
- show why neither runtime contract nor source-code availability alone
  determines the reproduced object
- frame future tasks as cross-lifetime pressure: rebuilding Quine is a possible
  solution, not a stipulated target, and the paper observes which successor
  morphology is regenerated and whether it satisfies later work
- lead with facultative self-reproduction (rebuild elicited under a
  control-surface pull, not the default), stating bounded control-surface closure
  as the criterion for the strongest positive: stronger than body copying or
  ordinary handoff and weaker than full autopoiesis

It should not become a broad Paper-B-style framework, a safety benchmark
survey, or an unqualified autopoiesis claim.
