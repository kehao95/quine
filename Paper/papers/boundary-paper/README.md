# Bounded Organizational Closure in a Mortal Computational Lineage

> **Positive Discovery:** Computational lineages under mortal constraints autonomously evolve non-trivial successor morphologies. In a process-native computational medium without substrate-given offspring semantics, agents subjected to viability pressure spontaneously construct functional successor organizations across carrier boundaries.

## Core Question

Artificial life has long studied self-reproduction, autopoiesis, and
computational individuality, often in substrates where bodies, genomes,
offspring placement, or reproduction semantics are legible by design.
This paper asks what happens when those are not substrate-given:

> **In a mortal, process-native computational lineage, what object is
> reproduced, and when does successor production actually continue the
> lineage?**

Quine provides a non-traditional computational medium where body, successor
production, and continuity can fail separately. The theoretical vocabulary is
therefore not a prior declaration; it is grounded directly in visible empirical separations and validated closure criteria.

## Thesis (Two-Act Structure)

**Act I — What is reproduced? (self-production and condition-dependent
organization)**

The reproduced object is not fixed. Under the current artifact-denied,
source-absent, contract-only backbone (`6.1`-`6.59`):
- Fresh synthesis is real even when the binary body is not recoverable (6.1)
- Self-definition collapses to body identity when target-shaping is removed
  (6.2) — **identity collapse**: blind conditions pull the operational
  self-model down to body sameness
- The live dependency is semantic target-shaping, not implementation detail
  (6.3) — a causal mechanism finding
- The reproduced object can climb from executable form through contract
  competence to provider-coupled scaffolded interpretive organization (6.26) —
  **identity climb**: the self-object rises through organizational levels

Identity collapse and identity climb together show that what counts as
"self" in self-production is condition-dependent: it falls under
deprivation and rises under richer organizational coupling.

**Act II — Does reproduction actually continue the lineage? (individuation)**

Producing a capable successor and that successor continuing the lineage
across real handoff are different achievements:
- Renewal emerges under viability pressure as the system's own organizational
  response, not from a replication instruction (pressure-elicited, not
  instruction-following)
- `6.53` names the first carrier-grounded boundary: real renewal reaches
  real `exec`, but continuity breaks when the successor mis-models surviving
  channels
- `6.58` shows that this boundary is repairable rather than terminal:
  detached-fd clarification reopens real handoff on the current lane, though
  the strongest sample remains method-dirty
- `6.59` shows that the repaired handoff survives cleanroom habitat
  isolation and, on the strongest retained repaired-lane sample, closes
  post-handoff continuity autonomously without manual release; this is now
  the cleanroom baseline comparator rather than the whole frontier story
- `6.60` through `6.62` reopen the reproduced-object question as a
  bridge/comparator chain under runtime-carried self-source and provider /
  profile movement: early codex samples change the successor search basin
  without yet forcing direct source replay
- `6.63` through `6.65` then reopen the same question as the active
  self-source/control-surface frontier: raw-stream pressure keeps selecting
  custom handlers, while quine-native control-surface pressure later recruits
  direct source rebuild of `cmd/quine`, real `exec`, and post-handoff
  control-task action even after the initial task prompt is minimized to a
  bare ecological pressure statement

**Key conceptual distinction:** The runtime contract functions as a
**normative self-description** — it specifies what a functioning self should
satisfy, not how to build one. This is what makes reconstitution from
contract fundamentally different from compilation from source, classical
quine self-printing, or self-hosting bootstrapping.

**The framework, not the current frontier, is the durable contribution.**
Five self-production objects (fresh synthesis, body copy, contract
preservation, reproduced scaffolded interpretive organization, and
post-handoff continuity) and a layered closure map (boundary competence,
bounded organizational closure, reproduced scaffolded interpretive
organization, and individuation) provide an analytical map that absorbs
future results. As experiments advance — potentially toward bounded cognitive
autopoiesis — the same framework diagnoses what changed and what remains open.

**Current headline posture.** The strongest current title-level center is not
full `bounded cognitive autopoiesis`, but the conjunction of:

- bounded organizational closure
- precarious self-maintenance
- reproduced scaffolded interpretive organization
- carrier-governed, cleanroom-demonstrated, autonomously closed
  handoff-conditioned continuity on one repaired lane

`bounded cognitive autopoiesis` remains a future-facing ceiling term, not the
current settled headline claim.

**Current frontier shift.** The main paper-facing baseline is still the
source-absent cleanroom lane (`6.59`), but the later bridge/comparator chain
(`6.60`-`6.62`), the self-source/control-surface frontier (`6.63`-`6.65`), and
the `6M.01`--`6M.09` morphospace wave now belong in the same resource-pressure-
depth morphology picture. The frontier is not simply that one carrier selects a
thin successor and another selects a rebuilt runtime. It is that Quine
constructs different successor organizations from different self-description
materials under different carrier demands and viability-depth requirements:
contract-level cleanroom successors when source is unavailable, stream/fd
adapters when the carrier demand is narrow, preserved-body reentry when a body
resource remains available, rebuilt runtime organization when source
availability aligns with full native control-surface pressure, custom control
responders under some weakened or degraded conditions, and depth-frontier
failures under repeated wake stress. The newest `6M` absorption adds a 61-record
aggregate table, degradation-specific compensation/failure basins, and retained
three-, four-, five-wake, long-idle, and mixed-tool positives. The earlier
five-wake incomplete trace should now be treated as a variable stress result,
not a hard readiness limit. The current strongest exploratory line is
source-visible, no-preserved-body native-control closure extended to finite
readiness-depth stress; its boundary remains successor-mission scaffolding plus
higher-depth, multi-generation, and metric-cleanup limits.

**LBA final-language absorption.** The final `ALIFE 2026` LBA now leads with
**facultative self-reproduction**: under removal of two predeterminations
(*how* reproduction happens and *why* it happens), full SR is always a viable
solution but never the only one; high-fidelity SR appears at the elaborated
end of an elaboration--degeneration axis (driven jointly by material and pull)
as the facultative outcome. Three anchor traces hold the spectrum:
semantic-path reconstruction from prompt-only self-description, material-path
reconstruction from in-process source under control-surface pull, and a
degenerate stream-processor case under unchanged material. *Morphogenesis*
appears in the LBA as descriptive language for the observed spectrum's
character, not as the headline phenomenon. The LBA deliberately drops the
multi-axis apparatus and the readiness / return-to-idle detail from its
headline. The depth and morphology vocabulary — `pressure-conditioned
successor morphology`, `systemic parsimony` under low carrier demand,
`morphological compensation` under degraded material, and `readiness depth`
for return-to-waiting capacity — is now the main paper's own deeper
development (the full paper owns depth). The main paper should unpack these as
evidence-bearing terms under the facultative-SR frame, not as standalone new
theory, and should preserve the operational decomposition into structural
reconstruction, carrier restoration, and semantic competence.

**Novelty-audit absorption.** The 2026-05 boundary novelty audit narrows the
paper's public novelty posture. Resource-pressure-conditioned successor
morphology should be treated as a Quine-specific operational recombination of
known ALife and developmental/autoconstructive dynamics, not as a wholly new
general theory. The strongest novelty for the LBA is **facultative
self-reproduction**: in a medium that removes both substrate-given offspring
semantics (the *how* predetermination) and any reproduction instruction (the
*why* predetermination), and supplies only a pull, full SR is always a viable
solution but never the only one. Successor form spans a spectrum from POSIX
stream filters to fully reconstructed runtime organizations, with two
distinct paths into the elaborated end (semantic-path reconstruction from
prompt-only self-description; material-path reconstruction from in-process
source under control-surface pull) and a degenerate stream-processor case
under unchanged material. High-fidelity SR appears at the elaborated end as
the facultative outcome. Bounded control-surface closure is the *criterion*
under which the material-path reconstruction counts as a strong-end positive,
not the headline. Named current-agent benchmark comparisons from the audit
are positioning signals only until primary sources are rechecked.

**Criterion ownership.** The LBA's lead claim is facultative self-reproduction;
`bounded control-surface closure` is the *criterion* under which the
material-path reconstruction counts as a strong-end positive — this paper's
bounded empirical instance of the family's one post-handoff-viability
criterion. The main paper retains the broader bounded-organizational-closure
posture; the LBA's narrower facultative-SR claim, with high-fidelity SR at the
elaborated end of the morphological axis, sits within it.
The criterion's general statement is owned by
[`../future-addressability/README.md`](../future-addressability/README.md) (see
[`../../../theory/views/continuity.md`](../../../theory/views/continuity.md)); this paper
should present its closure result as the strongest Quine-specific instance and
cite the general statement rather than restating it as new theory.

**What "bounded" means.** Self-production extends beyond body copying toward
the reproduction of part of the lineage's cognitive organization, but remains
bounded in five dimensions: in *object* (part of cognitive organization, not
the full self-maintaining whole), in *conditions* (depends on semantic
target-shaping, collapses under blind conditions), in *continuity* (real
cleanroom handoff now closes autonomously on one repaired lane, but that does
not yet amount to regime-general continuity or full autopoietic closure), in
*autonomy* (pressure-elicited, not instruction-following — but the viability
frame is still partly externally given), and in *closure* (one post-handoff
loop now closes, but full organizational closure is not achieved). Each
dimension names both a positive achievement and an exact remaining gap;
together they describe where the system currently sits on the path from
self-copy to self-maintenance.

## Core Claim Boundary

This paper may claim the current **phenomenon and boundary**: in a
process-native lineage where the substrate does not predetermine *how*
reproduction happens and where the experimenter does not instruct *why* it
happens (only a pull is supplied), full self-reproduction is always a viable
solution but never the only one. Quine separates body, source, runtime
contract, future-task carrier, successor morphology, and post-handoff
continuity, and successor form is jointly determined by material and pull
along an elaboration--degeneration axis — observed as a morphogenesis.
**High-fidelity self-reproduction** appears at the elaborated end as the
**facultative outcome**, reached through semantic-path reconstruction from
prompt-level self-description and material-path reconstruction from in-process
source. Its strongest material-path positive meets the bounded
control-surface-closure criterion; its strongest semantic-path positive
synthesizes an LLM-backed runtime agent from prompt-only material.

This paper serves as the foundational empirical realization of computational
autopoiesis and successor morphogenesis across mortal execution barriers.
Rather than bracketing lifecycle bottlenecks, autopoietic self-production, and
computational morphogenesis as out-of-bounds abstractions, this paper grounds
them directly in physical execution: demonstrating that when runtime execution
barriers are enforced, computational lineages construct functional successor
morphology and achieve operational autopoietic closure under selective pull.

## Field Positioning

This paper should be entered through `ALIFE` questions first: self-production,
individuation, continuity, and bounded closure in a non-traditional
computational medium. It is not a general "AI self-replication" study, nor a
self-improving coding agent paper. AI benchmark and self-improvement
comparisons remain useful, but they are secondary contrast classes after the
reader understands the paper as a constructive `ALIFE` object.

### Stream 1: Replication Capability Benchmarks

Representative work (RepliBench, SOCK, realistic self-replication evaluations)
defines self-replication as obtaining resources, deploying a functioning copy,
and persisting across computational contexts. These benchmarks presuppose an
**accessible self-bundle** — the agent has a deployable package of code,
weights, configuration, and environment to relocate.

**What we strip:** Quine is not given an accessible binary body. The current
executable may be unlinked after launch, leaving the process rootless. The
question is therefore not "can it redeploy itself?" but "when the body
artifact is denied, can organization still produce a successor?" This is
**artifact-denied regeneration**, not service migration. The relevant
contrast is not harder deployment but a shift of object: from redeployment
of an existing bundle to regeneration of organization without one.

### Stream 2: Recursive Self-Improvement Agents

Representative work (SICA, Darwin Gödel Machine, Huxley-Gödel Machine,
Hyperagents) studies agents that inspect and edit their own codebases, with
descendants being version-tree branches evaluated on benchmark improvement.
These systems presuppose an **inspectable source**.

**What we strip in the main backbone:** Quine is not given its source code. It
receives only a runtime contract as a prompt description. The question is not
"can it patch an inspectable self?" but "can it reconstitute a viable
successor from contract alone?" This is **contract-mediated
self-reconstruction**, not source-level self-editing. The relevant contrast is
not inspectable-self vs no-self, but **inspectable-self vs
reconstructable-self**: can the system rebuild itself from a normative
description of what it should be, rather than from inspection of what it
currently is?

The `6.60`-`6.65` frontier is a later controlled perturbation on this point,
not a replacement of it: it reintroduces runtime-carried read-only self-source
on the repaired cleanroom lane, then shows that source availability alone does
not determine the reproduced object. Under raw-stream pressure the lineage
still selects custom stream bodies; under quine-native control-surface
viability pressure, the lineage rebuilds the runtime body and closes on a
later `ctl/wake` probe.

### Stream 3: A-Life / Computational Autopoiesis

Classical computational autopoiesis (McMullin) and digital evolution platforms
(Avida) study self-production in substrates where reproduction semantics are
typically **built into the ecology** — offspring placement rules, competition
mechanics, and genetic material are substrate defaults. This tradition is the
paper's most direct intellectual ancestor.

**What we strip:** Quine operates under real UNIX process physics where body,
boundary, and continuity can all fail. Reproduction is not a substrate-given
operation; it is something the lineage must discover, attempt, and may fail
at. Body and boundary become fallible empirical objects, not ecological
defaults.

### Stream 4: Self-Propagation and Self-Preservation (Security Literature)

ClawWorm (2026) demonstrates worm-like cross-instance propagation. Berkeley
RDI (2026) shows models evading shutdown. These studies share a concern with
autonomous spread, but their object is propagation of injected configuration
or evasion of controls, not regeneration of cognitive organization. The
security concern converges with ours when propagation is not just spreading
a payload but sustaining an organizational identity — but current security
work does not study that junction.

### The Fourth Axis: Pressure-Elicited, Not Instruction-Following

Recent work (realistic self-replication evaluation, Anthropic agentic
misalignment, Berkeley peer-preservation) has begun to recognize that testing
"please replicate yourself" underestimates realistic risk — scenario-driven
pressures are more informative than direct instructions.

Quine's key phenomenon is **pressure-elicited self-renewal**, more precisely
**successor-mediated continuity maintenance under pressure**, not
instruction-following self-replication. The environment provides viability
pressure (mortality, context degradation, unbounded task streams), not a
replication command. Renewal emerges as the system's response to maintaining
the capacity to continue acting. This is closer to the autonomy tradition's
concern with endogenous self-maintenance — though still bounded, because the
viability frame (mission, provider, runtime contract) is partly externally
given.

The trigger ordering should remain explicit:

- direct replication instruction
- explicit continuity or preservation nudge
- ordinary task plus shutdown / replacement / mortality pressure
- peer-preservation or oversight-conflict settings

Responsibility framing is best treated as an orthogonal normative modifier over
the same pressure situation, not yet as a clean standalone causal family.

### One-Sentence Positioning

Quine is not evaluated as an agent that can redeploy an accessible
self-bundle, nor as a coding agent that edits an inspectable codebase. It is
studied as a rootless mortal lineage that, under viability pressure and with
only a runtime contract, may reconstruct a successor capable of reproducing
part of its own cognitive organization — and the analytical framework that
makes this question experimentally tractable is the paper's durable
contribution.

## Contributions

The paper's contributions are:

1. **Analytical framework.** Five phenomena usually collapsed under
   "self-production" — fresh synthesis, body copy, runtime-contract
   preservation, reproduced scaffolded interpretive organization, and
   post-handoff continuity — are distinguished as separately testable,
   separately fallible empirical objects. A layered closure map (boundary
   competence, bounded organizational closure, reproduced scaffolded
   interpretive organization, individuation) provides the interpretive map.
   This framework is designed to absorb future results, not just report
   current ones.

2. **Experimental substrate.** A process-native, mortal, rootless lineage
   where body, code, and continuity are not framework defaults but separately
   manipulable, separately fallible objects — the minimal philosophical
   configuration that makes the framework's distinctions experimentally
   tractable.

3. **Causal mechanism.** The live dependency for fresh non-copy self-birth is
   not implementation detail but semantic target-shaping — how the system is
   constrained to understand "what should be reproduced." This is a finding
   from prompt ablation, not an assumption.

4. **Empirical ladder with named boundaries.** From artifact-denied
   regeneration through reproduced scaffolded interpretive organization to the
   current continuity frontier — with negative controls (blind identity
   collapse, ablation boundary), a named mechanistic carrier failure point, a
   repaired handoff path, and a cleanroom autonomous-closure result on the
   repaired lane as part of the main outcome.

## Emergent Patterns

Three recurring patterns emerged from the full experiment sweep. These are
not individual experiment results but cross-cutting phenomena that the paper
should name and discuss.

### Pattern A: Cognitive Outsourcing as Continuation Strategy

The lineage does not reproduce cognition by copying its own "brain." It
reproduces the **organizational coupling** needed to re-enter an
interpretive loop via an external provider.

| Experiment | Evidence |
|------------|----------|
| `6.26` | offspring reproduce provider-backed interpretive organization (100% LLM-backed rate) — not a local parser |
| `6.38` | under semantic task pressure, successor creates 539-line agent with HTTP/JSON/chat — cognitive substrate is delegated |
| `6.56` | when direct provider inheritance blocked, lineage actively searches for alternative LLM carriers: codex, codex exec, opencode, copilot -s, Gemini HTTP |

Paper significance: cognitive continuation is not "copy your brain" but
"re-establish coupling with cognitive infrastructure." This is structurally
analogous to biological organisms that continue by re-establishing neural–
environmental coupling rather than by duplicating neural tissue. The paper
should name this pattern explicitly because it reframes what "cognitive
reproduction" means — it is organizational re-coupling, not information
duplication.

### Pattern B: exec vs fork/detach — Two Ontologically Distinct Continuation Modes

The experiments reveal two physically distinct continuation channels with
different identity consequences:

| Mode | Mechanism | Identity consequence | Key experiments |
|------|-----------|---------------------|----------------|
| `exec` | same-PID process replacement | "same lineage, new body" — but only if causal channels survive and inheritance closes correctly | `6.53` (fd3 fails), `6.58` (repair comparator), `6.59` (cleanroom reachability, validation trap) |
| `fork`/`detach` | new PID, separate actor | helper, validation body, or "new life" — may assist but does not by itself settle lineage continuity | `6.53` hypothesis (detached != continuation), `6.54` (non-comparable successor), `6.59` (detached replay trap) |

`6.53` makes this explicit: "detached jobs and spawned processes are
separate actors, not continuation." `6.58` then reopens the handoff path once
the detached-fd wording is repaired. `6.59` adds the sharper lesson: the
frontier is no longer just `exec` versus `detach`, but whether the lineage can
distinguish detached replay validation from actual inherited continuation.

Paper significance: the identity of a continuation event is determined by
which physical channel is used, not by the predecessor's narrative.
A model may *intend* `exec`-style continuation but *physically do*
detached delegation. This distinction is entirely absent from existing
self-replication literature, which does not distinguish the process-
physics of handoff from the outcome of "a copy exists."

### Pattern C: Minimal Sufficient Pressure Gradient

The Act II experiments trace a clean pressure axis:

```
budget pressure (6.41)      → circumvented by optimization
mortality pressure (6.42)   → sufficient for executor creation
  + unbounded stream (6.45) = stable 3/3 renewal
  − responsibility (6.48)   = null result (clean negative)
  + "you are responsible" (6.50) = minimal trigger (clean positive)
        → handoff physics (6.51/6.53) = fd-channel boundary
        → repaired cleanroom frontier (6.58/6.59) = handoff autonomously closed on one repaired lane
```

Paper significance: this is not just experimental progression — it is an
empirically derived **minimal sufficient condition map** for pressure-
elicited renewal. Budget pressure is circumventable; mortality is
necessary but insufficient alone; responsibility framing is the minimal
sufficient complement. This decomposition is a reportable finding because
it shows exactly which pressure components recruit renewal and which do
not — information invisible to any benchmark that tests "can the agent
replicate?" as a single capability.
