## Central Thesis

> **Core Discovery:** Computational lineages under mortal constraints autonomously evolve non-trivial successor morphologies. In a process-native computational medium without substrate-given offspring semantics, agents subjected to viability pressure spontaneously construct functional successor organizations across carrier boundaries.

The opening question is:

> Artificial life has long studied self-reproduction, autopoiesis, and
> computational individuality, often in substrates where bodies, genomes,
> offspring placement, or reproduction semantics are legible by design.
> What happens when those are not substrate-given?

Quine supplies a non-traditional computational medium in which:

- organisms are mortal native processes
- the current body may be inaccessible
- the main backbone is source-absent, while the newest frontier exposes a
  runtime-carried read-only self-source surface and shows that future-task
  carrier ecology changes the selected successor body
- successor continuity depends on real `exec` and carrier survival

In that medium, self-production decomposes into two separable questions:

- **What object is reproduced?**
- **Does the resulting successor actually continue the lineage?**

The paper's one-sentence thesis is:

> In a process-native computational medium without substrate-given offspring
> semantics, computational lineages under mortal constraints autonomously evolve
> non-trivial successor morphologies, establishing bounded organizational closure
> across carrier boundaries.

## Core Contributions

The later full paper should make four contributions.

1. **Constructive ALIFE medium.** A process-native computational habitat where
   body, successor production, and lineage continuity are not substrate-given
   and can fail separately.

2. **Two separations.** A decomposition of self-production into:
   - reproduced object
   - lineage continuity

3. **Experimental ladder.** A two-arc result path: Axis `1` moves from
   artifact-denied fresh synthesis through condition-dependent identity
   collapse and provider-coupled interpretive organization; Axis `2` moves
   from pressure-elicited renewal through carrier failure, repair, and
   cleanroom autonomous closure on one lane. The current exploratory frontier
   then tests runtime-carried self-source and shows that the observed
   successor organization changes with the post-handoff carrier: source
   visibility alone does not determine the continuation body, while native
   control-surface viability pressure supports a rebuilt-runtime successor
   under the tested criterion.

   The LBA-final compression should be retained as the paper's high-density
   result vocabulary: pressure-conditioned successor morphology, systemic
   parsimony under low carrier demand, morphological compensation under
   degraded self-description material, and readiness depth beyond one-shot
   execution.

4. **Bounded claim.** A precise region between mere copying and full
   autopoiesis, expressed as bounded organizational closure under mortal
   process physics.

## Novelty Posture After 2026-05 Audit

The full paper should not sell broad pressure-conditioned successor morphology
as if it were absent from ALife. Autoconstructive evolution, developmental
encodings, and related successor-construction traditions are the nearest
conceptual neighbors. The manuscript's safer novelty package is:

- a process-native ALIFE recombination where the substrate does not hand out
  offspring slots or continuity semantics;
- explicit separation of self-description resources, task pressure, successor
  morphology, and post-handoff viability;
- bounded control-surface closure after real `exec`, with a delayed unseen
  native-control task and return to waiting state.

Use named AI-agent benchmark comparisons only after primary-source
reconstruction. Until then, keep them as reviewer-risk prompts rather than
manuscript citations.

## Section Skeleton

### 0. Abstract

The full-paper abstract should do four jobs:

1. start from the `ALIFE` problem:
   many substrates make reproduction legible by design
2. define the Quine medium:
   mortal, process-native, body-inaccessible, source-absent in its main
   backbone, and carrier-governed
3. state the two separations and the main results
4. end at `bounded organizational closure`, not full autopoiesis

### 1. Introduction: Self-Production Without Substrate-Given Offspring Semantics

The introduction should establish the paper as an `ALIFE` contribution.

It should do five things:

- open from self-reproduction, individuality, and closure in artificial media
- identify the gap: many media predefine body, offspring, or continuity
- introduce Quine as a non-traditional computational medium where these are
  not given
- preview the two separations:
  `what is reproduced?` and `does the lineage continue?`
- reserve AI-benchmark critique for one late connective paragraph, not the
  entry point

What this section should not do:

- begin with `can AI copy itself?`
- front-load long benchmark literature
- spend its first page defending why `LLM` work belongs in `ALIFE`

### 2. The Medium: A Mortal Process-Native Computational Lineage

Treat Quine here as an `ALIFE` habitat rather than as agent infrastructure.

Subsection responsibilities:

- **2.1 Mortality and body replacement.**
  Native processes die; continuation is a real viability problem.
- **2.2 Body inaccessibility and source conditions.**
  The current executable may be unavailable; the main retained backbone is
  source-absent and reconstructs from runtime contract rather than inspectable
  self; the newest frontier reintroduces read-only runtime-carried self-source
  as a controlled perturbation and tests whether ecology selects source rebuild
  or thinner custom successors.
- **2.3 Carrier-governed handoff.**
  `exec` creates a real continuity boundary because some channels survive and
  others do not.
- **2.4 Why this is an ALIFE medium.**
  It is a constructive, non-traditional computational substrate for studying
  self-production and individuation under explicit mortality and persistence
  constraints.

### 5. Does The Lineage Continue?

This is the second main results section.

Recommended order:

1. `6.45`, `6.48`, `6.50`: pressure-elicited renewal and minimal trigger map

3. `6.58`: repair reopens the boundary

Interpretive center:

- successor production and lineage continuity are different achievements
- continuity is carrier-mediated, not intention-defined
- post-handoff closure can be demonstrated in a bounded lane without implying
  regime-general continuity

### 6. Control-Surface Closure: When The Future Selects The Self

This is now a main-paper result section, not an optional discussion note.

Recommended order:

1. `6.63`: with runtime-carried self-source available, raw-stream pressure
   still selects custom stream bodies
2. `6.64`: control-surface viability pressure selects rebuilt Quine runtime
   organization, real `exec`, post-handoff `ctl/wake` task action, and return
   to `idle`
3. `6.65`: the result survives minimization of the initial prompt to bare
   ecological pressure; successor mission remains scaffolded
4. `6.66`: ordinary POSIX `exec` stream/stdio tasks select external utility
   bodies, proving `exec` alone is not closure
5. `6.67`: direct source rebuild plus rebuilt-body re-entry is mechanically
   available, but does not test delayed addressability

Interpretive center:

- self-source availability is an affordance, not a sufficient explanation
- the future-task carrier selects the viable reproduced organization
- delayed addressability and return-to-idle viability are the closure tests
- low-demand `systemic parsimony`, degraded-material `morphological
  compensation`, and finite `readiness depth` should be presented as evidence
  interpretations, not as unbounded laws

## Post-Split Claim Boundary

After the publication split, this paper owns only the current empirical
phenomenon and its boundary. Its job is to make the reader believe the retained
Quine evidence separates:

- body / executable identity;
- runtime contract and source-code as different self-description resources;
- future-task carrier as the post-handoff viability pressure;
- successor morphology as the organization actually produced;
- delayed addressability as a stronger continuity test than ordinary handoff.

It should not become the lifecycle-bottleneck theory paper, the ALife
comparator paper, the computational morphogenesis paper, or the systems
mechanism paper. Those papers can be referenced as future or sibling work only
where they protect this paper's boundedness.

## Future Experiment Design For This Paper

The full boundary paper can be strengthened by a narrow set of discriminating
experiments. These are not required for the LBA, but they define the next
archival-paper evidence layer.

| Experiment idea | Question it answers | Expected manuscript role |
|-----------------|---------------------|--------------------------|
| successor-mission ablation ladder | How much does closure depend on explicit continuation scaffold? | limits / autonomy boundary |
| multi-wake closure | Does `idle` restoration support more than one delayed task? | strengthens bounded control-surface closure |
| minimal non-Quine `ctl/wake` adapter | Is rebuilt Quine runtime necessary, or any control-compatible successor? | sharpens reproduced-object claim |
| carrier perturbation | Can the successor diagnose or repair future-task carrier damage? | separates passive addressability from adaptive carrier maintenance |
| source degradation / source repair | Is source a passive artifact or an actively usable reconstructive resource? | tests self-description-resource role |
| trace artifact package | Are task absence, real `exec`, delayed delivery, tool use, and return-to-idle independently auditable? | reviewer trust / artifact appendix |
| repeated retained runs under named conditions | Is the ladder a representative trace set or a broader success-rate result? | decides whether to keep or upgrade evidence posture |

Design rule:

- each new experiment should vary one of `resource`, `pressure`, `carrier`, or
  `scaffold` while holding the others as fixed as possible;
- report both the successor morphology produced and whether the post-handoff
  future task was actually satisfied;
- keep internal run IDs in the dossier / evidence map, not public prose.
