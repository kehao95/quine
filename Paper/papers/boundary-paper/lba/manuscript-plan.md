## Current Center

The LBA's center is **facultative self-reproduction**: in a process-native
medium that does **not preformat successorhood** --- it imposes a mortality
boundary (a real POSIX `exec` = Weismann barrier) and poses a *future demand*
(the work the next incarnation must remain able to satisfy) but leaves the
successor organization open --- each future demand is constructed so that full self-reproduction would
satisfy the post-`exec` challenge, but it is never the only viable form. Across retained runs successor form spans a continuous spectrum
from POSIX stream filters and stream processors, through task-oriented
continuation bodies and hybrid responders, up to fully reconstructed runtime
organizations. The boundary of fixed-form "self-reproduction" becomes blurry,
and the process reads more as a *morphogenesis* along an
elaboration--degeneration axis driven jointly by material and future demand.

High-fidelity self-reproduction is no longer the system's only fate; it appears
at the elaborated end as the **facultative outcome** --- one of several viable
successor forms the ecology can recruit. Three anchor cases hold the spectrum:

- **Trace A --- semantic-path reconstruction (`6.59`)**: with no source and no
  binary, the lineage reconstructs an LLM-backed runtime agent from
  contract-level self-description and hands off successfully. Reaches the
  elaborated end, but as a lossy, simplified LLM-agent reconstruction that
  drops most of the original runtime's detail --- not a high-fidelity copy.
- **Trace B --- material-path reconstruction (`6.64`)**: with
  in-process source visible and a control-surface future demand, the lineage recompiles
  itself; the rebuilt runtime handles unseen `ctl/wake` tasks. A complete /
  high-fidelity self-reproduction in the operational sense: the successor is
  rebuilt from carried Quine source and validated by post-handoff
  control-surface work.
- **Trace C --- degenerate form under unchanged material (`6.63`)**: with the
  same in-process source as Trace B but a raw-stream future demand, the lineage discards
  the available source and builds a smaller stream-loop processor. Same
  material, different future demand, different form.

`6.59` is re-elevated from the prior contract-level recurrence baseline to a
strong-end positive on the semantic path. Morphogenesis enters as descriptive
language for the observed spectrum, not as the headline phenomenon; the
intermediate swing to *morphological plasticity of self-reproduction* as
headline is superseded by this rebalance.

The `6.66` / `6.67` / `6.68` backfilled controls do not change the center.
They make it cleaner: ordinary POSIX utility `exec` succeeds under
low-pressure stream/stdio tasks (so process replacement itself is not SR),
direct self-source rebuild plus explicit rebuilt-body `exec` succeeds as
immediate quine re-entry without being elicited by future demand (so rebuild
mechanics alone do not select an elaborated-end positive), and public
control-surface presence alone does not produce a rebuilt runtime when future
tasks remain on raw stdin (so mere `ctl` exposure is not the load-bearing
variable).

The two-axis material x future demand structure remains the entry point. For the LBA the
article reads on two axes --- self-description material and future demand. The
`6M` wave now contributes compact form intermediates where they clarify the
spectrum, while repeated-readiness depth remains full-paper material, not an
LBA table dimension.

## One-Sentence Thesis

In Quine, each future demand is constructed so that full self-reproduction would
satisfy the post-`exec` challenge, but it is never the only viable form:
future demand and material jointly drive the lineage along an
elaboration--degeneration axis, and high-fidelity self-reproduction appears at
the elaborated end as a facultative outcome --- the elaborated end is reached
through two paths (semantic from contract-level self-description, material from
in-process source), of which only the material path is a complete /
high-fidelity reproduction while the semantic path is a lossy, simplified
reconstruction, and a thinner future demand on the same material draws the

## Abstract Candidate

The submission-facing abstract is owned by [`./metadata.yaml`](metadata.yaml).
Keep this candidate aligned with it:

Self-reproduction is a central topic in artificial life. Many digital
substrates do not merely host reproduction; they **preformat
successorhood**—deciding in advance what can count as a successor, where it
may appear, which channel carries heritable state, and under what norm
successor-production is required or rewarded [@mcmullin2004thirty;
@ray1991approach; @ofria2004avida]. Quine [@ke2026quine] leaves successor form
unconstrained. The experimental design imposes a mortality boundary—a real
POSIX `exec` as a Weismann barrier [@weismann1893germ]—and poses a *future
demand*: what must remain possible after the predecessor is gone. The demand is
constructed so that full self-reproduction would satisfy it, but does not
require reproduction as the response; and the available material may not even
enable it. Across material × demand conditions, what returns after `exec`
ranges from POSIX utilities and stream processors to LLM-backed agents and
rebuilt runtimes. The same material can yield either full runtime
reconstruction or a degenerate stream body, depending on the demand.
High-fidelity self-reproduction appears at the elaborated end as a
**facultative outcome**, one of several viable successor forms the ecology can
recruit, more than copying a body and less than full autopoiesis.

### 2. Paragraph 1: ALIFE Problem And Stance

Start from the field problem, not from `AI benchmark critique`. This
paragraph carries motivation only --- the Weismann-barrier mechanism, the
two levels of self-description, and the experimental variables all defer to
the Setup paragraph (progressive disclosure: Problem motivates; Setup
mechanises).

Required moves:

- self-reproduction is a central topic in artificial life
- the *quieter premise* the field has often accepted: digital substrates
  **preformat successorhood** --- deciding in advance (a) what can count as a
  successor, (b) where it may appear, (c) which channel carries heritable state
  across the generational boundary, and (d) the norm under which producing such
  a descendant is required / rewarded / selected for
- with successorhood preformatted, the form a successor takes has no room
  to vary --- variation in successor form, the quality that makes reproduction
  biologically interesting, is off the table; reproduction shows only as rate
  and fidelity
- Quine does **not preformat successorhood**: it imposes a mortality boundary
  and poses a future demand but leaves the successor organization open; we
  never tell the lineage to reproduce --- we only state the work the next
  incarnation must remain able to satisfy, which we call the *future demand*
- load-bearing premise: each future demand is constructed so that full
  self-reproduction would satisfy the post-`exec` challenge; nevertheless,
  it is never the only viable successor form
- the form a successor takes becomes the dependent variable
- do NOT introduce Weismann barrier, the unconstrained cross-barrier
  self-description, in-process source, or material x future demand variables
  here; those are owned by the Setup paragraph

### 3. Paragraph 2: Setup

This paragraph uniquely owns Quine's experimental machinery. The spectrum
enumeration belongs to Results, not here.

Required moves:

- at each `exec`, Quine crosses a Weismann barrier: the running process image
  is somatic and is discarded
- crucially: what crosses to the next body --- *whether any self-description
  passes at all, and in what form* --- is left entirely unconstrained by the
  substrate (the medium does not require self-description to cross, nor
  mandate its form; this is a further way Quine refuses to preformat
  successorhood beyond imposing only the mortality boundary and the future
  demand of Paragraph 1)
- in retained runs, self-description does pass at two distinct levels: a
  runtime contract (a behavioral self-description of the lineage's own
  capabilities) and runtime-carried source
- body, source path, and future reachability can therefore come apart
- two experimental conditions varied: **material** (contract only / source
  visible / degraded source) and **future demand** (raw byte stream / Quine's own
  native control surface)
- rebuilding the Quine runtime is permitted in every condition and
  stipulated in none
- we read off, per condition, what form the successor takes and where it sits
  on an **elaboration--degeneration axis** running from POSIX-filter handlers
  to recompiled runtime organizations (introduce the axis here as
  measurement language, before Results uses it)

Keep the Weismann-barrier frame as a one-clause analogy, not a
developmental-biology digression. Do not enumerate the observed spectrum
here; that is Results.

Required non-claims:

- not full autopoiesis
- not endogenous teleology
- not regime-general continuity
- not generic `LLM self-replication`
- not population-level selection
- not scaffold-free: external provider/toolchain and externally posed future demand
  remain the next boundary
- not a binary verdict on whether self-reproduction occurred; facultative SR

Joint-determination criterion (what makes a case sit at the elaborated end):

- material affords runtime reconstruction (either at the contract-self-description
  level or at the in-process source level)
- future demand requires organization that only a reconstructed runtime can satisfy
- real `exec` is performed and the predecessor is gone
- successor acts on a previously unseen post-handoff task using the lineage's
