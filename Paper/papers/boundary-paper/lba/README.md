## Venue Posture

This configuration targets [`alife-2026-lba`](../../../venues/objects/alife-2026-lba.md)
under the [`2page-lba`](../../../venues/envelopes/2page-lba.md) format
envelope. Its job is to announce the facultative self-reproduction result in
ALife language without taking on the later full-paper evidence burden.

Its job is to announce the result, not to carry the full evidence or theory
burden. The current series routing is:

```text
LBA = announcement
main Paper A = evidence
future-addressability = generalization
```

Concretely, it should:

- position the paper as an `ALIFE` object first
- open from self-reproduction as the ALife problem, and diagnose how digital
  substrates **preformat successorhood** --- deciding in advance (a) what can
  count as a successor, (b) where it may appear, (c) which channel carries
  heritable state across the generational boundary, and (d) the norm under
  which producing such a descendant is required / rewarded / selected for; the
  cost is that variation in successor form (the quality that makes reproduction
  biologically interesting) is taken off the table
- frame Quine as a process-native medium that **does not preformat
  successorhood**: it imposes a mortality boundary (a real POSIX `exec` =
  Weismann barrier) and poses a *future demand* (the work the next incarnation
  must remain able to satisfy) but leaves the successor organization open.
  Defer the experimental machinery (Weismann barrier, cross-barrier-content
  openness, material x future-demand variables) to the Setup paragraph for
  progressive disclosure
- state the load-bearing premise: each future demand is constructed so that full
  self-reproduction would satisfy the post-`exec` challenge, but it is
  never the only viable successor form
- name the headline phenomenon as **facultative self-reproduction**; describe
  the observed character of the result as *morphogenesis* (the boundary of
  fixed-form SR blurs, and successor formation reads as morphogenesis along an
  elaboration--degeneration axis driven jointly by material and future demand); refer to
  the elaborated end as **high-fidelity self-reproduction**, the facultative
  outcome
- make the representative evidence visible through three anchor traces ---
  semantic-path reconstruction (contract-only material yields an LLM-backed
  runtime agent, a lossy simplified reconstruction), material-path
  reconstruction (carried source plus control-surface future demand yields a
  recompiled Quine runtime, the complete / high-fidelity self-reproduction),
  and a degenerate variant under unchanged material (same carried source plus
  raw-stream future demand yields a smaller stream processor) --- plus a
  compact spectrum table and controls (POSIX `exec`, direct rebuild,
  control-surface-present raw carrier)
- end at a bounded claim that is stronger than copying and weaker than full
  autopoiesis

It should not try to:

- compress the entire later full paper into `2` pages
- carry the full artifact / trace / benchmark comparison burden
- become the general future-addressability theory paper
- open from `AI benchmark critique`
- front-load long theory digressions
- over-translate one repaired-lane positive into full autopoiesis

## Keep In

- **facultative self-reproduction** as the headline phenomenon: each future demand is
  constructed so that full SR would satisfy the post-`exec` challenge, but it
  is never the only viable form; high-fidelity SR appears at
  the elaborated end of an elaboration--degeneration axis as a facultative
  outcome --- one of several viable successor forms the ecology can recruit
- the *preformatted successorhood* diagnosis: digital substrates decide in
  advance (a) what can count as a successor, (b) where it may appear, (c) which
  channel carries heritable state across the generational boundary, and (d) the
  norm under which producing such a descendant is required / rewarded /
  selected for --- the cost being that variation in successor form is off the
  table; Quine does not preformat successorhood
- the demand constrains what must remain *possible* after `exec`, not what must
  *exist*; it is built so that full self-reproduction would satisfy it but does
  not require reproduction, and the material may not even enable it; the
  experimenter never instructs the lineage to reproduce --- only the *future
  demand* (the work the next incarnation must remain able to satisfy) is
  supplied
- the load-bearing premise: each future demand is constructed so that full
  self-reproduction would satisfy the post-`exec` challenge, but it is never
  the only viable successor form
- *morphogenesis* as descriptive language for the observed character of the
  spectrum (the boundary of fixed-form SR blurs; the process reads more as
  morphogenesis) --- NOT as the headline term
- the observed spectrum of successor forms --- POSIX stream filters, custom
  stream processors, task-oriented continuation bodies, hybrid responders,
  LLM-backed runtime agents, recompiled Quine runtimes
- joint determination: the specific form is jointly determined by the
  **material** the lineage carries forward and the **future demand** it must satisfy;
  under a change in either, the lineage moves along an
  elaboration--degeneration axis
- two paths into the elaborated end, of which only the material path is a
  complete / high-fidelity reproduction: *semantic-path reconstruction*
  (Trace A, `6.59`) reaches the elaborated end but as a lossy, simplified
  LLM-agent reconstruction that drops most runtime detail, while
  *material-path reconstruction* (Trace B, `6.64`) is a complete
  self-reproduction in the operational sense: the successor is rebuilt from
  carried Quine source and validated by post-handoff control-surface work
- one *degenerate-end* trace under unchanged material (Trace C, `6.63`) ---
  same carried source, raw-stream future demand, smaller stream processor
- substrate-given offspring semantics as the thing being relaxed, not the
  absence of offspring semantics altogether
- process-native computational lineage
- `exec` framed as a Weismann barrier: the somatic process image is discarded;
  what crosses to the next body --- *whether any self-description passes at
  all, and in what form* --- is itself left open rather than preformatted by
  the substrate. The Weismann-barrier frame, with this cross-barrier-content
  point, is owned by Setup; Problem does not introduce it
- the **material** carried forward as self-description, along a fidelity stack:
  the lineage's own source code, the runtime binary (the body), or only a
  runtime contract (a behavioral self-description of the lineage's own
  capabilities). (The earlier *in-process source* gloss --- source carried by
  the running process rather than read from an external checkout --- was cut
  from the Setup body and is no longer asserted as a manuscript fact)
- **high-fidelity self-reproduction** as the name for the elaborated end of
  the axis; *facultative self-reproduction* is the bounded claim that
  high-fidelity SR is one of several viable forms the ecology recruits
- `6.66` / `6.67` / `6.68` only as clean baseline controls if space allows:
  ordinary POSIX utility handoff gives the `exec`-is-not-SR baseline, direct
  self-source rebuild gives the rebuild-alone-is-not-elicited baseline, and
  control-surface-present raw stdin shows that mere public control-surface
  presence does not recruit a rebuilt runtime
- what could internalize the future demand from within the lineage as the named next
  boundary
- explicit non-claims below full autopoiesis

## Keep Out

- long benchmark-literature setup
- long philosophy-of-identity setup
- every adjacent framework term
- generic `AI self-replication` language
- open-ended evolution as a current claim; it may appear only as distant
  motivation or future direction
- LLM-agent autonomy as the headline; the LLM agent framing is the medium, not
  the paper's primary venue claim
- unqualified `autopoiesis` headline escalation
- unqualified population-level `selection` language
- full-paper ablation matrices unless they are compressed into next-step prose
- `control-surface closure` as the headline result; it remains a demoted
  mechanism detail, not the paper's named claim
- *morphological plasticity* / *successor morphogenesis* as the headline
  phenomenon; morphogenesis is descriptive, not the announced phenomenon
- readiness depth, return-to-idle, and repeated-wake as LBA-level content; they
  belong to the full paper
- binary-judgement framing of self-reproduction (`did it reproduce or not`);
  facultative SR positions high-fidelity SR as one of several viable outcomes,
  not as a yes/no verdict
- minimality / optimality phrasing (`minimal viable`, `minimum`, `optimal`);
  the evidence supports contextually sufficient / down-selected successor
  forms, not a proof that no smaller organization could satisfy the demand
- necessity / entailment phrasing for future demand; demand *shapes* a position on
  the axis, it does not force a specific form
- demand-dominance claims; future demand and material jointly determine the form
