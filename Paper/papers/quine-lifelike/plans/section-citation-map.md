# Section Citation Map

This file maps the current citation skeleton onto the manuscript sections.

The goal is not to maximize citations. The goal is to give each argumentative move a small number of citations that do real work.

## Section 1: Introduction

Primary anchors:

- `Langton1989ArtificialLife`
- `Beer2024ALifeAsItCouldBe`
- `Ray1992SynthesisOfLife`
- `OfriaWilke2004Avida`

Use these to establish:

- ALIFE studies life in constructed media, not only life as currently instantiated
- Quine should be introduced as a new synthetic medium, not as a productized agent system
- classic digital-organism systems are relevant precedents, but they typically inhabit bespoke virtual ecologies rather than a real UNIX/POSIX habitat
- if the introduction now foregrounds the methodological gap, use `McMullin2004ThirtyYearsComputationalAutopoiesis` as the maintenance-aware comparator and keep the contrast narrow: prior systems often make viability real, but not obviously separable along the three pressure axes

Keep optional unless needed:

- `Bedau2003ArtificialLifeReview`

Use only if the introduction needs one extra sentence about ALIFE as bottom-up study of organization, adaptation, and complexity.

Optional ontology-facing additions if the introduction keeps one compact sentence on process-native individuality:

- `NicholsonDupre2018EverythingFlows`
- `Nicholson2018ReconceptualizingOrganism`
- `Pradeu2018GenidentityBiologicalProcesses`

Their job is to support one narrow point only:

- process ontology is a legitimate way to think about identity-through-time in living systems
- for this paper, that process stance should be used methodologically rather than as a grand metaphysical thesis

Historical-precedent caution:

- if the introduction or discussion gestures at Quine as unusual for being process-native, keep the claim narrow
- the internal note on `DREME` / `Eden` means the paper should avoid saying no ALIFE system ever tied agents to OS processes
- the safer public claim is that Quine makes the process/object distinction explicit and experimentally consequential in a current soft-ALIFE setting

Optional addition:

- `OdlingSmeeLaland2011EcologicalInheritance` if the prose needs a sharper distinction between ecological inheritance and generic external note-taking

- `Dawkins1982ExtendedPhenotype`
- `Turner2000ExtendedOrganism`

Their job is narrower:

- support `environmental organ` as an introduced analogy rather than an established ALIFE category

Red line:

- do not cite `MaturanaVarela1980Autopoiesis` in a way that implies the paper has already demonstrated strong autopoiesis
