# Plans

This directory holds forward-looking support plans for the ALIFE conference paper.

Current files:

- `alife-reference-deep-research-plan.md`: external-reading and investigation plan for the paper's ALIFE framing
- `experiment-escalation-plan.md`: graded plan for when to rerun, repair, or redesign experiments, plus how to backfill resulting evidence into the manuscript
- `fork-lifecycle-paper-skeleton.md`: downstream paper skeleton for computational life cycles, renewal, branching, and bounded reproduction
- `fork-next-experiment-designs.md`: next-step experiment ladder after the repaired `3.2` fork-born lineage assay
- `process-ecology-experiment-map.md`: durable map from broader process-ecology directions to the current experiment inventory and evidence strength
- `quine-philosophy-novelty-map.md`: lane split for process ontology, ALIFE novelty, systems claims, and future autopoiesis claims
- `section-citation-map.md`: section-by-section citation placement map for the current manuscript draft
- `submission-checklist.md`: submission-facing checklist tracking current build state, open decisions, and final pre-submit checks

These files support drafting. They do not replace the dossier, manuscript plan, or manuscript source.
