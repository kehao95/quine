# Dossier: Substrate-Centered

**Status: Prior framing** (superseded 2026-04-02; retained for reference)

This dossier captures the prior root-paper center.

## Core Question

In a non-traditional computational substrate built from mortal processes and
durable environmental state, what forms can continuity, inheritance, and
self-maintenance take when mortality, persistence, and maintenance become
experimentally manipulable pressures?

## Core Claim

Quine is a synthetic ALIFE substrate in Langton's sense: a real UNIX/POSIX
habitat whose process boundaries, durable state, permissions, and host
interventions make viability pressures experimentally tractable.

## What It Foregrounds

- the habitat as the paper's causal medium
- substrate novelty as venue-facing shell
- reproducible, auditable, tunable experimental knobs
- pressure-sensitive selection inside a non-traditional computational medium

## What It Plays Down

- direct `what could life be here?` language as the opening center
- stronger interpretive language about life-like organization
- OS-as-substance wording unless it is tied to method

## Main Risks

- can read like a systems paper dressed in ALIFE language
- can over-invest in defending the medium rather than answering the biological question
- can leave the reader remembering the habitat more than the organizations
