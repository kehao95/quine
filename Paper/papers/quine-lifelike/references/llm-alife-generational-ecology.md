# LLM-ALIFE Work on Mortality, Ecological Inheritance, and Environmental Memory

This note absorbs recent LLM-ALIFE and digital niche-construction comparators relevant to Quine's mortality, persistence, and maintenance claims.

It is a reference-side synthesis, not manuscript prose.

## Current Read

The closest recent near-neighbor to Quine is no longer generic `LLM agents in ALIFE`, but the narrower class:

- LLM agents embedded in persistent ecologies with explicit mortality, resource pressure, reproduction, and artifacts that outlive individuals

The most important direct comparator in that class is:

- `PaoloEtAl2026TerraLingua`

The strongest non-LLM evidence standards for ecological inheritance continue to come from digital / embodied niche-construction work:

- `ChibaEtAl2020NicheConstruction`
- `JaafarEtAl2025EcoEvoDevo`
- `MatthewsEtAl2014UnderNicheConstruction`

## LLM-ALIFE Comparator Tiers

### 1. Closest conceptual neighbor: persistent LLM ecologies with explicit mortality

`PaoloEtAl2026TerraLingua` is the most important recent comparator for any claim that Quine is unusual because it studies LLM agents under:

- finite lifespans
- resource constraints
- reproduction
- persistent artifacts that survive individuals

Why it matters:

- it weakens any broad novelty claim of the form `Quine is the first LLM ecology with death, reproduction, and persistent cultural traces`
- it strengthens the case that ALIFE now has a recognizable subarea around LLM ecologies rather than isolated toy demonstrations

Why Quine can still differ:

- Quine's death boundary is infrastructural and process-level rather than only in-world energy/lifespan
- Quine's main method claim is not cumulative culture in general, but **substrate-level separability** of mortality, persistence, and maintenance pressures
- Quine's pressure axis includes detached maintenance channels, which are not the center of TerraLingua

### 2. Mortality / survival pressure without strong ecological inheritance center

`MasumoriIkegami2025SurvivalInstinctSugarscape` is the clearest comparator for:

- LLM agents under explicit energetic viability
- death at zero energy
- scarcity-sensitive shifts in behavior

It matters because it shows:

- recent work already treats LLM agents as ecological entities under survival pressure
- mortality and scarcity effects are not unique to Quine

But it does **not** obviously undercut Quine's ecological-inheritance claim because:

- the main environmental substrate is resource and obstacle layout, not a durable artifact lineage
- persistence and cross-generational environmental legacy are not the center of the contribution

`ZhouEtAl2025MoralEvolution` belongs in the same supporting tier:

- evolutionary simulation with survival / reproduction language
- useful as evidence that generational replacement exists in recent LLM work
- weaker as a niche-construction or environmental-memory comparator

### 3. Multi-generational cultural evolution without metabolism-style death

These papers matter for narrowing Quine's novelty boundary:

- `VallinderHughes2024CulturalEvolutionCooperation`
- `HoriguchiEtAl2024SocialNorms`

They show:

- LLM populations can be analyzed across generations
- norm transmission and cultural-evolution questions are already active

They do **not** directly compete with Quine's mortality or ecological-inheritance claims because:

- generations are usually an experimental deployment structure rather than enforced viability turnover
- they are closer to social/cultural evolution than to life-like organization under explicit death and persistence pressure

### 4. ALIFE-adjacent but not organism-under-pressure comparators

These papers help delimit the broader `LLM + ALIFE` space without being direct competitors:

- `MeyersonEtAl2024LanguageModelCrossover`
- `SuzukiArita2024PersonalityEvolutionLLM`
- `RuanEtAl2025SwarmBench`

Their role in the manuscript should be defensive and classificatory:

- they show that LLMs are already used as evolutionary operators, genotype-phenotype maps, or decentralized coordination agents
- they do not make mortality, persistence, and maintenance into the paper's central manipulable pressures

## Ecological Inheritance: What Counts as Evidence

### Theory-side standard

`MatthewsEtAl2014UnderNicheConstruction` is the most useful methods-facing citation here.

Why it matters:

- it gives an operational way to distinguish environmental modification from genuine niche-construction evidence
- it emphasizes explicit tests that organism-mediated environmental change alters selection pressures and evolutionary response

For Quine, this implies a useful hierarchy:

- weak: predecessor wrote an artifact
- better: successor reads and behavior changes
- strong: persistence/accessibility ablation changes downstream behavior or viability
- strongest: the environmental legacy measurably reshapes lineage-level success, not only information access

### Digital ALIFE precedent

`ChibaEtAl2020NicheConstruction` is a strong precedent because it explicitly evolves:

- niche-constructing behaviors
- ecological inheritance of structures and birthplace
- fitness / diversity effects under inheritance toggles

Why it matters for Quine:

- it shows digital ALIFE already treats persistence toggles and inheritance conditions as experimentally meaningful variables
- it raises the bar above `agent wrote files` by tying environmental carry-over to adaptivity and diversity effects

`JaafarEtAl2025EcoEvoDevo` is also useful because it shows:

- ecological inheritance can be varied in degree
- more persistence is not automatically better
- environmental carry-over can become maladaptive when obstacles accumulate

This is a valuable caution for Quine:

- do not write environmental memory as necessarily beneficial
- write it as a manipulable condition whose effects can be positive, neutral, or harmful depending on the ecology

## Main Implications for Quine

### What Quine can no longer plausibly claim

After adding these comparators, avoid claims like:

- first LLM ecology with mortality and reproduction
- first LLM ecology with persistent artifacts
- first digital ALIFE system where environmental persistence matters

`PaoloEtAl2026TerraLingua`, `MasumoriIkegami2025SurvivalInstinctSugarscape`, and the digital niche-construction literature are too close.

### What Quine can still plausibly claim

The defensible novelty pocket is narrower and better:

- Quine makes **process-level mortality, environmental persistence, and detached maintenance channels jointly available in one substrate**
- those pressures are **experimentally separable enough** to support pressure-specific contrasts
- the paper demonstrates not only persistent artifacts, but also detached maintenance as a distinct pressure-sensitive organization

### What this does to the paper's center

This pushes the main claim away from:

- `Quine shows LLMs can behave like organisms`

and toward:

- `Quine provides a distinctive experimental medium for separating viability pressures that recent LLM ecologies and classic ALIFE substrates often couple differently`

## Recommended Use In Manuscript

### Introduction

Use one compact paragraph to say:

- ALIFE and LLMs already intersect in the literature
- recent work has begun embedding LLM agents in ecologies, including mortality-aware and artifact-bearing ones
- Quine's niche is not `LLMs in ALIFE` generically, but pressure separability plus process-level death and detached maintenance

### Methods / Section 2

Use `MatthewsEtAl2014UnderNicheConstruction` plus `ChibaEtAl2020NicheConstruction` / `JaafarEtAl2025EcoEvoDevo` to motivate:

- persistence ablations
- accessibility / sham-persistence controls
- why environmental memory needs more than file existence

### Discussion

Use `PaoloEtAl2026TerraLingua` as the closest living comparator and state the distinction narrowly:

- TerraLingua foregrounds cumulative culture and social organization in a persistent LLM ecology
- Quine foregrounds separable mortality, persistence, and maintenance pressures in a process-native computational lineage

## Red Lines

- do not write as if TerraLingua, Sugarscape-style survival, and LLM cultural-evolution papers do not exist
- do not claim ecological inheritance from artifact persistence alone without emphasizing ablations and downstream behavioral consequences
- do not frame Quine's novelty as `LLM agents + ALIFE`; that territory is already occupied
- do not overclaim that detached maintenance is equivalent to autopoiesis or general self-repair in ALIFE writ large
