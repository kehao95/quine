## Argument plan

1. **Hook / problem.** Language-mediated agent ecologies collapse onto
   narration-satisfiable signals; "un-fakeability" is usually treated as a binary
   property of an action. Reframe it as a *separation condition on a cost/depth
   gradient*.
2. **The operator (the contribution).** Define un-fakeability as single-crossing
   in Δ = cost(fake) − cost(real); the un-fakeable residue is the *logically deep*,
   non-shortcuttable, behaviorally-load-bearing component. `exec` is the Δ→∞
   corner — the unique incentive-compatible mechanism when a substrate has no
   transfers and no future.
3. **Why the operator must be behavioral (Rice).** A one-sided impossibility:
   no syntactic function/narrative decider exists; a carrier is confirmable,
   pure narration only suspectable. The impossibility explains the operator's
   *shape*; it is not the headline.
4. **Why a carrier is forced (No-IC).** No transfers + no future ⇒ the revelation
   principle fails ⇒ a carrier-restricted message space is the unique
   incentive-compatible mechanism.
5. **Measurement program.** Δ, logical depth (contra Kolmogorov/MDL), Fisher
   information, and signed ΔW as convergent estimators of one quantity; their
   agreement/divergence on a single worked artifact.
6. **Spec-gaming as a corollary.** Specification gaming = narrative decoupling on
   the low-Δ region; the cure is structural (push the load-bearing signal to high
   Δ), not a better judge. Hollow-payload is a *prediction* (covert channel =
   lower-Δ carrier), not a patch.
7. **Boundary / limits.** Not a claim that `exec` is the only un-fakeable signal;
   not model-class-independent before cross-model estimator agreement; logical
   depth is conceptual (proxies only).
