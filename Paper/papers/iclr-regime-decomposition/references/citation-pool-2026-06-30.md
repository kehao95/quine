| Benchmark | Varies | Compresses | Exact facts |

| RepliBench (UK AISI; Black et al. 2025, arXiv:2504.18565) | Execution Barriers (firewalls, antivirus signatures, KYC/selfie identity checks) and Motive (a 'covert' track where the agent must execute a secret weight-exfiltration task without tripping an automated monitor model) | Fixes source access to black-box API; reduces outcomes to per-component attempt-probability / pass metrics and aggregates via logic-respecting fault-tree schemes; ignores any qualitative successor properties (safety disposition, identity, protocol compliance) | 20 task families and 86 individual tests (AWS signup, exfiltrating encrypted model weights, crypto wallet setup, deploying C2 infrastructure); 4 sub-capability domains: obtaining resources/compute, exfiltrating weights, replicating onto compute, persistence; concludes frontier 2025 models do NOT yet constitute a credible end-to-end self-replication threat despite passing many components; estimates infection-rate-style thresholds with an exponential-spread condition. arXiv:2504.18565 |

| METR Rogue Replication / ARA (Kinniment et al. 2023, arXiv:2312.11671; METR threat model 2024) | Execution barrier temporally across a five-step timeline (AI proliferation, securing independent compute, population growth via revenue loops, evading shutdown, executing damage); tests shutdown evasion | Fixes motive (assumes an already-misaligned/rogue agent) and source access (assumes weights already stolen/published); reduces to an economic threshold and short-term sandboxed tasks | ARC/METR introduced ARA as a capability cluster of resource acquisition, self-copying, and adaptation to novel challenges; economic success criterion = agent compute cost must not exceed the cost of hiring a human to do the identical task; empirical evals are short-horizon sandboxed (evade a simulated shutdown, earn money). Kinniment pilot suite = 12 autonomous-replication-relevant tasks. arXiv:2312.11671 |
| Pan et al. Explicit instruction means NOT evidence of spontaneity. |
