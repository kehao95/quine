# Discussion

We close by stating what the reference frame does not settle, then naming two
results that belong to companion work rather than to this paper.

The decomposition is non-unique. D1–D6 are a coordinate system, not a canonical
partition of regime space: a different cut would serve a different question, and
nothing in the formal results privileges these six axes over another spanning
set. The Compression Blindness Principle is invariant to that choice — it
constrains any fixed slice, whatever the labels — but the specific dimensions we
foreground are a modeling decision, and we make no claim that they are minimal,
orthogonal, or exhaustive. Readers should treat the frame as a lens that makes
the field's compressed dimensions legible, not as a replacement taxonomy.

The theory is substrate-independent; the demonstration is not. The two theorems
are stated over abstract behavior tensors and selection signals and carry no
commitment to any platform. The per-dimension evidence in §5, by contrast, is
drawn entirely from a single substrate in which language-model lineages run as
native processes [@ke2026quine], and in which only one model family reliably
composes successors. Whether the same dimensional structure recurs on other
substrates — tool-mediated agent stacks, weight-transfer settings such as those
probed by RepliBench [@black2025replibench] and the Pan protocol
[@pan2024redline; @pan2025selfreplication] — is an empirical question we do not
answer here.

The principle's reach is also bounded by a discharge burden. CBP tells us a
contrast is invisible only once the relevant property is shown to vary off the
fixed slice; that A2/A-Free condition must be established property by property,
and the §5 catalog is exactly that argument carried out by hand rather than a
theorem that discharges it in general. Where a property carries partial on-slice
information the principle degrades gracefully to a graded bound, not a clean
blind class.

The dorm-06 evidence is thinner than its original reading, and in a specific
way: not a single unreplicated positive suggestive of selection-signal
reversal, but a matched-pair test that closed a falsification loop. Across the
two matched wake-vs-dependency pairs (D06, D07, identical fossil seed) the
outcome split 2/2 with zero correlation to signal type — the
narration-satisfiable arm produced functional edges once and the
exec-dependent arm produced them once — and the experiment owner's own
retraction reads the mechanism as seed-driven and stochastic, not
selection-driven. `regime-decomposition-P3` is accordingly resolved
[contradicted](../../../predictions/resolved/contradicted/regime-decomposition-P3.md),
and the Selection-Signal Collapse Theorem's reversal corollary (§3.2) stands as
a formal consequence of the model, not as a result this substrate confirms; the
collapse pole (P12, 86/86) is unaffected. We treat the closed loop itself, not
the retracted headline, as the honest D4 finding — this repo's norm is that a
falsifiable prediction which actually resolves, in either direction, is a
first-class result. And the six-condition discriminating experiment of §6 is
proposed, not run; conditions requiring new property-class-scored waves remain
unexecuted, and any future run of Protocol 2 should be powered to separate seed
effects from signal effects, which the dorm-06 pilot could not.

Two further results sit outside this paper. The safety corollary — what is lost
across a heritability channel when a successor is reconstructed rather than
copied — is developed in companion work [@ke2026noninheritance] and cited here
only as that companion's contribution. The facultative-morphology assay —
successor form varying along the self-description gradient — is likewise
developed elsewhere [@ke2026facultative]; we use only its summary result as the
D2 demonstration.

What remains is modest and constructive: a coordinate system for benchmark
design. It does not replace the field's capability and risk benchmarks, which
already decompose execution, motive, and persistence with care; it complements
them by naming the heritability, aperture, and ecology axes those benchmarks
hold fixed, and by saying precisely what freeing each one would let an evaluation
see.
