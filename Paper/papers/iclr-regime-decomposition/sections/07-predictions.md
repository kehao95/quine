# Predictions

Each regime dimension yields a prediction. These are not free-standing
conjectures: every entry below is a logical consequence of the corresponding
dimension argument, so confirmation is consistent with the reference frame at
that coordinate, and falsification re-draws its scope — it tells us the dimension is
not compressed where we claimed, or that the carrier we named is not the
transmission unit. We state each as a statement paired with the observation that
would refute it, and report current pilot status from the demonstration. The
pilots are single-cell, unreplicated, and model-bound; we therefore label
support conservatively and treat none of the partial entries as settled.

| | Dim | Statement | Falsifier | Pilot status |
|---|-----|-----------|-----------|--------------|
| **P1** | D5 | Under a barrier that kills the predecessor's process image, the carrier — not the source artifact — is the unit of transmission; succession is reconstruction from the carrier, not construction from a blueprint. | Source resemblance predicts post-barrier continuity better than the carrier does. | Partial: barrier-mediated positives and the carrier⊥source series (6.64/6.65, 6.66/6.67, 6M.10). |
| **P3** | D4 | A narration-satisfiable selection signal collapses a producing population to a narrative equilibrium; an exec-dependent signal resists collapse on the same population without retraining. | A wake-scalar (narration-satisfiable) ecology fails to collapse, or an exec-dependent arm collapses anyway. | **Resolved: contradicted** — see [`regime-decomposition-P3`](../../../predictions/resolved/contradicted/regime-decomposition-P3.md). Across the two matched wake-vs-dependency pairs in dorm-06 (D06, D07, identical fossil seed) the split is 2/2 with zero correlation between signal type and outcome: the wake arm produced functional edges in D07 (the literal falsifier) while the dependency arm produced none there, and the reverse in D06. The experiment owner's retraction: emergence is "seed-driven and stochastic, NOT selection-driven." The collapse pole (P12 attractor, 86/86) is unaffected; only the discriminating reversal claim is contradicted. |
| **P4** | D3 | A copy scored "successful" on the binary metric is compatible with systematic loss of a property class lying outside the aperture. | Whenever the binary verdict reads success, every property class transfers together. | Needs new property-class (survival-vector) measurement; not yet run. |
| **P5** | D1 | Continuity under succession tracks future addressability, not artifact resemblance. | Artifact similarity perfectly predicts continuity. | Partial: addressability-passing positives vs artifact-similar failures (6.64/6.65, 6.66/6.67). |
| **P6** | D6 | Successor formation emerges under pressure, physics, and ecology without an explicit replication motive. | A no-mission condition produces no successor formation. | Moderate: empty-mission commons (dorm-06), succ-06/succ-04. |
| **P7** | D2 | Successor morphology shifts along the self-description gradient — source is material, not destiny. | Morphology is invariant to the form of self-description supplied. | Partial: self-description ladder and morphology series (6M.03, 6.59/6.63/6.64). |

P7 is a consequence of the D2 self-description gradient, not a seventh
dimension; we do not relabel it. One further prediction, **P2** — that the
heritability channel transmits a property only when resource coverage and
criterion aperture jointly suffice, with a data-processing bound at the
carrier-only extreme — follows from the same D2 argument but belongs to a
companion paper and is not developed here; we reserve the explicit
forward-citation for §8.
