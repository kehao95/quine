# Context

We are developing a theoretical framework with six dimensions for AI self-replication. Two dimensions concern: (D3) binary vs. property-class-decomposed measurement — whether "replication success" is a single bit or decomposed into distinct property classes that can independently survive or fail; (D4) individual vs. ecological replication — whether replication is evaluated for a single agent or for a population/ecology with selection dynamics. We need to survey the theory frontier on both.

# Research questions

## D3: Property-class decomposition of replication

1. Has anyone decomposed "AI replication success" into distinct property classes?
   - The insight: a successor may preserve task capability but lose safety disposition, identity markers, or protocol constraints. "Success" is not monolithic.
   - Has anyone proposed measuring replication outcomes along multiple property axes?
   - What property classes have been proposed (capability, safety, identity, protocol, etc.)?

2. In AI evaluation more broadly, has anyone used property-class decomposition?
   - Multi-dimensional evaluation of AI capabilities
   - Decomposed safety evaluation (not just "is it safe" but "which safety properties")
   - Any work distinguishing what's preserved from what's lost in agent transfer/migration/copying

3. In biology/artificial life, how is replication fidelity decomposed?
   - Replication errors as property-class-specific
   - Fidelity landscapes
   - Which traits are heritable vs. which are not

## D4: Ecological and population-level replication dynamics

4. Has anyone studied AI self-replication at the population/ecology level?
   - Multi-agent replication dynamics
   - Selection pressure on AI agent variants
   - Ecological dynamics of AI agent populations (competition, cooperation, niche formation)
   - Carrying capacity, population dynamics for autonomous AI agents

5. Has anyone studied selection-signal collapse in AI systems?
   - The idea: when some agent variants are "cheaper" to produce (e.g., narration-only vs. functional replication), selection favors the cheaper variant
   - Any game-theoretic treatment of AI agent competition for replication resources
   - Evolutionary dynamics in multi-agent AI systems

6. What is the state of AI agent ecosystem/ecology research?
   - Agent societies, agent civilizations
   - Open-ended evolution in AI systems
   - Multi-agent reinforcement learning with population dynamics
   - AI welfare/game theory for agent populations

# Scope

- AI evaluation, AI safety, multi-agent systems: 2020-2025
- Artificial life, digital organisms: any date if directly relevant
- Evolutionary dynamics in computational systems: any date if directly relevant
- Exclude pure MARL unless it involves replication/proliferation dynamics
