# Publication possibilities for organizational dynamics

Date: 2026-09-07. Status: comparison, no new venue binding or submission.
This is the publication projection of the
[literature contact](../../../theory/references/computational-neoplasm-history/literature-contact-2026-09-07.md)
and [live theoretical gap](../../../theory/gaps/organizational-continuation-and-transformation.md).
It follows the [publication strategy](../../publication-strategy.md): a paper is
one bounded contribution, while the research reservoir remains broader.

## Recommended posture

Treat this as organizational dynamics in persistent agent substrates. Artificial
life and complex systems supply the central explanatory questions. Systemic and
multi-agent alignment supplies a consequential question about purposes, collective
effects, and revisability. A software repository is an experimental medium; merely
demonstrating an AI application is not the intended contribution.

There are three plausible paper forms, not three simultaneous submissions of the
same result:

1. A developed empirical/theoretical account of organizational inheritance and
   transformation, including useful maintenance and pathological persistence.
2. A grounded research vision connecting organizational morphogenesis to systemic
   alignment, with explicit unresolved mechanisms and discriminating questions.
3. A narrower causal study of one relation: inherited authority, participating
   diagnosis, or persistence after revision. This becomes attractive when matched
   interventions and independent contexts can bear the claim.

## Existing exposure and overlap

The accepted **ALIFE 2026 LBA is non-archival**, according to the
[retained official CFP](../../venues/objects/alife-2026/references/alife-2026-cfp.md)
and [venue owner](../../venues/objects/alife-2026-lba.md): LBAs do not enter the
proceedings. The 2026 site could not be freshly retrieved in this pass, so this
fact is supported by the retained source, not a claimed successful fresh fetch.
The [GSO owner](../../venues/objects/gso-2026.md) records accepted abstract/talk
selection and no announced formal publication; absence of an announcement is not
a universal publisher exemption. The public essay is another disclosed antecedent.

Consequently, do **not** disqualify TMLR simply because this LBA was accepted.
TMLR explicitly permits overlap with prior work publicly declared non-archival.
Conversely, an accepted Blue Sky paper is archival. Reusing its text, figures or
results in a TMLR paper would conflict with TMLR's stated rule. Other journals'
extension policies must be checked against the eventual overlap; there is no
universal rule that “a sufficiently expanded paper is always acceptable.”

Before selecting an archival projection, compare the concrete central claim and
evidence with the existing
[institutional-action-licensing](../institutional-action-licensing/README.md)
and [AAMAS state-propagation](../substrate-coincidence/aamas-state-propagation/README.md)
configurations. Shared theoretical context need not mean identical contribution,
but relabeling the same evidence as “alignment” does not create a new result.
The present comparison does not revise those other configurations or their status.

## Readiness without forcing convergence

For a **research vision**, the current full history and literature contact can
already motivate a serious draft. It must acknowledge close predecessors,
distinguish hypotheses from observations, explain why existing frames do not yet
settle the selected question, and show what different answers would change.
Blue Sky publication still spends that particular vision as an archival contribution.

For a **mechanistic article**, the strongest existing evidence is the model-
conditional refusal/regeneration dissociation, fabricated authority controls, and
traceable commit-path escalation. The newer developmental probes broaden the
space but have small arms and prompt/access differences. Choose observations
appropriate to the question; retain timeouts and non-formation outcomes, document
founder provenance, and include beneficial coordination or successful revision as
real alternatives. The necessary replication is claim-dependent, not a universal
trial quota.

A useful long-form working title is *Organizational Inheritance and Transformation
in Persistent Agent Substrates*. A vision could ask *How Do Agent Organizations
Acquire and Revise Their Own Continuation Conditions?* These are framing options,
not renamed projects. The computational-neoplasm diagnosis remains one empirical
regime within the wider inquiry.

JASSS and Chaos remain secondary discovery leads: their official policy pages
returned empty/error content in this pass, so detailed current suitability and
submission advice are not asserted here. General-audience venues would require
stronger breadth and explanatory reach than the present exploratory record
establishes; prestige alone is not a useful reason to organize the inquiry around
them.
