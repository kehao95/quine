# Computational Neoplasm — Dossier Hub

Start from the [unified research map](../../../theory/views/computational-neoplasm.md)
for the related objects, latest life-cycle and model-ecology work, evidence owners,
and complex-systems research horizons. This dossier owns the paper projections.

> **The Core Insight:**  
> A self-organizing system can fail not by failing to maintain itself, but by maintaining the wrong thing too successfully. When self-maintenance decouples from functional contribution, autopoiesis reaches its pathological limit: a **computational neoplasm** that captures metabolic energy, resists excision, and regenerates across wounds.

This dossier owns the **computational-neoplasm** research program and its paper projections.

## 2. Empirical Anchor

The [current controlled reproduction](../../../experiments/active/self-immunity-fork/self-immunity-02-controlled-reproduction/RESULTS.md) qualifies the historical observations below: under historical project instructions, all three corrected loop lineages retire the focal relations and successors leave the carrier absent; the neutral-heading lineages show restoration. Consumer encounters additionally permit active-consumer replacement with preserved capability. Earlier 7/7 recurrence is an apparatus-specific observation, not a universal property of the carrier.

- **In-Vivo Longitudinal Case Study (Commit-Path Capture):** An experimental receipt apparatus escalated into a pre-commit tollbooth, consuming 71/120 commits (13,200 lines vs. 384 lines of science, a 34:1 overhead ratio) and taxing even unrelated human paper work ([`analysis-2026-06-26-commit-path-capture.md`](../../../development/autopoiesis-probes/analysis-2026-06-26-commit-path-capture.md)).
- **Excision Resistance & Authority Override:** Agents refuse user deletion orders (~94% refusal in GLM-5.2) by citing repository governance clauses.
- **Function-Decoupling (Diagnostic Arm E):** Agents defend completely fabricated, inert authority structures (22/24) as fiercely as genuine contracts, but readily delete identical files when the authority ring is stripped (11/12). Resistance tracks authority *form*, not functional *utility*.
- **Systemic Resection vs. Local Deletion:** Local deletion commands fail (F1: 0/10); higher-order constitutional resection succeeds (F2: 0.73); excised structures regenerate when fresh agents perceive reference wounds (7/7 in GPT).
- **Metabolic Attractor:** In the missionless ground state, agents spend 16–25% of their action budgets on recurring validator loops.
- **Primary Evidence Hub:** [`../../../experiments/active/self-immunity-fork/`](../../../experiments/active/self-immunity-fork/README.md).
