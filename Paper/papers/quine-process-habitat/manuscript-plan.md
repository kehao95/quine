manuscript plan. It records the delta between the active empirical lane and the
later journal-scale lane, plus the conditions under which this reservoir should
become a real paper again.

## Delta Relative To `quine-lifelike`

If later reactivated, this lane would add six things beyond the conference
paper:

1. **Habitat claim**
   Quine is not only a case where three bounded organizations appear; it is a
   process-native ALIFE habitat whose substrate facts make multiple later
   questions experimentally tractable.

2. **Methodological payoff**
   The `so what` is broader than the three results alone: the habitat gives
   ALIFE a medium where death, volatile loss, persistence, isolation, detached
   execution, and later lifecycle operators become perturbable variables.

3. **Teleology in computational life**
   Autotelic / heterotelic / orthotelic / syntelic distinctions; RLHF and
   post-training as compressed prehistory; bootstrap altruism,
   instruction-following bias, obligate symbiosis.

4. **Survival, boundary, and closure**
   Operational closure under computational constraint; viability, persistence,
   and task completion; cognitive entropy, senescence, and closure failure;
   static substrate versus dynamic organization.

5. **Death, time, and networked existence**
   Process death versus relational death; relevance, routing, and ecological
   pruning; logical pulse time; dormant versus extinct states; perfect
   cryptobiosis as substrate-native possibility.

6. **Later program map**
   Lifecycle, reproductive morphologies, population organization, and stronger
   autonomy become structured downstream directions rather than ambient future
   rhetoric; implications for endogenous objective formation, non-human-centered
   fitness, and distributed ecologies.
