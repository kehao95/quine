# Gap Survey Round 3 — Direction III (evidence-independent, premise-hunting)

Mode: **gap survey**, corrected protocol (no evidence front-loaded), same as
round 2. Direction III's thesis ("structure alone directs/sustains lifelike
agency in a frozen, missionless, reward-free, **selection-free** agent") came from
GS-5 (an evidence-anchored round). This round premise-hunts III's neighborhood
for the **reversible unstated premise** that gives III an inversion core of the
same grade as I and II, and to sharpen III's boundary against II (II = control
theory / *what kind of thing the effect is*; III = agency / *what the agent
becomes*).

Deliverable (identical to round 2): 3–6 findings ranked by importance × novelty.
Each = (1) the unstated premise as a sentence the field would endorse without
having written it; (2) evidence it's the operative assumption (quote/close-
paraphrase or honest "surveyor-identified pattern"); (3) THE INVERSION + why
important AND novel; (4) the CLASS of result needed to earn it (no assumed
apparatus). Flag unverified citations. Separate "field names this open" from
"field never thought to ask." Special ask: sharpen how III's inversion differs
from II's ("originates direction in a goal-less agent") — III should be about
*agency/becoming/life*, not *control*.
