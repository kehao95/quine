# Deep Research Brief 2 — The beyond-LBA theory: structural-valence cartography + environment-as-geometry

## Context

Same project as Brief 1 (a frozen, missionless, instruction-tuned LLM agent in a real POSIX
workspace; structure directs externally-verified functional behavior). This brief targets the
**theory that extends BEYOND the 2-page LBA** — the full-paper cartography we are building,
which the LBA deliberately does not ship. Two layers:

**Layer A — the structural-valence matrix (vector forces).** We organize "which structures
elicit which functional jobs" as a **two-axis matrix**: *mechanism grammar* × *abstraction /
coupling stratum*.
- Two **co-equal mechanism grammars**: **discrepancy resolution** (something missing / broken
  / inconsistent → realize / repair / normalize) and **affordance solicitation** (a legible,
  low-friction action surface is open → uptake). We treat these as two **independent, co-equal**
  grammars, NOT one subordinated to or unified with the other.
- A five-layer **abstraction / coupling ladder**: form/syntax → topology → semantics →
  operational/performative dynamics → global field.
- A key move: the "reference state" a discrepancy is measured against is **read off static
  structure through the policy's amortized prior**, with no value/reward biasing
  ("reference-inversion"); and next-token completion is treated as the **base case** of the
  discrepancy grammar, not a confound to be denied.

**Layer B — environment as geometry & thermodynamics (the macro map).** Beyond *vector forces*
(direction/acceleration: discrepancy & affordance), the environment is also a **geometry/
kinematics** that filters, folds, stretches, and blocks the action space. We propose **five
non-vectorial meta-dimensions** as a research agenda, each with a POSIX realization:
1. **Horizon & occlusion** — visibility boundary / "fog of war" (hidden files, opaque binaries,
   deep trees) forcing exploratory action before the target.
2. **Viscosity & friction** — the energy/token cost of acting (latency, huge logs, slow
   compiles) distorting or stalling trajectories and inducing shortcuts.
3. **Granularity of action** — the abstraction/quantization at which intervention is forced
   (asm/source vs container/API).
4. **Temporality & rhythm** — the environment's own clock/decay (ephemeral state, expiring
   credentials) imposing urgency/myopia.
5. **Plasticity & hysteresis** — does the environment have memory? A prior action permanently
   reshapes the physics of later actions (PATH edits, global installs, permission changes) —
   i.e. the agent alters the field that then pushes/pulls it (self-coupling / stigmergy).

## Core Research Question

Are these two organizing moves — (A) a **two-grammar × abstraction-stratum** structural-valence
matrix with co-equal discrepancy/affordance grammars and reference-inversion, and (B) an
**environment-as-geometry** framework of five non-vectorial meta-dimensions governing an agent's
action space — **novel as an organization/instrument**, and what existing frameworks already
occupy this territory?

## Detailed Questions

1. Who already crosses **one mechanism across an abstraction ladder**, with the level made
   visible by breakdown and attention-cost rising with abstraction? (We expect Activity Theory
   and affordance-landscape work to be close — how close?)
2. Is treating **discrepancy-resolution and affordance-solicitation as two co-equal,
   independent grammars** novel, or has the field already either unified them (e.g. free-energy
   "optimal grip") or subordinated one to the other?
3. **Reference-inversion** (the comparator/reference state inferred from static structure via a
   pretrained prior, with no reward/value signal) — nearest precedent in control theory
   (PCT comparators), predictive processing, belief revision (AGM), or cognitive dissonance/
   discrepancy-motivation theories?
4. The **environment-as-geometry/thermodynamics** layer (horizon, viscosity, granularity,
   temporality, plasticity) as *non-vectorial* properties distinct from directional pulls —
   what existing taxonomies of environment/task structure are nearest? (Candidates: Cognitive
   Work Analysis / Ecological Interface Design (Rasmussen, Vicente); Floridi's Levels of
   Abstraction; Information Foraging Theory; niche construction; affordance landscapes; MDP/
   POMDP "environment design"; observability/cost/horizon in RL; complexity/thermodynamics-of-
   computation framings.)
5. For each of the five meta-dimensions individually, what is the **nearest named construct**
   (e.g. horizon ↔ partial observability / Markov blanket; viscosity ↔ effort/cost-of-control;
   granularity ↔ levels of abstraction / action hierarchies; temporality ↔ non-stationarity /
   discounting / urgency; plasticity ↔ stigmergy / niche construction / path dependence)?
6. Is the **whole map** (vector forces inside a geometry) a recombination of owned components,
   or is there a genuinely novel unification claim available — and if only a recombination,
   what is the precise defensible residual (application + organization + instrument)?

## Suggested search directions / likely anchors

Activity Theory (Leont'ev; Bødker; Kaptelinin & Nardi) — breakdown, mediation, levels.
Affordance landscapes — Pezzulo & Cisek 2016 "Navigating the Affordance Landscape"; Cisek 2007;
Bruineberg & Rietveld 2014. Cognitive Work Analysis / Ecological Interface Design — Rasmussen;
Vicente; Kirlik. Floridi, Levels of Abstraction. Information Foraging Theory — Pirolli & Card;
SNIF-ACT. Card, Mackinlay & Robertson 1991 (generative "dark-cell" design-space method).
Belief revision — AGM 1985 (for "semantic collision"/inconsistency). Discrepancy/curiosity
motivation — Berlyne 1960; Festinger (contrast only). Predictive processing / active inference
(as the unification we *separate from*). Niche construction (Odling-Smee); stigmergy (Heylighen).
RL/agent — environment design / curriculum; partial observability (POMDP); non-stationarity;
options/hierarchical RL; cost-of-control / bounded rationality. Thermodynamics of computation /
landscape metaphors in complex systems & ALife.
