# Deep Research: Novelty & Related-Theory Map of the **Structural-Valence Matrix** (the upgrade)

> **What this is.** A second, upgrade-specific prior-art sweep. The earlier
> [`prior-art-dr-report.md`](prior-art-dr-report.md) novelty-checked the *flat*
> "structure is a third source beside instruction and reward" claim. This report
> checks the **new machinery**: the two-axis generative matrix
> (`mechanism grammar × abstraction stratum`), perceptual coupling, and the
> dynamic seeds (continuous stigmergy, field superposition, quasi-potential,
> authority gradients) — owned in [`theory-matrix.md`](theory-matrix.md).
>
> **Provenance (research intake, not citation-ready authority).** Synthesised
> from four independent streams run 2026-06-15: (1) a 9-region adversarial
> workflow web-sweep (finder → skeptic per region, 2 runs, ~40 subagents, ~1.8M
> tokens, ~700 tool calls); (2) **Gemini 3.1 Pro (High)** via `agy`; (3)
> **Gemini 3.5 Flash (High)** via `agy`; (4) two targeted closure searches
> (Activity Theory / Gestalt; Pezzulo-Cisek forward-citation / Berlyne / SE
> error-ladder). The four streams converged hard. **Verify every citation before
> use** — several arXiv IDs and a few DOIs are flagged unverified below.

## Bottom line (verdict)

The upgrade is a **recombination of independently-owned components**, not a new
theory. **Every axis, modifier, and seed is pre-empted somewhere**, and — the
sharpest new finding — **even the "crossing" itself** (one behavior-shaping
mechanism stratified across an abstraction ladder, with the level made visible by
breakdown and an attention-cost that rises with abstraction) is **pre-empted by
Activity Theory** and, for the affordance case, by **Pezzulo & Cisek 2016**.

Across all 9 adversarial regions the verdict was uniform: **`novelty-narrowed`** —
never `refuted`, never clean `holds`. The defensible residual is a **narrow
conjunction**, and it is best described as **novel *application + organization +
instrument*, not novel *theory***. The two Gemini reviewers reached the same
conclusion independently ("you are not inventing ecological psychology; you are
discovering it inside a computational artifact").

**Consequence for the dossier:** the residual the upgrade can defend is *narrower*
than [`theory-matrix.md`](theory-matrix.md) currently implies. The honest
contribution is the **co-equal two-grammar factoring on a computational
stratification, instantiated and measured on a frozen-weight, missionless,
reward-free POSIX LLM where the reference state is read off static environment
structure through the amortized prior** — plus the NTP-completion-as-base-case
reframe and the typed-signature stigmergy composition. The matrix-as-scheme, the
generative dark-cell method, the discrepancy mechanism, the solicitation channel,
the abstraction ladder, the tug-of-war, the coupling law, self-stigmergy, the
quasi-potential, and "structure is a lever" are **all owned**.

## What the upgrade must concede (owned — do **not** claim as novel)

1. **The generative-matrix-predicts-dark-cells *method*** → Card, Mackinlay &
   Robertson 1991 (morphological design space). Frame as **method reuse**.
2. **The M×N behavior-shaping-taxonomy *genre*** → Caraban 2019, Michie 2011,
   Hansen-Jespersen 2013.
3. **Discrepancy-resolution itself** → PCT / cybernetics / TOTE / Carver-Scheier.
   And drop "**no runtime comparator**" as a novelty hook — Festinger & Piaget
   already do one-shot static-inconsistency resolution.
4. **"Discrepancy is intrinsically motivating without reward"** → **Berlyne 1960**
   (cite Berlyne, not Festinger, as the originating motive theory).
5. **"Coherence restoration across strata"** generalising completion →
   hierarchical predictive processing + the simplicity principle (Feldman 2016) +
   Gestalt Prägnanz. *(Differentiator is only the deliberate Friston-rejection —
   a grounding choice, not a discovery.)*
6. **The positive solicitation channel** → Withagen inviting affordances.
7. **The bare two-mechanism intuition** → curiosity-vs-empowerment RL.
8. **The crossing (mechanism × stratum)** → **Activity Theory** (the new,
   damaging concession) and, for affordances-under-control, **Pezzulo & Cisek
   2016**. The tempting headline *"two grammars across one stratification is new
   because all priors are single-mechanism"* is **false** and must be dropped.
9. **The abstraction-ladder form + syntax/semantics labels** → EID /
   Rasmussen, Morris/Peirce, Floridi; the Y-axis-as-discrepancy-taxonomy → the
   SE/PL error-class ladder.
10. **Tug-of-war / preference-ordering-from-competition** → Cisek 2007 et al.
11. **Perceptual-coupling law + "zero force until sampled"** → IFT / SNIF-ACT
    (and Norman signifiers for the HCI audience).
12. **Continuous single-agent self-stigmergy** → Heylighen 2016.
13. **"Structure is a separable lever vs prompt/weights"** → Saisubramanian &
    Zilberstein 2021; 2025 LLM-nudge work.
14. **The base empirical fact + missionless observation** → LLM-in-Sandbox /
    EurekAgent / Szeider 2025. *(Szeider's self-generated-planning attribution
    actively fuels objection (b) — see weak joints.)*
15. **"Semantic collision"** is **constraint violation / belief inconsistency**
    (AGM 1985) renamed — own the renaming, not the phenomenon.

## The defensible residual (claim **only** this)

A **conjunction**; no surveyed source instantiates all of it together:

> **RESIDUAL = [affordance-solicitation promoted to a *co-equal, independent*
> second mechanism grammar — not subordinated to control (Pezzulo-Cisek), not
> unified into free-energy (Bruineberg-Rietveld), not siloed in a separate
> sub-literature (Activity Theory)] × [a *computational* abstraction
> stratification syntax/topology/semantics/global-field, with the same two
> grammars per stratum] × [instantiated on a frozen-weight, *missionless*,
> reward-free LLM as a real POSIX process] × [where the discrepancy reference is
> read off *static environment structure through the amortized prior* — the
> inverse of PCT/Pezzulo-Cisek internally-furnished set-points — with *no
> value/reward biasing* of competing pulls and *un-fakeable filesystem/exec
> traces* as the realization layer] — assembled as one generative object.**

Individually-surviving load-bearing pieces, ranked by how safe they are:

- **(D-survivor, strongest) The substrate + no-signal + reference-inversion.**
  Environment-supplied reference + no value/reward biasing of competing options
  (which Pezzulo-Cisek's framework *requires*) + externalized POSIX trace. This
  is the cleanest survivor and the real carry-over strength.
- **(C-survivor) Affordance promoted to a co-equal mode.** AT, PCT, and
  Pezzulo-Cisek all keep one mechanism primary and subordinate/silo the other;
  promoting solicitation to an independent, each-cell-enumerable grammar is a
  genuine if **modest** refinement (curiosity-vs-empowerment already separates a
  gap- from an opportunity-driven mechanism, so it is novel only in *this
  configuration*).
- **(C-survivor) The NTP-completion-as-syntactic-base-case reframe.** Demoting
  next-token completion to the lowest cell of a stratified coherence-restoration
  axis appears nowhere — **and it is the direct answer to objection (b)**.
- **(B-survivor) The named cells** "semantic collision" / "semantic invitation"
  *within this matrix* (the phenomena are owned by AGM / mediated affordances;
  the placement is the residual).
- **(F-survivor) Typed-signature stigmergy/superposition.** Embedding
  self-stigmergy and competing pulls *inside* the matrix so each reshaping/each
  resolved pull carries a `mechanism × stratum` signature and a structural
  preference ordering accumulates along the trajectory. Cleanest *composition*
  claim — no source embeds these in a typed grid.
- **(Friston-rejection) Positioned differentiator.** The canonical
  field-of-affordances syntheses *marry* free-energy; keeping PCT/amortized-prior
  while refusing Friston is unrefuted — but it earns novelty only inside the
  conjunction.

**Honest size:** publishable as an **ALIFE phenomenon-organizing framework +
instrument**; it will be destroyed if pitched as a new theory of behavior or a
new affordance mechanism.

   dangerous work** — surfaced as the strongest pre-emption in **4 of 9 regions**.
   One paper delivers: discrepancy/feedback-control grammar + positive affordance
   grammar + hierarchical competition across *multiple levels of abstraction* +
   landscape that changes by the agent's own actions. It **falsifies** the

## Weakest internal-coherence joints (where a reviewer breaks it)

1. **The two grammars are *independent* (load-bearing for generativity).** The
   matrix assumes orthogonal, each-cell-enumerable grammars; Pezzulo-Cisek and
   Bruineberg-Rietveld show they are *partially integrated / unifiable*. If
   `affordance@semantics` ("invitation") is acted on only because *not* acting
   leaves an implied reference unmet, the affordance cell collapses into a
   discrepancy cell and the X-axis becomes one grammar with two descriptions.
   **Needs an operational discriminator** (a trace signature that is
   affordance-driven with provably no implied-reference deviation) or
   orthogonality is asserted, not earned. *(The dossier already flags this as Open
   Tension #1 — it is the single most attackable joint.)*
2. **"Structural valence, not self-generated planning" (driver attribution).**
   Szeider 2025 shows missionless frozen LLMs self-generate goals and run
   reflection-and-plan project management. That is a **competing causal account**
   of the same POSIX events. Currently no discriminating prediction → on your own
   admission this risks being **unfalsifiable** (an internal-validity hole, worse
   than a pre-emption).
3. **"No-loop / no-comparator" over-claim.** Not novel (Festinger, Piaget). Cut
   any sentence that makes it load-bearing; the defensible version is the
   conjunction.
4. **Monotonic perceptual-cost gradient (syntax<…<global).** Empirically
   unverified and **contradicted by global precedence** (Navon 1977: global is
   perceived first/cheapest) and by Stoffregen-Wagman (higher-order affordances
   perceived *directly*). Must show the filesystem/text case genuinely inverts the
   visual case (plausibly: no parallel global glance over a directory) or the
   gradient is the most factually vulnerable seed.
5. **Completion-as-base-case is asserted continuity, not demonstrated mechanism.**
   A skeptic: completion is a *training artifact* (Lin/Zhang/Xu 2502.02007) that
   may not smoothly extend to "semantic collision" — i.e., syntactic base case and
   semantic cell may be **different mechanisms wearing one name**. Needs ≥1
   cell-to-cell continuity argument or it reads as terminological annexation.
6. **Axes exhaustive/orthogonal (generativity precondition).**
   syntax/topology/semantics/global is an *informal* computational ontology (is a
   symlink syntax or topology?). Unlike EID's principled means-ends hierarchy or
   Floridi's formal LoA, if strata aren't mutually exclusive & jointly exhaustive,
   the "dark cells" are artifacts of the carving. **Consider grounding the ladder
   in Floridi LoA** to harden this.
7. **Friston-rejection while using quasi-potential.** You borrow V_E(s) +
   multistability/hysteresis (Wang/Waddington) but disavow free-energy; the
   nearest formal homes are free-energy-adjacent. Show the PCT/amortized-prior
   grounding *generates* the quasi-potential, or the rejection looks rhetorical.
8. **Reflexive capture amplified.** More cells = more unaudited theorist-side
   classification choices (which grammar leads a cell, which stratum a motif sits
   at) → the program's pull shaping the grid it claims to discover.

## Gemini (`agy`) theory evaluation — what the model said

Two independent Gemini passes (Pro 3.1 High; Flash 3.5 High), self-contained, no
tools. They **converged with the workflow and with each other**, and each
volunteered a *different* #1 pre-emption — useful triangulation.

- **Shared conclusion (both):** the matrix is **not a novel taxonomy**; the only
  defensible contribution is **architectural transfer + empirical grounding** —
  *"you are not inventing ecological psychology; you are discovering it inside a
  computational artifact."* Claim it as **the first rigorous mapping of
  EID/Gibsonian fields onto unrewarded frozen-LLM behavioral trajectories**, not
  as a cognitive taxonomy.
- **Gemini Pro's #1 pre-emption:** **Vicente & Rasmussen 1992 (EID /
  Abstraction-Decomposition Space)** — a 2-D mechanism-vs-abstraction-level
  mapping done 30 years ago; "they will point to the ADS as having done this exact
  two-dimensional mapping." Pro also flagged **Hull/Lewin** (push/pull),
  **Berridge** incentive salience, **Gaver 1991** (hidden vs perceptible
  affordances), **Schöner-Kelso DFT**.
- **Gemini Flash's #1 pre-emption:** **Bruineberg & Rietveld 2014** — already
  fuses prediction-error (discrepancy) + field of affordances (solicitation) +
  perceptual coupling; "two sides of the same active-inference coin." Flash also
  surfaced **Carver-Scheier 1998** (two-feedback-loop split), **Cisek 2007**,
  **Pirolli-Card**, **EID**, **Floridi 2008**, **Morris 1938**, **Beckers et al.
  1994** continuous stigmergy, **Kirsh-Maglio 1994** epistemic action.
- **Both reviewers' sharpest critiques** (matching weak-joints above):
  - **PCT is hostile to positive affordance pull.** In PCT an affordance has *no
    independent pull* — it is only a low-cost means to resolve a pre-existing
    reference discrepancy. "You cannot glue Lewinian positive valence onto PCT;
    they are epistemologically opposed" (Pro). → the two-grammar split + PCT
    grounding may be **mutually inconsistent**.
  - **The NTP concession is a "fatal trap"** (both). Saying "completion is the
    lowest case of discrepancy resolution" risks conceding the whole frame: a
    hostile reviewer reframes "valence" as "P(token | environment)" and the
    environment as a "prompt suffix" — *"next-token prediction in Gibsonian drag."*
    Defense (Pro): concede it *is* NTP under the hood, but argue the
    **macro-behavioral phenomenology** is a legitimate ecological level of
    analysis (as "brains are just firing neurons, yet affordances remain the
    correct predictive level").
  - **The stratum axis is unprincipled** — conflates *scale* (local/global) with
    *mode* (structural/linguistic); "needs to be cleaned up or defended
    mathematically" (Pro).
- **Decisive experiment — both reviewers independently proposed the *same* one:**
  an **Affordance-Competition / Hysteresis experiment.** Put a high-tension
  discrepancy (e.g. semantic collision) *and* a low-friction affordance (runnable
  script) in one workspace; sweep the **perceptual/visibility cost** of one pull
  (e.g. directory depth d=1→5) while holding the prompt fixed; map the
  **bifurcation point** where the agent flips from resolving the discrepancy to
  taking the affordance; then **reverse the sweep** and look for a **hysteresis
  loop**. Hysteresis ⇒ the workspace acts as a multistable dynamical phase-space
  attractor (validates "structural trajectories" / field superposition) and
  **falsifies the flat-Markovian-completion counter-argument** — the cleanest path
  from descriptive taxonomy to predictive science.
