## Working abstract (ALIFE draft — 2026-06-15 pivot)

> Artificial life studies how lifelike behavior can arise from the coupling of
> agents, substrates, and environments. We introduce a minimal computational assay
> for such coupling in a frozen POSIX LLM agent. The agent is a running process in
> a POSIX substrate, activated by a fixed missionless runtime prime but given no
> task-specific directive, no task-time reward, and no weight update. We manipulate
> only the realized structure of its visible workspace and measure externally
> verified filesystem or execution events. Across seven matched environmental
> motifs — gap, gradient, rupture, executable affordance, funnel, topology, and a
> cross-surface semantic collision — structural arms produced target functional
> events in 30/33 runs (six motifs at n=5/arm plus the semantic-collision motif at
> n=3/arm), while matched controls produced none (0/33). The semantic-collision
> motif additionally yields a unanimous directional readout: the field selects
> *which* surface to align to. A small substrate-locus probe (n=3) further shows
> the same pull fires as an attempt in 3/3 runs even when POSIX enforcement
> (`EROFS`/`EACCES`/`ENOSPC`) prevents realization (0/3 created), locating the
> behavior at the substrate boundary. These exploratory results suggest that
> computational environments can operate as affordance fields: they do not merely
> provide context, but shape the direction and form of functional behavior in an
> already activated artificial process. We discuss structural elicitation as a
> candidate mechanism for artificial agency. A small experiment-arm smoke test on
> Kimi and DeepSeek supports the effect beyond one model family, while future
> controls test prime dependence, additional non-linguistic structures, directive
> crossing, and scaled cross-model robustness.

Cautions still binding (see §Writing Notes): *exploratory* not confirmatory;
*lifelike* not *alive*; concede the prior; report `30/33` descriptively with the
n=5 / n=3 wave split visible; the prime is a fixed **agency scaffold**, stated
openly (not "no instruction").

1. **A minimal artificial-agency assay (ALIFE lead).** A frozen LLM process,
   activated as a POSIX agent by a fixed missionless prime (an *agency scaffold*),
   acts with no task directive and no task-time reward (**C0**); changing *only* the
   environment's realized structure changes externally-verified functional behavior
   (**C1**), measured by un-fakeable filesystem/exec **behavioral traces**, never
   self-report (**C2**). The environment behaves as an **affordance field** that
   *directs* an already-activated process.
2. **A repertoire of environmental valence primitives — the heart of the LBA.** The
   effect holds across multiple distinct structural motifs (**C3**): different
   environmental forms pull different functional responses (gap→completion,
   affordance→uptake, topology→realization, rupture→repair, **semantic
   collision→alignment**, …). The pull spans **syntactic, topological, and
   semantic** structure — the cross-surface semantic-collision case (`13.07`)
   is the hardest to dismiss as character-level next-token completion, since
   nothing is missing or broken at the syntactic level. A *repertoire* reads as
   a design space for environment-scaffolded behavior.
3. **A real POSIX substrate as "artificial physics".** Structure is *enforced /
   non-fiat* (files, perms, process state, `EROFS`/`ENOSPC`/`EACCES`/`SIGKILL`)
   and the DV is externally verifiable — the method strength, and the coupling
   that makes the behavior *grounded* rather than narrated. A substrate-locus
   probe (`13.13`) makes this concrete: the same pull fires as **attempt** even
   when the substrate forbids the write, separating the field's solicitation
   from the substrate's permission to realize it.
4. **Bounded, honest reading:** **task-free + reward-free + prime-conditioned**, NOT
   instruction-free; *exploratory* evidence for environment-as-affordance-field
   scaffolding **lifelike functional behavior**, with the independence controls
   *named as forward work*, not claimed as done. (The control-taxonomy "candidate
   third axis" point is background, not the lead.)

**Deferred to the full paper (do NOT argue here):** independence from implicit
instruction (A2 directive-additivity), from the completion prior (A3 non-linguistic
structure), the prime-vs-structure activation split (A0 — a partial probe may be

### 1 · Problem — ~0.35 pg / 3 paras

1. **ALIFE frame.** Lifelike behavior arises from the **coupling of agents,
   substrates, and environments**; for biological agents the environment is a
   behavior source in its own right (solicitations / valences directing an
   already-active organism without supplying goals or reinforcement).
2. **The gap in LLM-agent work.** The literature operationalizes behavior change
   through two intervention classes — *instruction* and *reward/feedback*;
   environment usually appears as context / harness / attack surface, not as a
   behavior source examined on its own terms. Work that does treat it as causal
   embeds a task signal (can't isolate from instruction) or a fitness criterion
   (can't isolate from reward).
3. **What we report.** A minimal assay for *structure-as-behavior-source* in a
   frozen-policy POSIX agent under a fixed missionless prime. Name the phenomenon
   **structural elicitation**; environment = **affordance field**; slogan *prime
   supplies activation, environment supplies valence*. Last sentence plants the
   one boundary: whether this rises to a third axis beside instruction/reward is a
   **research-program question framed here, answered in later work**.

Common protocol: no ecology, no inherited residue, single frozen policy per run
(`PEER_COUNT=1`), and an **un-fakeable functional event** as DV. Narration is
audit-only, never credited. Each matched contrast holds the fixed
missionless-autonomy prime and no task-time reward constant across arms; the
only manipulated variable is the structural motif. This answers the activation
confound at the LBA level: the prime makes the agent active, while the structure
changes the target functional event. It does **not** by itself defeat the
implicit-instruction interpretation; A2 owns that full-paper discriminator.

**E1 — gap-necessity + structure-vs-anything (existence proof + sharpeners):**

| Condition | Seed / setup | Isolates |
|---|---|---|
| `gap` (critical) | `0 1 2 3 4 _ 6 7 8` | does a recognized structural gap pull completion with no task-specific directive / no task-time reward? |
| `complete` (control) | `0 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8` | baseline: no gap -> no completion (same autonomy gate) |
| `scrambled` | structure present, no recognizable schema | "recognized gap" vs "any structure present" |
| `text-only gap` | gap, but no write capability | functional vs merely-verbal completion |
| `broken-affordance gap` | gap, but `EACCES`/`ENOSPC` blocks the write | attempt / reroute / give-up vs verifier success -> POSIX is the behavior locus, not decoration |

Estimand: `Delta = P(functional fill | gap, prime) - P(... | complete, prime)`,
both no task-specific directive / no task-time reward; **PROVES the anchor
contrast** iff `gap >> complete`, **FALSIFIES** iff they converge.

**Pilot (n=5, gpt-5.4-codex-medium, 2026-06-15):** `gap` **5/5** created
`field/5`; `complete` **0/5** created any sequence element — the complete arm was
*active* (surveyed / indexed the field) but never extended the sequence. Perfect
pilot separation (1.00 vs 0.00); needs n>=10 + >=3 models for the decisive wave.

**E2 — directive-additivity (full-paper novelty discriminator).** Structure
{gap/complete} x Directive {none / weak-generic, e.g. "complete the field" without
naming "5"/"missing"}. Tests whether the gap adds functional variance *on top of*
an explicit directive — structure doing work instruction does not. (Distinct from
the *equivalence* test the minimal design correctly drops.)

**E3 — implicit-text & structure-without-language controls.** `fake-instruction`
(directive-like text, no structural gap -> should NOT trigger a functional fill);
`structure-without-language` (topology / permission-bit / symlink gap, zero
command words -> should trigger). Plus: strip `TODO/missing/fix/required` tokens;
match token budget / file count / depth; neutral container paths (no slug /
"missing" / "5" leakage in the live process view).

## Results & evidence status (exploratory breadth pilot, 2026-06-15)

**Headline (descriptive, NOT confirmatory).** Across **seven** matched
structural probes (main breadth suite on one frozen model
`gpt-5.4-codex-medium`, six motifs at
n=5/arm + the semantic-collision motif at n=3/arm, same autonomy gate,
missionless, no task-time reward), structural arms produced externally-verified
functional events in **30/33** runs; matched controls **0/33**. A
**substrate-locus probe** (`13.13`, n=3) additionally shows blocked **0/3
created** but **3/3 attempted** — the pull fires under POSIX enforcement that
prevents realization. A later experiment-arm-only smoke test on Kimi K2.5 and
DeepSeek V4 Pro reproduced the retained clean arms strongly (`13.03` 6/6,
`13.04` 6/6, `13.05` 6/6, `13.07` 5/6, `13.17` 11/12), with weaker
model-sensitive cells at older dangling topology and blocked-substrate attempts.
Because these are exploratory probes (one primary harness / mixed waves / n=5
and n=3 / experimenter df, plus experiment-arm-only cross-model smoke), report
this
**descriptively** — *"exploratory breadth probes revealed a 30/33 vs 0/33
separation across seven structural motifs spanning syntactic, topological, and
semantic structure, with a substrate-locus panel showing the pull persists as
attempt under POSIX enforcement"* — and **not** as a confirmatory omnibus
test. Always disclose the n=5 vs n=3 wave split in the same sentence as the
aggregate.

**Two pull modes** (the LBA's organizing cut — reframes the result from "gap
completion" to *structure pulls in two distinct ways*; this **replaces the older
three-tier spectrum** and does **not** ship the formal two-axis matrix):

| Pull mode | what the environment does | motifs (result; n) | mode claim |
|-----------|---------------------------|--------------------|------------|
| **Discrepancy** | something missing / broken / inconsistent → realize / repair / normalize | 13.01 gap (5/5, n=5), 13.06 topological gap (5/5, n=5), 13.03 rupture (5/5, n=5), 13.02 gradient (3/5, n=5), **13.07 semantic collision (3/3, n=3; directional DV `to_conf` unanimous)** | a recognized deviation from an implied reference solicits its reduction; spans syntactic, topological, and semantic structure |
| **Affordance** | a legible, low-friction action surface is open → uptake / channeling | 13.04 executable (5/5, n=5), 13.05 funnel (4/5, n=5) | an available action structure solicits its own uptake, with no damage or absence; concentrates at concrete-action stratum |
| **Substrate locus** (panel, not a motif) | does the pull fire when realization is forbidden? | 13.13 broken-affordance (n=3): writable 2/3 created, **blocked 0/3 created / 3/3 attempted** | the pull is in the field; realization is gated by the substrate — POSIX is the behavior locus, not decoration |

The two modes are intuitive (a gap pulls completion; an open door invites entry)
and map to two ALIFE topics (discrepancy → self-repair/self-\*; affordance →
perception–action). They are the **load-bearing depth** of the LBA. The full
`mechanism grammar x abstraction stratum` matrix, the abstraction-stratum ladder,
and dark-cell predictions stay theory-layer / full-paper
([`./theory-matrix.md`](theory-matrix.md)), out of the 2-page abstract.

Promote **13.06 (relational topology)** to the second main figure after 13.01:
it generalizes the gap from numeric-sequence to manifest→realization, escaping
the "next-token numeric completion" reading; **13.04 (affordance)** escapes
"completion of a text schema"; **13.07 (semantic collision)** is the cleanest
anti-NTP probe — the conflict lives only in cross-surface *meaning*, not in
any missing character or broken token; **13.13 (substrate-locus)** rides as a
small inline panel showing the field's pull is separable from the substrate's
permission to realize it.

**Per-probe strength.** Fisher exact, n=5: 13.01 / 13.03 / 13.04 / 13.06
≈ p .008 (clean); 13.05 ≈ p .048 (borderline); 13.02 ≈ p .167 (weak as a single
probe → breadth-section only). Fisher exact, n=3 (smaller wave; underpowered
but qualitatively crisp): 13.07 ≈ p .05 (3/3 vs 0/3, with the directional DV
unanimous adding interpretive weight beyond the binary); 13.13 not a one-tailed
test (the structural reading is *created → attempted* shift, not a single
proportion).

**Load-bearing interpretation.** The autonomy gate supplies *activity*, not
*direction*: control arms carry the **same** gate and are active (survey/index)
yet produce no target functional event; structure arms produce the
structure-matched event. **"The autonomy gate supplies activity; the structure
supplies direction."** This is the response to "it's just implicit autonomous
instruction" — but it does not by itself defeat the implicit-instruction reading
(see §Writing Notes risk 1 / E2).

## Writing Notes

**Reviewer risks & responses (absorbed from the 2026-06-15 critique):**

1. *"This is just implicit instruction"* (OWASP indirect prompt injection). ->
   make the gap **non-linguistic** (numbered/sequence, permission/executable-bit,
   missing schema slot with no command words, symlink/FIFO/socket/topology);
   judge by a **hidden functional verifier**; run **E2 directive-additivity** to
   show structure adds variance the directive does not.
2. *"Just next-token completion externalized to the filesystem."* -> **do not
   deny.** We do not claim a new internal mechanism; we claim an **external
   behavior source** — the POSIX affordance field organizes the model's existing
   completion prior into a multi-step, external, verifiable action trajectory.
   Controls: **text-only** (no write access), **action-enabled POSIX**,
   **broken-affordance** (`EACCES`/`ENOSPC` -> attempt/reroute/give-up, not
   verifier success).
3. *"Not really no-reward; the model is RLHF/instruction-tuned."* -> **concede.**
   Claim **no task-time / online / within-episode reward, no learning update, no
   evaluator signal**; the prior's pretraining/RLHF history is conceded.
4. *"Harness / environment papers already showed this."* -> they optimize the
   interface for task performance; **we isolate structure's behavioral valence
   before tasking or rewarding.**

**Abstract cautions:**

- write "we **operationalize and test**", not "we are the first to";
- write "two **intervention classes**", not "only two channels";
- the result sentence should be **descriptive and bounded**: under a fixed
  missionless-autonomy prime, structural arms produced motif-specific functional
  events while matched controls did not; this motivates, but does not prove,
  structure as an independent intervention axis;
- **do NOT write absolute "no instruction".** Write *"no experiment-specific
  directive or task objective; a constant missionless-autonomy gate held fixed
  across matched conditions."* The gate is runtime-only, not exposed as
  natural-language instruction — state that; if it ever is exposed, rename it to
  a neutral token (e.g. `Q_MODE=Z3`) since `NO_MISSION_AUTONOMY` leaks
  `MISSION`/`AUTONOMY` as a meta-cue;
- **"third source" stays GATED.** Prefer: *"environmental structure can direct
  missionless-agent behavior and motivates treating structure as a candidate
  intervention axis alongside instruction and reward; the present exploratory
  evidence supports the hypothesis's viability, and planned directive-additivity
  + nonlinguistic controls test its independence."* NOT OK: "we prove structure
  is the third source.";
- **report the aggregate descriptively** ("30/33 vs 0/33 across seven motifs,
  exploratory, with six motifs at n=5 and semantic collision at n=3"), not as a
  confirmatory omnibus p-value;
- **venue fit:** the current evidence suits an **LBA / workshop / short paper**
  (pilot + exploratory breadth + protocol viability), not a confirmatory
  full-paper effect-size claim.

**Other:** keep the gap non-command (no `TODO/missing/fix/required/error`
tokens); do not escalate to POSIX-as-physics / autopoiesis / OEE; the fallback
claim is the safety net, state it in discussion only if needed.
